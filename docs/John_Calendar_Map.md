# John — Calendar Map (the authoritative sequence)

The 150 content lessons pinned to real dates and day-types, with the 2 pre-finals review days and 2 finals windows. This supersedes the draft Scope & Sequence for sequencing, and is the backbone the lesson generator walks (Approach B). Machine-readable copy: `john-calendar-map.json`.

**Container:** 150 firm content lessons (Semester 1 = 71, John 1-12; Semester 2 = 79, John 13-21), plus a tentative 151st flex day on 2027-05-21 (graduation) if sophomores meet. Day-types: 50-min regular, 36-min Thursday, 26-min half-day.

## Resolutions baked in

- **Half-days (26 min) never carry a dense exposition.** Each of the 4 half-days is the containing unit's memory-quiz-and-recap beat; the unit teaches its passage in its full-length beats.
- **Unit 1 reshaped** so the deity passage (1:1-5) gets a full period: the whole-Prologue read-aloud orientation lands on the Sept 10 Thursday, and 1:1-5 moves to Friday Sept 11.
- **Thursdays (36 min)** carry a single-focus version of their passage. The dense spring discourse makes some unavoidable; reshape a unit's internal order at build time to spare its densest beat.
- **4 checkpoints** (2 per semester) and **2 pre-finals review days** placed; **finals** Jan 11-14 and May 25-28.
