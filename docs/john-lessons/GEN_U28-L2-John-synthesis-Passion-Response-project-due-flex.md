**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will recall the seven predicated "I am" sayings and what each offers.
2. Students will explain the absolute "I am" (8:58) as the divine name.
3. Students will explain 14:6 as the only way to the Father, held warmly.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus told people who he is with "I am" statements.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the "I am" sayings show what Christ is to us and who he is in himself.
- **Stretch** (disciple): "before Abraham was, I am" (8:58) as the name of Exodus 3:14.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. Synthesis: gather from the room, sort, land. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "I am the way, and the truth, and the life" (John 14:6).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Which of Jesus' 'I am' sayings means the most to me right now, and why?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Read the "I am" sayings back to back so the room hears them as one voice. Assign readers for each:
  6:35 (bread), 8:12 (light), 10:11 (good shepherd), 11:25 (resurrection and life), 14:6 (way, truth, life), 15:5 (vine). Then 8:58 (the absolute "I am").
- Navigation help: these are scattered across John 6 through 15, plus 8:58.
- One-sentence setup: over and over, Jesus told people who he is, starting with two words: "I am."

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, gather and sort)

Pull it from the room. Two moves: what Christ is to us, and who he is in himself.

### The seven predicated "I am" sayings (about 12 min)

1. **Gather (John groups).** List the "I am the..." sayings, and for each, what does Jesus offer, and what human need does it meet?
   - Fill the board:
     - Bread of life (6:35): he feeds the hungry soul.
     - Light of the world (8:12): he gives sight and drives out darkness.
     - The door / the good shepherd (10:7, 10:11): he protects and lays down his life for the sheep.
     - The resurrection and the life (11:25): he conquers death.
     - The way, the truth, and the life (14:6): he is the road home to the Father.
     - The vine (15:5): he is our life and strength; apart from him, nothing.
2. **Sort: what he is to us.** Look at the list. What's the pattern? What does Jesus keep saying he is?
   - Land it: he keeps saying he's exactly what you need most. Hungry? I'm the bread. In the dark? I'm the light. Lost? I'm the way. Dead? I'm the resurrection. Empty? I'm the vine. Every "I am" meets a real human need with himself. He doesn't just give you things. He gives you himself.

### The absolute "I am" (about 8 min)

3. **Gather.** There's one more kind of "I am" in John, without anything after it. Read 8:58: "before Abraham was, I am." What happens right after Jesus says it (verse 59)?
   - Draw out: they pick up stones to kill him.
4. **Sort: who he is in himself.** Why would "before Abraham was, I am" make them want to stone him? What is he claiming? (Think of Exodus 3:14, where God tells Moses his name is "I AM.")
   - Land it: he's using God's own name, "I AM," the name God gave Moses at the burning bush. Jesus is claiming to be that God, existing before Abraham, eternal. The predicated sayings tell you what Christ is to you. This one tells you who he is in himself: the God of the burning bush, standing in the temple. That's why they reached for stones.

### The way home (about 5 min)

5. **Deeper (whole class).** Of all the "I am" sayings, 14:6 is the one people argue about: "no one comes to the Father except through me." In our mixed-faith room, how do we hold that honestly and warmly, the way we did back in Unit 19?
   - Draw out: we hold both halves. It's an exclusive claim, one way to the Father. And it's the kindest news there is if it's true: there IS a way home, and it's open to anyone. Jesus said it to comfort a scared friend (Thomas), not to slam a door. We state it plainly and warmly, and we don't soften it or weaponize it.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what do the "I am" sayings tell you Jesus is?
   - Exactly what you need most (bread, light, shepherd, life, way, vine), and the eternal God himself ("I am").

Circulate. Short probes. "Which one is that?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Framed fresh.

- Land the pattern. Listen to how Jesus talks about himself. Bread. Light. Shepherd. Life. Way. Vine. Every single time, he names himself as the thing you're missing. Whatever your deepest need is, his answer is the same: I am. Not "I'll get you some." I am it. He gives you himself.
- Land the name. And under all of them is the biggest claim in the book: "before Abraham was, I am." He took the name of God from the burning bush and put it on himself. The man saying "I'm the bread, I'm the light" is the God who made you.
- Land the way home. And the sharpest one, the one that costs something to believe: I am the way, and no one comes to the Father except through me. That's not narrow-mindedness. It's the best news a lost person could hear: there's a way home, his name is Jesus, and the door is open to you.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus tells you who he is with two words, "I am," and offers himself as everything you need and the only road home.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Every 'I am' saying offers you Jesus himself for your deepest need. Which need do you most feel, and which 'I am' meets it?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.
