**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain why the world has "no excuse for their sin" (15:22-24).
2. Students will explain "they hated me without a cause" as Christ the innocent sufferer (15:25).
3. Students will explain the Spirit's job: to bear witness about Christ, alongside the church (15:26-27).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus did nothing to deserve being hated.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the Spirit's whole job here is to point people to Jesus, and the church joins in.
- **Stretch** (disciple): "hated without a cause" (Ps 69:4 / 35:19) as the innocent sufferer; two witnesses, one message.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. This is the unit's closer, so leave room to gather the chapter.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "The Spirit of truth... he will bear witness about me" (John 15:26).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What's one thing from John 15 I don't want to forget?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 15:22-27 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 15, verses 22 through 27.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus explains why the world's hatred is inexcusable, and then ends on hope, the Spirit who testifies about him.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verses 22 and 24, Jesus says the world has "no excuse for their sin." What had they seen and heard that leaves them with no excuse?
   - Cold-call check: they'd heard Jesus' words and seen his works, things no one else did, and still hated him. They can't say they didn't know.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 25: "they hated me without a cause." What does "without a cause" mean about Jesus? What did he do to earn their hatred?
   - Guide them: nothing. "Without a cause" means groundless, undeserved. Jesus healed, taught, loved, and told the truth, and they hated him for it. He's the innocent one. The hatred says everything about them and nothing against him.
3. **Core.** Jesus says this fulfills what was "written in their Law" (the Psalms). Why would it matter that the innocent one being hated for no reason was written down ahead of time?
   - Land it: it means even this, even the groundless hatred and the cross it's driving toward, isn't a surprise or a failure. It's exactly what Scripture said would happen to God's chosen one. God is still in control, right through the hatred.
4. **Core.** Now the turn to hope. Read verses 26 and 27. Who does Jesus promise, and what's that Spirit's job? Who else bears witness?
   - Draw out: the Helper, the Spirit of truth, whom Jesus sends from the Father. His job is to "bear witness about me," to testify about Jesus. And the disciples bear witness too. Two witnesses, one message: Christ.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** The Spirit testifies about Jesus, and the disciples testify about Jesus. Same message, from both. What does that tell you the Spirit is for, and what does it make the church's job, right down to you?
   - Draw out: the Spirit isn't a vague feeling; he has a specific assignment, to make Christ known. And the church shares that assignment. When a Christian points someone to Jesus, the Spirit is doing the same thing right alongside them. Your witness and the Spirit's are aimed at the same person: Jesus.
6. **Memory verse cement.** Have students recite John 15:5 together, then in pairs. Make sure "apart from me you can do nothing" is solid, and that they can say the vine-and-branches picture in their own words.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the chapter?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and close the unit. Framed fresh.

- Name the innocence. The world hated Jesus without a cause. He never gave them a reason. He healed the sick and told the truth and washed feet, and they wanted him dead. When you see the world's hatred of a spotless Jesus, you're looking straight at what sin is.
- Show them God still in charge. Even that groundless hatred was written down ahead of time. The cross it was driving toward wasn't God losing control. It was God's plan, right on schedule.
- End on the Spirit and the mission. Jesus doesn't leave them staring at hatred. He promises the Spirit, whose whole job is to point to him, and he tells his friends they'll point to him too. That's the church's job and yours: bear witness to Jesus, with the Spirit doing it right beside you.
- Gather the chapter. This whole unit was one picture: stay joined to Jesus. He's the vine, and out of him comes fruit, love, joy, friendship, and even the courage to be hated for him. The Christian life is a Person you stay connected to.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus, hated without cause and testified to by the Spirit, is the vine his people abide in and the one they bear witness to.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "The Spirit's job and yours are the same: point people to Jesus. Where's one place you could do that this week?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep John 15:5 sharp; it stays in the rolling memory quizzes. Read John 16:1-4 once before next class.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 15:5 joins the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up Unit 21: Jesus finishes preparing them for what's coming, and promises that in him they'll have peace (the next memory verse, 16:33).

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- This is a sermon-less fresh-build closing the unit. Two payoffs: Christ the innocent sufferer "hated without a cause" (Ps 69:4 / 35:19), and the Spirit as a Person with a specific job (bear witness to Christ), the U17/U19 point held.
- Keep the Spirit concrete and Trinitarian: sent by Jesus from the Father, aimed at making Christ known. Guard against a vague Spirit.
- Cement 15:5 here (planted at L2). This is the unit's graded anchor.
- Close by gathering the whole chapter on the vine, and hand off to Unit 21 (John 16, plants 16:33). Also models the Q3 project skill: land a chapter on Christ, not on a moral.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
