# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 9 Lesson 2 of 7: walking on the water (John 6:16-24)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U9 Bread of Life |
| Lesson | 2 of 7 |
| Date / Day type | Wed Oct 28 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 6:16-24 (ESV) |
| Big idea | Jesus walks to his frightened disciples across the storm, says the divine name over their fear, and gets in the boat. |
| Christ connection | Jesus comes to his own in the middle of the trouble and joins them in it. |
| Memory verse | No new verse yet this lesson. Keep the five sharp: John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16, 4:24. The unit's new verse (John 6:35) plants at L4. |
| Resources | Paired sermon: Adam Vega, "The Savior's Power" (John 6:1-21), second half. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will describe the disciples' situation on the sea (6:17-19).
2. Students will explain what Jesus says and does when he reaches them (6:20-21).
3. Students will explain why "It is I" carries the weight of God's own name (Exodus 3:14).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): the disciples are scared in a storm, and Jesus walks right out to them.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): John's point is that Jesus gets in the boat, present in the trouble.
- **Stretch** (disciple): "It is I" (I am) as the divine name (Exodus 3:14), and the "I am" thread that runs through John.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "It is I; do not be afraid" (John 6:20).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Where do I feel like I'm in a storm right now?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write the fear honestly, even if you're not sure anyone's out there.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 6:16-24 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 6, verses 16 through 24.
- One-sentence setup: after the feeding, the disciples row across the sea at night and hit a storm.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verses 17 through 19, what's the disciples' situation? Name three things (time of day, the water, how far out).
   - Cold-call check: it's dark, the sea is rough with a strong wind, and they've rowed three or four miles. Jesus isn't with them yet.
2. **Entry.** When you're scared and alone, what do you most want someone to do, fix the problem or just be there? Say why.
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verses 19 through 21. What does Jesus do, what does he say, and what happens when they take him into the boat?
   - Guide them: he walks on the water to them, says "It is I; do not be afraid," and they gladly take him in. The boat reaches shore right away. He comes to them in the storm.
4. **Core.** The other Gospels say Jesus calmed this storm. John doesn't mention that. John's whole miracle is that Jesus got into the boat with them. Why might that detail matter for someone in a hard season?
   - Land it: Jesus doesn't always take the storm away. He comes into it with you. His presence in the trouble is the gift, before or even without the fix.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** "It is I" is literally "I am," the name God gave himself when Moses asked who he was (Exodus 3:14). Why would Jesus say that name while walking on a stormy sea?
   - Draw out: only God walks on the sea and quiets fear with his own name. Jesus is claiming to be that God. This is the same "I am" the woman heard at the well (4:26), and it sets up the big one coming, "I am the bread of life" (6:35).
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what did Jesus do for his disciples in the storm?
   - He came to them on the water and got in the boat, and said "do not be afraid."

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Build on the second half of sermon 07.

- Start with the fear. Dark water, a strong wind, miles from shore, and Jesus not there yet. Some student in the room is in a version of that boat right now.
- Name what Jesus does. He walks out into the storm to reach them. The sermon puts it plainly: Jesus will do whatever he needs to do to get to you in your hour of need.
- Hold the honest part. John doesn't say the storm stopped when Jesus showed up. He says Jesus got in the boat. Sometimes God comes into the trouble before he takes it away, and having him in the boat is enough.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus comes to his own in the storm, speaks God's own name over their fear, and gets in the boat.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus got in the boat before the storm stopped. Where do you need him with you in the trouble, even if the trouble doesn't go away yet?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 6:25-29 once before Thursday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L3: the next day the crowd hunts Jesus down, and he asks them why they're really looking for him.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Build on sermon 07's second half: the miracle John keeps is that Jesus gets in the boat. Adam tells this out of his own hard season (a frightening pregnancy) where God got in the boat without fixing the storm right away. Use it, gently, for the anxious student.
- Teach "It is I" (I am) at the disciple tier as the divine name (Exodus 3:14). Connect it back to 4:26 and forward to 6:35. Don't over-explain it for the whole room; give the plain comfort first.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
