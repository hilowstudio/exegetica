**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain what Jesus has given his people, and why (17:22).
2. Students will explain the unity as Christ in them and the Father in Christ (17:23).
3. Students will explain what the world will know: the Father sent Jesus and loves his people (17:23).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): God loves Christians the way he loves Jesus.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the unity is Christ living in his people, deeper than getting along.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the glory shared by the indwelling Christ; the Father's love for believers equal to his love for the Son.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "You... loved them even as you loved me" (John 17:23).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Do I believe God loves me as much as he loves Jesus? Why or why not?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write the honest answer.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 17:22-23 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 17, verses 22 through 23.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus keeps praying for the unity of his people, and says something almost too big to believe about how much the Father loves them.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 22, what does Jesus say he has given his people? Why did he give it?
   - Cold-call check: the glory the Father gave him, so that they may be one.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 23: "I in them and you in me, that they may become perfectly one." What makes Christians actually one, according to this verse? Is it that they all agree, or something deeper?
   - Guide them: something deeper. Jesus lives IN his people, and the Father lives in Jesus. So the oneness comes from God actually being in them, connecting them from the inside. It's not a club where everyone happens to agree. It's people who all have the same Christ living in them.
3. **Core.** Verse 23 says the world will know two things when Christians are one. What are they?
   - Draw out: that the Father sent Jesus, and that the Father loved his people "even as you loved me." The united church is proof to the world that Jesus is real and that God loves his people.
4. **Core (the stunning line).** Read the last part of verse 23 slowly: God "loved them even as you loved me." How much does the Father love his people, according to this? Compare it to how he loves Jesus.
   - Land it: the same amount. The Father loves his people the way he loves his own Son. That's not "God tolerates you" or "God loves you a little." God loves you with the exact love he has for Jesus. Sit on that. It's almost too much to believe, and Jesus prayed it out loud.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Verse 22 says Jesus gave his people "the glory that you have given me." How does Jesus living in his people (verse 23) connect to him sharing his glory with them (verse 22)?
   - Draw out: the glory is shared because Christ himself dwells in them. They partake of his glory as people joined to him, members of his body. It's not that they earn glory or manufacture it. He's in them, and his glory comes with him. Union with Christ means his glory becomes theirs.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: how much does the Father love his people, according to verse 23?
   - As much as he loves Jesus, his Son.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Framed fresh.

- Land the love, and don't rush it. Read verse 23 again: God "loved them even as you loved me." Do you hear that? The Father loves you with the same love he has for Jesus. Not a smaller version. Not on a good day. The full thing. If you've spent your life thinking God puts up with you, this verse takes the floor out from under that. He loves you like he loves his Son.
- Show them the unity underneath it. And the way that love holds all his people together is that Christ lives in them. That's why the church is one, when it's really the church: the same Jesus is inside all of them. Deeper than agreement, deeper than personality, deeper than getting along.
- Make it evidence. And here's the weight of it: when Christians actually love each other like that, the world sees it and believes. Your love for other believers isn't a private feeling. It's a signpost pointing the world to Jesus.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus lives in his people, shares his glory with them, and the Father loves them the way he loves his Son.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "The Father loves you the same way he loves Jesus (verse 23). If you really believed that today, what's one thing it would change?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 17:3. Read John 17:24-26 once before Wednesday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L8: Jesus finishes his prayer by asking that his people be with him forever and that the Father's love live in them.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Two things to land: the unity is Christ indwelling his people (17:23, deeper than agreement; the same guard as L6 against both false unity and sectarian dismissal), and the stunning 17:23 that the Father loves believers "even as" he loves the Son. Let the love land slowly; don't rush past it.
- 17:22: the glory shared by the indwelling Christ (union with Christ; we partake as members of him). Keep it for the disciple tier.
- Derive the frame from sermon 31; cite it, don't read it aloud.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
