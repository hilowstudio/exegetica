**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will list what Jesus knows about himself on this night (13:1, 13:3).
2. Students will explain how Jesus' security lets him stoop to serve.
3. Students will explain "he loved them to the end" (13:1).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): the person in charge of everything is about to do the lowest job in the house.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): Jesus serves out of security, not to earn anything.
- **Stretch** (disciple): humility that flows from a settled standing (the meekness of John the Baptist, Unit 5).

---

**Talk budget:** short day, single-focus. One key question worked in groups. Keep your sustained voice well under half the 36 minutes.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (5 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away. This is the first day of the second half of John.
- Centering line on the board: "having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end" (John 13:1).
- One quiet minute in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Where do I grab for status or approval?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3 min)

- Have one student read John 13:1-3 aloud, slowly. Read it twice.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 13, verses 1 through 3.
- One-sentence setup: before Jesus does anything on his last night, John tells us exactly what Jesus knows about himself.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 16 min, one key question, no lecture)

Open with a quick read-check, then work the key question.

1. **Read-check (any student can answer).** Look at verses 1 and 3. List everything the text says Jesus knows about himself right now.
   - Cold-call check: his hour has come to go to the Father, the Father has given all things into his hands, and he came from God and is going back to God. He knows he holds everything and knows exactly who he is.
2. **Key question (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Hold onto verse 3: Jesus knows he holds all things and knows where he is from and where he is going. Now read the very next verse (13:4), where he takes off his clothes to wash feet like a servant. Work this: how does being that secure make him able to go that low? What would an insecure person have done with all that power?
   - Guide the groups, don't lecture. Draw out: an insecure person uses power to get served, to prove themselves, to climb. Jesus, completely sure of who he is and whose he is, has nothing to prove, so he's free to kneel. His humility isn't low self-esteem. It's the overflow of total security. Remember John the Baptist back in Unit 5? He could decrease because his identity was settled. Jesus can go all the way to the floor for the same reason, and further.
   - For the disciple tier: that same settled security is given to you in Christ. You don't serve to earn your place. You serve because your place is already secure.
3. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what lets Jesus kneel to wash feet?
   - He's so secure in who he is and whose he is that he has nothing to prove and is free to serve.

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and set up the whole unit. Framed fresh.

- Name the shocking distance. Verse 3 says the Father put all things into his hands. The very next thing those hands do is wash dirty feet. The God who holds the universe holds a towel.
- Explain the freedom. Jesus can do that because he isn't trying to prove anything or earn anything. He knows exactly who he is. That's what makes real humility possible. Insecure people grab. Secure people give.
- Turn it toward the room. So much of what teenagers do to climb, to be seen, to prove they matter, comes from not being sure they're enough. Jesus offers you a settled place in him, and out of that place, you get free to serve without needing the credit.
- "He loved them to the end." Verse 1 says it. He loved his own to the last moment and to the fullest degree. What you're about to watch, the towel and the cross, is that love in action.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus is the Lord of everything who was secure enough to kneel, and he loved his own all the way to the end.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus served from total security, with nothing to prove. Where would being sure of your place in Christ free you to serve instead of climb?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 13:4-11 once before Friday. That's the foot-washing itself.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L2: Jesus takes the towel and washes their feet, and Peter tries to refuse.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: short day, single-focus. One key question, worked in groups. Keep it moving and protect Move 4.
- This is a fresh-build (no sermon). The frame in 13:1-3 is the interpretive key to the whole foot-washing: the humility flows from the security. Tie it to the meekness of Unit 5 (John the Baptist decreasing from a settled standing).
- Teach "he loved them to the end" (13:1) with its double sense: to the last moment, and to the uttermost.
- This opens the Book of Glory. Set the mood as intimate and warm, a dying man's love for his own.
