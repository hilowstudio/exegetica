# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 18 Lesson 7 of 7: the towel and the cross (John 13 synthesis)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U18 The towel and the basin |
| Lesson | 7 of 7 |
| Date / Day type | Fri Jan 29 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 13:1-38 (whole-chapter synthesis) |
| Big idea | John 13 holds the whole gospel in one room: the Lord who kneels, the washing that points to the cross, control even over betrayal, and a new command to love. |
| Christ connection | Jesus the whole way through the chapter, the towel and the cross, whose love makes a new people. |
| Memory verse | John 13:34-35 was planted at L5. Review and cement it here, do not re-plant. Keep the nine earlier anchors sharp too. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will connect the four movements of John 13 (the kneel, the cleansing, the betrayal, the new command).
2. Students will recite and apply John 13:34-35.
3. Students will name where the chapter lands on Christ (the model for the Q3 project).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): one chapter, one room, the whole heart of who Jesus is.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the towel and the cross are the same love, measured for us.
- **Stretch** (disciple): how to teach a chapter so it lands on Christ (the Q3 project skill).

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. This is a synthesis and review day, so let students do the connecting.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end" (John 13:1).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What's one thing from John 13 I don't want to forget?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 13:1 and 13:34-35 aloud, the bookends of the chapter.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 13.
- One-sentence setup: today we pull the whole chapter together and see how it all fits.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students do the connecting.

1. **The four movements (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** John 13 has four big moves. In your group, put one sentence to each:
   - The kneel (13:1-3): Jesus, knowing he holds all things, gets on the floor to wash feet.
   - The cleansing (13:4-11): the washing points to the cross, where he makes his people clean.
   - The betrayal (13:18-30): Jesus stays in complete control even as Judas leaves into the night.
   - The new command (13:31-35): love one another, as I have loved you.
   - Cold-call check: have each group share their sentence for one move.
2. **Connect the towel and the cross.** Look at "as I have loved you" in verse 34. Point back to the towel (verses 4-5) and forward to the cross. How are the towel and the cross the same love?
   - Draw out: both are Jesus lowering himself all the way down to serve and save people who don't deserve it. The towel is a small picture of the cross. "As I have loved you" means love like the towel and the cross.
3. **Where does the chapter land on Jesus?** If you had to say in one sentence who Jesus is in John 13, what would you say?
   - Land it: Jesus is the Lord of everything who kneels to serve and dies to save, and calls his people to love the same way. That's the chapter. Everything in it points to him.
4. **The project skill (for the Q3 groups).** Notice what we just did. We didn't stop at "be humble" or "be nice." We landed on who Jesus is and what he asks. That's exactly what your group project needs to do with your Farewell chapter: teach the text, and land it on Christ.
   - Quick check: what would it look like to teach John 13 so the class ends on Jesus instead of on a moral?
5. **Memory verse cement.** Have students say 13:34-35 together, then in pairs. Make sure the "as I have loved you" and the "by this all people will know" are solid.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the chapter?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and close the unit. Framed fresh.

- Gather it. In one room, on one night, John shows you the whole thing: the Lord of the universe on his knees with a towel, the washing that reaches to the cross, a Savior in total control even of his own betrayal, and a new command that turns his love into the mark of his people.
- Hold the two together one more time. His glory and his humility are the same person. The King is the servant on the floor. That's the answer to "who is Jesus" that the whole first half of the book built toward, now shown in a basin of water.
- Point ahead, warmly. This is the doorway into his last night. Next unit, he turns to these same scared friends and says, "let not your hearts be troubled." The best of John is right in front of us.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus loved his own to the end, kneeling with a towel and going to a cross, and made that love the badge of everyone who belongs to him.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "John 13 shows the Lord of everything kneeling to serve and dying to save. What's one way that changes how you'll treat someone this week?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 13:34-35. Read John 14:1-7 once before next class.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 13:34-35 stays in the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up Unit 19: Jesus comforts his troubled disciples and says, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life" (the next memory verse, 14:6).

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: this is a synthesis and review day. Let students connect the movements rather than re-teaching each scene. Keep your voice light.
- 13:34-35 was planted at L5. Cement it here (review), do not re-plant.
- Model the Q3 project skill explicitly: teach the text and land it on Christ, not on a moral. John 13 is the first chapter groups may teach, so this lesson shows them the move.
- Close by handing off into the Farewell Discourse. Unit 19 opens with "let not your hearts be troubled" (14:1) and plants 14:6.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day.
