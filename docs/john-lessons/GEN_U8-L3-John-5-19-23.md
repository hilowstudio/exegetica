| Christ connection | Jesus shares the Father's own work and honor, which leaves no room to call him only a good teacher. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain what 5:19 claims: the Son does only what the Father does, and does all the Father does.
2. Students will name the two God-only acts the Son shares (5:21-22, giving life and judging).
3. Students will explain why "honor the Son just as they honor the Father" (5:23) rules out calling Jesus only a good teacher.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus says he does what only God does. That's a claim you have to decide about.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): "good teacher" isn't an option Jesus leaves open here.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the dependence in 5:19 is the proof of the equality.

---

**Talk budget:** short day, single-focus. One key question worked in groups. Keep your sustained voice well under half the 36 minutes.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (5 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "that all may honor the Son, just as they honor the Father" (John 5:23).
- One quiet minute in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Who do I actually think Jesus is?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3 min)

- Have one student read John 5:19-23 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 5, verses 19 through 23.
- One-sentence setup: last class Jesus claimed to do God's own work, and now he explains exactly what he means.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 18 min, one key question, no lecture)

Open with two quick read-checks, then work the key question.

1. **Read-check.** In verse 21, name the two things Jesus says the Son does. (Who else does those?)
   - The Son "gives life" and (verse 22) has been given "all judgment." Raising the dead and judging are things only God does.
2. **Read-check.** In verse 23, how does Jesus say people should honor the Son?
   - "Just as they honor the Father." Equal honor.
3. **Key question (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** People love to say Jesus was a great moral teacher and leave it there. Look at verses 19 through 23. Work this: does Jesus leave that option open? What is he actually claiming, and what does verse 23 do to the "good teacher" idea?
   - Guide the groups, don't lecture. Draw out: Jesus claims to do exactly what the Father does, to give life, to judge everyone, and to deserve the same honor God gets. A good teacher who says all that about himself is either telling the truth or badly wrong. There's no calm middle where he's just a wise man. He forces a verdict: reject him or worship him.
   - Push the disciple tier: notice verse 19 says the Son "can do nothing of his own accord." That's not Jesus being less than God. It's him being so perfectly one with the Father that he never acts alone. The dependence proves the equality.

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. This is the sermon's first question, "Is Jesus God?"

- Say the claim without softening it. Jesus takes God's own jobs (giving life, judging the world) and God's own honor. He puts himself where only God belongs.
- Close the exits. The room will want to keep Jesus as a kind teacher they admire from a distance. This passage takes that option away. A teacher who claims God's work and God's honor is either lying, deluded, or telling the truth. Those are the choices.
- Make it personal, warmly. It's a person asking you what you'll do with him. And the good news of the next lesson is that worshiping him leads straight to life.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus does the Father's own work and takes the Father's own honor, so the only honest responses are to reject him or worship him.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus doesn't leave 'good teacher' on the table. Which way do you lean right now about who he is, and what makes you lean that way?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 5:24-30 once before Friday. That lesson has an optional memory verse.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L4: what this Son does with his authority, give life and raise the dead.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: short day, single-focus. One key question, worked in groups, not a lecture on the Trinity. Keep it a person making a claim that forces a verdict.
- Build on sermon 06's trilemma: Jesus makes you either want him dead or worship him, with no "good teacher" exit. Let that land as the point of the day.
- Guard the dependence line (5:19). "Can do nothing of his own accord" is the proof of his perfect unity with the Father. Say it once, clearly, for the disciple tier.
- Day-type note: Thursday 36 minutes. Keep the Work block to the one key question and protect Move 4.
