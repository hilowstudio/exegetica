# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 2 Lesson 4 of 4: Philip, Nathanael, and the ladder (John 1:43-51)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U2 Witness & first disciples |
| Lesson | 4 of 4 |
| Date / Day type | Mon Sep 21 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 1:43-51 (ESV) |
| Big idea | The chain reaches a flat-out skeptic, and being known by Jesus turns him into a worshiper. The pile of titles resolves in Nathanael's confession, and Jesus names himself the ladder between heaven and earth. |
| Christ connection | Jesus is the meeting place of heaven and earth, the true Bethel, the house of God now a person. You reach heaven by coming to him. |
| Memory verse | Seal John 1:12 today (planted in L3). Nathanael believes, another man is received and given the family, and "believed in his name" reaches its height in "Son of God, King of Israel." Roll 1:14 in review: "dwelt among us" lands on the ladder. |
| Resources | Paired sermon: Adam Vega 02, "The First Disciples" (John 1:35-51), second half. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will retell how Philip meets Nathanael's scorn and how Nathanael's scorn breaks (1:46-49).
2. Students will identify the titles given to Jesus in the passage and state that each names the same person.
3. Students will explain "the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man" (1:51): Jesus is the ladder where heaven and earth meet.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Nathanael came to scoff and left worshiping, and what changed him was being seen and known; Jesus knows you fully and calls you anyway.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the seven titles across this unit are one person; can you say plainly that they all mean Jesus?
- **Stretch** (disciple): the Jacob-Nathanael reversal (the deceiver's name redeemed), the Bethel ladder of Genesis 28, and "Son of Man" reaching to Daniel 7, the heavenly figure given everlasting dominion.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. This is the unit climax, so let the titles resolve and land the ladder.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!" (John 1:49).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Nathanael was fully known by Jesus before he ever trusted him. Does the idea that Jesus already knows everything about you feel like a threat or a comfort? Be honest."
- On-ramp for the doubter: honest either way.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 1:43-51 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 1, verses 43 through 51.
- One-sentence setup: the last man in the chain shows up already sneering, and by the end he is worshiping.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** Philip tells Nathanael about Jesus. What does Nathanael say back (verse 46)? How does Philip answer the scorn?
   - Draw out: "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?" Philip does not argue. He says, "Come and see," the same invitation from the day before. He lets Jesus make his own case.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verses 47-48. What does Jesus say about Nathanael before Nathanael has said anything, and what breaks the scorn?
   - Guide them: Jesus greets him as "an Israelite indeed, in whom there is no deceit," and then says, "when you were under the fig tree, I saw you." Jesus knew him before he ever saw Jesus. Being truly known, and named for something good, undoes the sneer.
3. **Core.** How does Nathanael respond in verse 49? Stack up the names Jesus has been given across this whole unit. What are they doing?
   - Land it: "Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!" Across the unit: the Lamb of God, Rabbi, Messiah, Son of God, King of Israel, Son of Man. They are not a vocabulary list. They are camera angles on one person, building toward what Thomas will say at the end: "My Lord and my God" (20:28).
4. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** In verse 51 Jesus promises Nathanael he will see "the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man." That is Jacob's dream (Genesis 28), the ladder between heaven and earth. What is Jesus claiming?
   - Draw out: Jacob saw a ladder in the desert and called the place the house of God. Jesus says he himself is that ladder, the one place heaven and earth are joined and God comes down to be with people. The house of God is now a person. The Word "dwelt among us" (1:14) is standing right there.
5. **Cold-call comprehension check.** What changed Nathanael? What does Jesus call himself in verse 51?
   - Being known by Jesus; the ladder, the Son of Man where heaven and earth meet.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where is that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. This is the unit's climax.

- Preach the ladder. You do not climb your way up to God. You come to the person who is the ladder, and the house of God is now a man standing in front of you.
- Preach the grace of being known, and lead with it. Jesus saw Nathanael honestly, named something true and good in him, and called him. Being fully known by Christ is safe for the one who hides. He knows everything and calls you anyway.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus is the ladder joining heaven and earth, and being known by him turns a scoffer into a worshiper.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Nathanael was fully known and still wanted. If that is true of you with Jesus, what would it change about coming to him?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 1:12. Read John 2:1-11 once before next class.
- To the gradebook: today's participation and the Respond entry. Keep 1:12 and 1:14 in the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up Unit 3: the first sign at a wedding in Cana, water into wine, and Jesus clearing the temple.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Lead with grace on "when you were under the fig tree, I saw you" (1:48). For an anxious teen, "he sees everything you hide" lands as a threat. The scene is grace: Jesus saw Nathanael honestly and called him, so being fully known by Christ is safe.
- Refuse the vocabulary quiz. The titles are angles on one person, building toward 20:28, not names to recite.
- Grounding for your own prep: "Israelite indeed" plays on Jacob (Israel), the deceiver, now a man "in whom there is no deceit," and Jesus hands him Jacob's own dream (Genesis 28), so he is elevated above Jacob; "Son of Man" reaches to Daniel 7:13-14 (everlasting dominion) and pays off when Stephen sees the Son of Man standing (Acts 7:56). One Bethel note and one Son-of-Man note is the ration.
- A scoffer is among the called. Do not turn discipleship into a personality test.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day.
