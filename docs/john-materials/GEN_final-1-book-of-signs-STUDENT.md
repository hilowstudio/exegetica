## Part C. The "I am" sayings (15 points)

C1. List the "I am" sayings from the Book of Signs (John 1-12) and give the reference for each. (There are five predicated sayings; for the ceiling point, name the one absolute "I am" as well.)

C2. What does "before Abraham was, I am" (8:58) claim that the other "I am" sayings do not, and how did the crowd react (8:59)?

## Part D. Short answer (20 points)

Answer in one or two sentences each.

D1. In the Prologue, who is the Word, and what happens in 1:14?

D2. What does a "sign" do in John's Gospel, and why does John say he recorded them (20:30-31)?

D3. What must a person have to see the kingdom of God (chapter 3), and what image does Jesus use in 3:14-15 for how he will save?

D4. At the well in chapter 4, what does Jesus offer the Samaritan woman, and what does he say true worship is (4:24)?

D5. In chapter 5, what does Jesus claim about his relationship to the Father, and why did it make the leaders want to kill him (5:18)?

D6. What is the sixth sign (chapter 9), and what does John show through the man's growing sight and the leaders' response?

D7. In the Good Shepherd chapter, what does the shepherd do for the sheep (10:11), and what does Jesus say about his life in 10:18?

D8. Chapter 12 closes the public ministry. What does Jesus mean by "the hour has come" (12:23), and what does the grain of wheat picture (12:24)?

## Part E. Essay (20 points)

Choose ONE. Write a clear, organized response that shows you understand what John is showing about who Jesus is.

**E1.** John says he wrote the signs so his readers would believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God (20:30-31). Trace at least three of the seven signs and explain what each reveals about who Jesus is, and how the set builds toward that purpose.

**E2.** The "I am" sayings run through the Book of Signs. Choose three and explain what each shows about who Jesus claims to be. Then explain what "before Abraham was, I am" (8:58) adds that the others do not.

**E3.** The Book of Signs ends on "the hour has come" (12:23) and on unbelief (12:37). Explain what John means by "the hour," and how chapter 12 turns the Gospel from the public signs toward the cross.
