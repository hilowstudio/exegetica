# Daily page, Unit 9 Lesson 2: Walking on the water (John 6:16-24)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "It is I; do not be afraid." (John 6:20, ESV)

Where do I feel like I am in a storm right now? Write the fear honestly, even if you are not sure anyone is out there.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:16-24** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: that same night, after the feeding, the disciples row across the sea in the dark and hit a storm, and Jesus is not with them yet.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 17 through 19, name three things about the disciples' situation (the time of day, the water, how far out they are).

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 19 through 21. What does Jesus do, what does he say, and what happens when they take him into the boat?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "It is I" is literally "I am," the name God gave himself when Moses asked who he was. Read Exodus 3:13-14. Hold the question of why Jesus would say that name while walking on a stormy sea.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what did Jesus do for his disciples in the storm?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus got in the boat before the storm stopped. Where do you need him with you in the trouble, even if the trouble does not go away yet? Write honestly.
