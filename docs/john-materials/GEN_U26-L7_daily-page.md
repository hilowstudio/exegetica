# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 7: My Lord and my God (John 20:24-29)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Do not disbelieve, but believe." (John 20:27, ESV)

What is a real doubt or question I have about Jesus? Be honest. This page is safe, and doubts are welcome here.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:24-29** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Thomas wasn't there the first time, and he won't take anyone's word for it.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 25, the others tell Thomas they have seen the Lord. What does Thomas say he needs before he will believe? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verses 26 and 27, Jesus comes back. Watch what he does with Thomas's doubt. Does he shame him? What does he offer him? Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

In verse 28, what does Thomas finally say to Jesus? Write his exact words.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verse 29 Jesus says, "Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed." Thomas got to see and touch. Hold the question of who Jesus is speaking that blessing over, and whether you might be standing inside it. Don't tie it off; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus met the doubter with patience, not shame, and the doubter made the biggest confession in the book. Write the one thing you want to remember about how Jesus treats honest questions.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus met Thomas's doubt with patience, and the doubter became the biggest believer in the book. What honest doubt or question do you want to bring to Jesus instead of hiding it? Write honestly. Doubts are welcome here, and belief is never scored.
