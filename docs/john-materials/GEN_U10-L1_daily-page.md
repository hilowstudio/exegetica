## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 7:1-13** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: it is the Feast of Booths, a week-long celebration in Jerusalem remembering how God cared for Israel in the wilderness, and Jesus' brothers are pushing him to go make a splash there.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 3 and 4, what do Jesus' brothers tell him to do?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 5. What does John tell us about the brothers, right after their advice?

______________________________________________________________

In verses 6 and 8, Jesus says "my time has not yet come." Whose schedule is he keeping instead of his family's?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 2 says this is the Feast of Booths, when Israel remembered God dwelling among them in the wilderness. Read John 1:14, where the Word "dwelt among us." Hold the question of what is rich about Jesus keeping this feast in person.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, why would Jesus not go up to the feast the way his brothers wanted?

## 5. Respond

Jesus knows what it is like when your own family does not get your faith. Write honestly about your home, and one way you could keep trusting Jesus there. Honest is welcome, and no one grades what you believe.
