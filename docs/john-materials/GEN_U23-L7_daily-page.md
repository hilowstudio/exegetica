# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 7: The glory he shares (John 17:22-23)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "...that the world may know that you sent me and loved them even as you loved me." (John 17:23, ESV)

Do I believe God loves me as much as he loves Jesus? Why or why not? Write the honest answer.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:22-23** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: still listening in on the Son praying for everyone who will believe. He keeps praying for their unity, and says something almost too big to believe about how much the Father loves them.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 22, what does Jesus say he has given his people? Why did he give it?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 23, what makes Christians actually one? Write what the verse says ("I in them and you in me").

______________________________________________________________

In verse 23, what two things will the world know when Christians are one?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read the last line of verse 23 slowly, God "loved them even as you loved me." Hold the question of how much that is, measured against how the Father loves his own Son.

## 4. The one thing from today

Read the last part of verse 23 again. How much does the Father love his people, compared to how he loves Jesus? Write what you found.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The Father loves you the same way he loves Jesus (verse 23). If you really believed that today, what is one thing it would change? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
