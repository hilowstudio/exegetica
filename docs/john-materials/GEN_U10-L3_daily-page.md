# Daily page, Unit 10 Lesson 3: Can this be the Christ? (John 7:25-36)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "He who sent me is true, and him you do not know." (John 7:28, ESV)

How well do I actually know God, versus know about him? Write what you would want to know for sure about who Jesus is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 7:25-36** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the crowd in Jerusalem is arguing about whether Jesus could be the Christ, and the leaders send guards to arrest him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 27, what do some people claim to know about Jesus, and why do they think it rules him out as the Christ?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 28 and 29. What does Jesus say they do not actually know?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 30. They wanted to arrest Jesus and could not. What reason does the verse give?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verses 33 and 34 Jesus says he is "going to him who sent me," and the crowd thinks he means moving to another country (verse 35). Hold the question of where he was really talking about going.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, the crowd was sure they knew where Jesus came from. What did they miss?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

There is a difference between knowing about Jesus and knowing him. Which is more true of you right now, and what would move you toward actually knowing him? Write honestly.
