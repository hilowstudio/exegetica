# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 1: The stone is gone (John 20:1-2)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Now on the first day of the week Mary Magdalene came to the tomb early, while it was still dark, and saw that the stone had been taken away from the tomb." (John 20:1, ESV)

Where do I go, or what do I do, when I'm grieving something? Write one honest line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:1-2** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: last unit ended in the dark at a sealed grave. It's Sunday morning now, and Mary comes back.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 1, when does Mary come to the tomb, and what does she find when she gets there? Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 2, who does she run to, and what does she assume has happened to the body? Write her words.

______________________________________________________________

Look back at how Unit 25 ended: everyone came to that tomb to bury a dead man, and a stone was rolled over it. Write the one thing that has changed by verse 1.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Mary comes "while it was still dark." John tells you the time of day on purpose. Hold the question of what is about to happen to that darkness, and don't answer it yet.

## 4. The one thing from today

Mary was in the dark, minutes from the greatest joy of her life, and she had no idea. Write the one thing you want to remember about that.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Mary was in the dark, minutes from the greatest joy of her life. Where do you need to trust that your story isn't over where it feels like it ended? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and there is nothing here to earn.
