*Go deeper (optional):* one of God's commands is "honor your father and mother." Hold the question of how Jesus is keeping that command, perfectly, even here.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus is in the worst pain there is, and he still stops to make sure his mother will be cared for. Write the one thing that shows you about him.

## 5. Respond

Even on the cross, Jesus made sure the people he loved were taken care of. Who in your life needs you to think of them this week, even when you are stressed or hurting? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
