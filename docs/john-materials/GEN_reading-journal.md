# Reading Journal

> This is your Reading Journal for the year. Each class, you write down what you see in the passage and what it asks of you. Keep your entries here so they build up. Your teacher reads this one, and it counts as participation, not as a test. There is no wrong observation.

The three questions we ask of every passage:

1. Who is Jesus here?
2. What does believing him look like?
3. What does this ask of me?
