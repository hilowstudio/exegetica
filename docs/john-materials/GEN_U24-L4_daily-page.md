# Daily page, Unit 24 Lesson 4: The rooster crows (John 18:25-27)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "Peter again denied it, and at once a rooster crowed." (John 18:27, ESV)

Have I ever gone quiet about Jesus because it was easier? What happened? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 18:25-27** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: back at the fire, Peter is asked two more times if he knows Jesus.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 25 through 27, how many more times does Peter deny Jesus, and what happens right after the last denial? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Look back at John 13:38. What had Jesus told Peter would happen? Write it, and set it next to what just happened in verse 27.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* John notes Peter is warming himself at a charcoal fire (18:18). That same word for the fire shows up in only one other place, John 21:9, after the resurrection. Hold the question of what Jesus does for Peter at that second fire.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus knew Peter would deny him, and had already told him so, and still washed his feet and still went to the cross for him. Write the one thing that means for someone who has failed.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Peter failed as badly as a friend can fail, and it was not the end of his story. Where have you failed in a way that makes you feel disqualified? What might Peter's story say to that? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
