# Daily page, Unit 20 Lesson 2: Abide in me (John 15:4-5)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing." (John 15:5, ESV)

Where am I worn out from trying to be good on my own? Be honest about the exhaustion.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 15:4-5** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus gives the whole secret of the Christian life in one word, "abide."

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 4, what can a branch not do by itself, and what does it have to do to bear fruit?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 5, what happens to the branch that stays connected, and what can you do apart from Jesus? Write the last line exactly.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* this is Jesus' seventh and last "I am" saying. You have met the bread (6:35), the light (8:12), the door and the good shepherd (chapter 10), the resurrection and the life (11:25), and the way (14:6). Hold the question of why the set is now complete at seven.

## 4. The one thing from today

What is the branch's one job, and who grows the fruit?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"Apart from me you can do nothing." Is that harsh or freeing to you right now? What would change if you believed the fruit was Jesus' job to grow through you? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
