# Daily page, Unit 15 Lesson 2: I am the resurrection and the life (John 11:17-27)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live." (John 11:25, ESV)

What am I really hoping is true about death? If you have an honest question about what happens when we die, write that.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 11:17-27** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Lazarus has been dead four days, and grieving Martha comes out to meet Jesus on the road.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 21, what is the first thing Martha says to Jesus? In verse 24, when does Martha think her brother will rise?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 25 slowly. What does Jesus say he himself is? Write his exact words.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 25 and 26 together. There are two promises. What does Jesus say happens to the believer who dies? What does he say happens to the believer who is alive and believes?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 27, what does Martha call Jesus?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* you have heard Jesus say "I am" before, the bread, the light, the shepherd. Line this one up next to those. What is different about an "I am" that answers a grave? Hold the question for class.

## 4. The one thing from today

Say John 11:25 back in your own words. What does Jesus claim to be?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus asks Martha, "Do you believe this?" Write your honest answer, and what makes it hard or easy for you to believe. No one grades what you believe.

**Memory:** new verse. Learn John 11:25: "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live." Learn it in two chunks: (1) "I am the resurrection and the life," (2) "Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live."
**At home:** ask me what it means that the resurrection is a Person you can know now, and not only a someday.
