# Daily page, Unit 3 Lesson 4: The belief Jesus can read (John 2:23-25)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "He himself knew what was in man." (John 2:25, ESV)

If someone could see everything in you, would that feel like a threat or a relief? Be honest, either way.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 2:23-25** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the last three verses of the chapter, and the sting in its tail. People believed, and Jesus did not trust their belief.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 23, what did many people do, and why? Then verse 24: what did Jesus do, or not do, in response? Write both.

Verse 25 says Jesus "needed no one to bear witness about man, for he himself knew what was in man." In the text's own words, what does Jesus know?

*Go deeper (optional):* read the very last line of chapter 2, then the first line of chapter 3, "Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus" (John 3:1). John set that up on purpose. What kind of man do you expect Jesus to meet next? Hold it. We meet him tomorrow.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice that Jesus knows every heart in the crowd, and still holds himself out to be received.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

This is good news, not a test you could fail. Jesus already knows everything about you, and he still holds himself out to be received. Write your honest reaction to being that known and still wanted.
