## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 12:37-43** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: John stops the story to explain something troubling, why so many people saw Jesus' signs and still would not believe.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

This is a hard passage. It is okay to sit with it.

In verse 37, what had the people seen, and what did they do about it?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 37, then verse 39. Verse 37 says they "did not believe." What does verse 39 say about whether they "could" believe, and why? (Hold both. We will work how they fit in class.)

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 41. Whose glory does John say Isaiah saw?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 42 says many leaders actually believed, and would not say so, because they loved the glory that comes from people more than the glory of God. Where do you stay quiet about Jesus because of what people would think? Bring it, or just sit with it.

## 4. The one thing from today

After we work it, write in one sentence what verse 41 tells you about who Jesus is.

## 5. Respond

The honest response to this passage is not "I am glad I am not like them." It is "God, keep my heart soft." Write a version of that prayer in your own words. No one grades what you believe.
