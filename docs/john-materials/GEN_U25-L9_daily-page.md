# Daily page, Unit 25 Lesson 9: It is finished (John 19:28-30)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "When Jesus had received the sour wine, he said, 'It is finished,' and he bowed his head and gave up his spirit." (John 19:30, ESV)

What am I always trying to be good enough at? What would it feel like to hear "it is finished, you can stop earning it"? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 19:28-30** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus comes to his last moment, and speaks his final word. This is the center of the whole story.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 28, what does Jesus say, and what does John tell us it fulfilled? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 30, what does Jesus say right at the end? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 30, what does Jesus do right after he says it? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "It is finished" is one word in Greek, tetelestai, the word a merchant wrote across a bill when it was paid in full. Hold the question of what work Jesus means is finished, and what that changes for you.

## 4. The one thing from today

"He bowed his head and gave up his spirit." Look back at John 10:18. Write the one thing you notice about how Jesus dies, compared to how a person usually dies.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

If the most important work is already finished, and the debt is already paid, what does that free you from trying to earn? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and there is nothing here to earn.
