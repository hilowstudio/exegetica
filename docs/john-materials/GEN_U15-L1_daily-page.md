# Daily page, Unit 15 Lesson 1: The report, the delay, and Thomas (John 11:1-16)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus." (John 11:5, ESV)

Have I ever felt like God showed up late for me? Write the honest hurt, whatever it is. This chapter is for exactly that.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 11:1-16** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus' close friend Lazarus is dying, his sisters send for him, and Jesus waits.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Read verses 5 and 6 slowly. What does the text say about Jesus' love for this family? What does he do right after he hears Lazarus is sick?

______________________________________________________________

Look at verse 4 and verse 15. What does Jesus say this sickness is for?

______________________________________________________________

In verses 11 through 14, what word does Jesus first use for Lazarus's death? Then what does he say plainly in verse 14?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read verse 6 one more time and notice the small word "so." He loved them, so he stayed two more days. Sit with how those two things fit together. Hold the question for class.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words, why did Jesus wait two days on purpose? And write one thing today did not say about why God waits.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Where have you felt like God was late? After today, what is one thing you can say to yourself about God's love and God's timing? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
