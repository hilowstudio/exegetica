# Daily page, Unit 13 Lesson 3: I was blind, now I see (John 9:24-34)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "One thing I do know, that though I was blind, now I see." (John 9:25, ESV)

What is one true thing Jesus has done in my life or in someone I know? Write what you would want to be true, even if you are not sure yet.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 9:24-34** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the leaders call the man back a second time and pressure him hard to turn on Jesus.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 24 the leaders demand the man agree that Jesus is a sinner. In verse 25, how does he answer?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 30 through 33. The man is not a scholar, and he reasons circles around the experts. What does his confession climb to by verse 33?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 34, what do the leaders do to the man?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the man is bold in front of powerful people. Read the story again and ask where the boldness comes from: did he work up courage to earn Jesus, or did his courage grow out of what Jesus had already done for him? Hold the question of which came first.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what is the one thing the man is sure of, and what did it cost him to say it?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

You do not need all the answers to say what Jesus has done. Write your own "I was blind, now I see," one true thing you have seen him do. Honest is welcome. No one grades what you believe.
