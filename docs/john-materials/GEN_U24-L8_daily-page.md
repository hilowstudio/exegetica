## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

Have I ever gotten away with something someone else paid for? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 18:39-40** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Pilate offers to release one prisoner for the feast, and the crowd makes its choice. This closes the unit and opens the door to the cross.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 39, what does Pilate offer to do? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 40, who does the crowd choose instead of Jesus, and what does the verse tell you about the kind of man he was? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

Watch the swap. One man is guilty, and one man is innocent (Pilate just said so in verse 38). What happens to each of them? Write what the scene does with each one.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read Isaiah 53:6 on your own ("the Lord has laid on him the iniquity of us all"). Hold it next to what happens to Barabbas, and ask whose place is being taken.

## 4. The one thing from today

The guilty man walked out free because the innocent one took his place. Write the one thing you want to remember from that.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Barabbas walked free that morning because Jesus was condemned in his place, an innocent Savior taking the sentence a guilty man had earned. How do you respond to a Savior who would do that for you? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
