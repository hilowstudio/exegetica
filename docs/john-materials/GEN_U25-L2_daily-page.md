## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 19:4-7** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Pilate brings the bleeding Jesus out to the crowd, hoping they will settle for this instead of death.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 5, what does Pilate say as he brings Jesus out? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verses 4 and 6, how many times does Pilate say he finds no guilt in Jesus? Write what he says.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 7, what reason does the crowd finally give for why Jesus must die? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the crowd wants Jesus put to death for claiming to be the Son of God (verse 7). Hold the question of what it means that the one charge against him is something that is actually true.

## 4. The one thing from today

The judge himself kept saying "no guilt," and handed Jesus over anyway. Write the one thing you notice about that.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus was declared innocent and condemned anyway, in your place. What might his innocence make possible for you? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
