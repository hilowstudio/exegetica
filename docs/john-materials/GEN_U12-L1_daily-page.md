## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 8:12-20** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: last unit we learned that the Feast of Booths lit sixteen giant lamps in the temple courts, so bright the whole city glowed. When those lamps went dark after the feast, Jesus stood in that same court and said something about light.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 12, what does Jesus call himself, and what does he promise the person who follows him?

______________________________________________________________

Jesus does not say he brings a light or teaches about light. Write the exact words he uses about himself.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 13 the Pharisees say Jesus cannot testify about himself. In verses 17 and 18, who does Jesus name as his second witness?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read John 1:4-5 from the very start of the book, where the life was "the light of men" and the darkness could not overcome it. Hold the question of what it means that light does two things at once, guiding you out of the dark and shining on what you had hidden, and why both could be mercy.

## 4. The one thing from today

## 5. Respond
