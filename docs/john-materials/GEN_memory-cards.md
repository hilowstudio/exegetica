# Gospel of John: 16 memory verses

> Course: Sophomore Bible, Yorkville Christian School  
> Purpose: Learn 16 key verses from John by heart, one card per verse, in the ESV.  
> Count: 16 flashcards  
> Materials: Cardstock or index cards; scissors if you cut and fold  
> Learning objective(s):  
> Students will recall each of the 16 assigned verses in the Gospel of John (ESV) from its reference.  
> Scripture: John 20:31; 1:14; 1:12; 3:16; 4:24; 6:35; 8:12; 10:11; 11:25; 13:34-35; 14:6; 15:5; 16:33; 17:3; 19:30; 21:17 (ESV)

## How to use these cards

> **Read** the front (the reference) and try to say the verse out loud before you flip.  
> **Check** the back word for word. If you miss anything, say the whole verse again.  
> **Sort** the cards into two piles as you go: "got it" and "review." Run the "review" pile again the same day.  
> **Space it out.** Practice all 16 today, then again in 2 days, then again in 5 days. Short and often beats one long cram.

Each back gives only the verse's own words. Where the verse sits inside a longer quotation in John, the outer quotation marks are dropped so nothing dangles.

## Cut-and-fold layout note

- To make physical cards, copy each row onto its own index card: reference on the front, ESV words on the back.
- If you print, fold each card so the reference shows on one side and the verse on the other, then tape the top edge.
- Keep card 15 (John 19:30) and card 16 (John 21:17) even though the backs are short. Those few words carry the weight of the cross and Peter's restoration, so know exactly where they land.

## Track your seven titles of Jesus

Six of these cards are "I am" sayings where Jesus names who he is. Notice how they gather into one picture: bread that feeds you (6:35), light to walk by (8:12), a shepherd who dies for the sheep (10:11), resurrection and life over the grave (11:25), the only way to the Father (14:6), and the vine you stay joined to (15:5). Learn the words, and you're learning Christ.
