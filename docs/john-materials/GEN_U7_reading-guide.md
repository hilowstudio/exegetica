# Reading guide, Unit 7: Sign 2

> Passage: John 4:43-54
> Big question: when you ask Jesus for something and you can't watch him do it, will you take him at his word?
> Memory work: no new verse this unit. Keep your five review verses sharp.
> Read it in the ESV.

## How to use this guide

You don't turn this in. Keep it open next to your Reading Journal while we work the passage in class.

- **Read** the passage first, slowly, before you write anything.
- **Observe** means say what the text actually says. Who asks, what Jesus says, what changes.
- **Interpret** asks what it means and who Jesus is in it.
- **Respond** asks what it's asking of you.
- **Go deeper** prompts are optional. Take them if you want more.

This is the second miracle John records, and he calls it the "second sign" (4:54). Both of Jesus' first two signs happen at the same town, Cana. Watch how a worried dad's faith grows across the story.

## Lesson 1: the welcome that wanted a show (John 4:43-48)

**Read it.** John chapter 4, verses 43 through 48. Use the table of contents, turn to John, then find chapter 4 and the verse numbers.

Jesus goes back to Galilee, his home turf. The crowd is glad to see him, because they saw the amazing things he did in Jerusalem. A court official from Capernaum comes begging Jesus to come heal his dying son. Jesus answers the whole crowd first: "Unless you see signs and wonders you will not believe" (4:48).

- **Observe.** In verse 45, why exactly do the Galileans welcome Jesus? What had they seen?
- **Observe.** In verses 46 and 47, who comes to Jesus, and what does he ask for?
- **Interpret.** The crowd loves the miracles. Jesus wants more than a fan club. What's the difference between being impressed by Jesus and actually trusting him?
- **Respond.** Where do you look for proof before you'll trust God? Write one honest place.
- **Go deeper (optional).** Look back at John 2:23-25. Jesus wouldn't "entrust himself" to people who only believed because of signs. How does that help you read the welcome in 4:45?

## Lesson 2: take him at his word (John 4:49-54)

**Read it.** John chapter 4, verses 49 through 54. This is the healing.

The dad won't quit: "Sir, come down before my child dies." Jesus stays where he is and gives him a sentence: "Go; your son will live." The dad believes the word and starts the long walk home. On the way his servants meet him. The boy got better at the exact hour Jesus spoke. The dad believes again, and so does his whole household.

- **Observe.** In verse 50, what does Jesus tell the dad, and what does the dad do about it? (He hasn't seen anything yet.)
- **Observe.** In verses 52 and 53, what does the dad find out about the timing?
- **Interpret.** Verse 50 says the man "believed the word," and verse 53 says "he himself believed, and all his household." Why does John show us the dad believing twice?
- **Respond.** Jesus healed a boy fifteen miles away with one sentence. What would it look like for you to trust something Jesus said before you can see it work out?
- **Go deeper (optional).** Read Psalm 33:9: "For he spoke, and it came to be." The same word that made the world healed this boy. What does that tell you about Jesus (see John 1:1-3)?

## Key words to know

- **Sign.** John's word for Jesus' miracles. A sign points past itself to who Jesus is. This one shows him as the giver of life.
- **Official.** A man who worked in the king's court (Herod's). Someone with power and status, and here he's on his knees begging.
- **Cana.** The Galilee town where Jesus turned water into wine (John 2). Both of his first two signs happen here.
- **Believed the word.** The dad trusted what Jesus said before he saw any proof. That's the move the whole passage is about.

## Where the whole unit lands on Jesus

Jesus healed a dying boy he never saw, from a day's walk away, with one sentence. His word carries God's own power to give life. The dad had to trust that word the whole way home, and by the end his whole house believed. Jesus asks you to take him at his word too.
