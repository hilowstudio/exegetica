# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 5: Peace, and the wounds (John 20:19-20)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus came and stood among them and said to them, 'Peace be with you.'" (John 20:19, ESV)

What am I most afraid of right now, or where have I failed lately? Write it honestly. No one reads this but you.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:19-20** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: it's Sunday evening, and the disciples are hiding behind locked doors, afraid.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 19, where are the disciples, and why are the doors locked? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Still in verse 19, Jesus comes and stands among them. What is the very first thing he says? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 20, what does Jesus show them, and how do the disciples react? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* these are the men who ran away when Jesus was arrested. Hold the question of why his first word to them is "peace" and not blame, and why he still carries the wounds. Don't answer it yet; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

The first word to the friends who ran was "peace," not blame. Write the one thing you want to remember about how Jesus meets people who have failed.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus met his friends' fear and failure with "peace." Where do you need to hear him say "peace" to you, in a fear or a failure you're carrying? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and nothing here is earned.
