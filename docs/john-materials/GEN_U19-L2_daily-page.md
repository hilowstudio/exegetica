# Daily page, Unit 19 Lesson 2: The way, the truth, and the life (John 14:5-7)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus said to him, 'I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.'" (John 14:6, ESV)

Where in my life do I feel lost or unsure of the way? It is okay to write that you do not know.

*Go deeper (optional):* this is Jesus' sixth "I am" saying. You have already met the bread (6:35), the light (8:12), the door and the good shepherd (chapter 10), and the resurrection and the life (11:25). Hold the question of what John is building, and what the seventh will be.

## 4. The one thing from today

Who is Jesus talking to when he says verse 6, and what kind of mood is that person in?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"No one comes to the Father except through me." Flip it over: it means there is a way home, and it is open to anyone. Is that good news or bad news to you right now? Write honestly, either way. Belief is never scored.
