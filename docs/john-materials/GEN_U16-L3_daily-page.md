# Daily page, Unit 16 Lesson 3: The hour, the seed, and the cross (John 12:20-36)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "The hour has come for the Son of Man to be glorified." (John 12:23, ESV)

What are you afraid of that you might have to face anyway? Write the honest fear.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 12:20-36** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: some Greeks (non-Jews) come looking for Jesus, and it sets off the biggest moment yet.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 20 and 21, who comes looking for Jesus? In verse 23, what does Jesus say has finally come? (The whole book has said his hour "had not yet come.")

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 24. What has to happen to a grain of wheat before it bears fruit?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 27. What does Jesus say about his own soul just now? What does he decide to do (verse 27b and 28)?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 32. What does Jesus say will happen when he is "lifted up"? (Verse 33 tells you what "lifted up" means.)

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "lifted up" is a phrase Jesus keeps using. Turn back to John 3:14 and John 8:28 and read both. What one thing is he pointing at every single time? Hold it for class.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what is Jesus the grain of wheat saying about his own death?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus faced the cross afraid, and went anyway, for you. What does it change to know he understands fear and still chose you? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
