# Daily page, Unit 2 Lesson 4: Philip, Nathanael, and the ladder (John 1:43-51)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!" (John 1:49, ESV)

Nathanael was fully known by Jesus before he ever trusted him. Does the idea that Jesus already knows everything about you feel like a threat or a comfort? Be honest, either way.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 1:43-51** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the last man in the chain shows up already sneering, and by the end he is worshiping. This is where the unit peaks.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Philip tells Nathanael about Jesus. What does Nathanael say back (verse 46)? What two words does Philip use to answer him?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 47-48. What does Jesus say about Nathanael before Nathanael has said a word? What does Jesus say he saw?

______________________________________________________________

How does Nathanael answer in verse 49? Write the names he gives Jesus. Then list the other names Jesus has been given across this whole unit (look back through chapter 1).

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verse 51 Jesus talks about angels going up and down "on the Son of Man." Read Genesis 28:12, Jacob's dream of a ladder joining earth and heaven. What is Jesus putting himself in the place of? Hold it; we will not answer it for you.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what actually changed Nathanael from a scoffer into a worshiper.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Nathanael was fully known and still wanted. If that is true of you with Jesus, what would it change about coming to him? Write honestly.
