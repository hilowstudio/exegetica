# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 2: It is the Lord (John 21:4-8)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "That disciple whom Jesus loved therefore said to Peter, 'It is the Lord!'" (John 21:7, ESV)

Where have I worked hard on my own and come up empty? Write honestly.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:4-8** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: at dawn, a man they don't recognize calls out to the tired, empty-handed fishermen.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 5 and 6, what does the stranger on the shore tell them to do, and what happens to the net? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 7. Who figures out it is Jesus, and what does he say? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

Still in verse 7, what does Peter do the moment he hears it is the Lord? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the very first time Jesus called Peter (Luke 5) looked almost exactly like this, a night of nothing and a huge catch at Jesus' word. Hold the two scenes side by side, and sit with why Jesus might stage it again now. Leave it open.

## 4. The one thing from today

The second Peter heard "it is the Lord," he dove in, even after failing him. Write the one thing you notice about what Peter does with his shame.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Peter dove in to get to Jesus, even after failing him. Where are you hanging back from Jesus out of shame, and what would diving in look like? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
