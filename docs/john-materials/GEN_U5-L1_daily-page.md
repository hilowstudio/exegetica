# Daily page, Unit 5 Lesson 1: The friend of the bridegroom (John 3:22-30)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "He must increase, but I must decrease." (John 3:30, ESV)

Where do I most want to be noticed? If you are not sure God is there, write the question straight to him and keep going.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:22-30** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus and John the Baptist are both baptizing, the crowds are shifting to Jesus, and John's followers do not like it.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Look at verse 26. What are John's disciples worried about? Put your finger on the exact words.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 27-29. What title does John give himself, and what is that person's actual job at a wedding?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 30, what does John say has to happen to Jesus, and what has to happen to himself?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in verse 27 John says, "A person cannot receive even one thing unless it is given him from heaven." How does that one line take the pressure off John about the shrinking crowd? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice where John's joy comes from at the very moment his crowd shrinks.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

John's joy grew as he stepped back for Jesus. What is one place in your life where making room for Jesus would actually make you more glad, not less? Write honestly.
