# Reading guide, Unit 2: Witness and first disciples

> **Course:** Sophomore Bible, the Gospel of John: Yorkville Christian School  
> **Passage range:** John 1:19-28; 1:29-34; 1:35-42; 1:43-51 (four lessons)  
> **Memory verse:** John 1:12 (ESV)  
> **The one big question this unit asks:** When the first people meet Jesus, who do they say he is, and what do they do about it?  
> **Learning objectives:** Students will read John 1:19-51 with understanding and personal response. Students will know and review the memory verse, John 1:12.  
> **Scripture:** Quoted in the ESV.

---

## How to use this guide

- Keep this guide next to your **Reading Journal**. This page holds the questions. Your journal is where you write.
- You don't finish this at home. We work through it **in class**, together, across four lessons.
- **Read** each passage first, out loud or on your own, before you answer anything.
- For each lesson you'll find three quick prompts: **Observe** (what does the text actually say?), **Interpret** (what does it mean, and who is Jesus here?), and **Respond** (what does it ask of you?).
- The **Go deeper** prompts are optional. They're for anyone who wants to chase a thread further. Skip them or dig in.
- New to the Bible? You'll see a **Find it** note where a passage is tricky to locate. Use it. Nobody here is expected to already know their way around.

> **What a good observation looks like:** it points to real words on the page. "John says four times who he is *not*" is an observation. "John was humble" is a conclusion. Start with what the text says, then build from there.

This is the day Jesus' public life begins, and before he does a single miracle, his death is already announced over him.

## Key words to know

- **Witness / testimony:** someone saying what they saw so others can know it's true. John's Gospel uses this word constantly. The whole unit is a chain of witnesses pointing to Jesus.
- **The Lamb of God:** Jesus, pictured as the sacrifice who takes away sin. In the Old Testament, lambs were killed on an altar to cover sin. John says Jesus is *the* Lamb.
- **Messiah / Christ:** the same title in two languages (Hebrew and Greek). It means God's promised rescuer and King, the one Israel had waited centuries for.
- **Rabbi:** "Teacher." It's the first thing the disciples call Jesus, before they grow into calling him Lord.
- **Son of Man:** Jesus' favorite title for himself. It reaches back to a heavenly figure in the book of Daniel who is given a kingdom that never ends.
- **Nazareth / Bethsaida / Bethany across the Jordan:** small towns in the region where this happens. Nazareth had a bad reputation, which is why Nathanael scoffs at it.

## Memory verse (ESV)

> **John 1:12**, "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God."

**Review note:** This verse comes from earlier in chapter 1, and we plant it here on purpose, because Unit 2 is where "receiving him" stops being an idea and becomes a scene you can watch. Learn it in three moves:

- **"received him"**: what the disciples do in this chapter: they come, they stay, they follow.
- **"believed in his name"**: the confession that grows across the unit, from "Rabbi" all the way to "Son of God."
- **"he gave the right to become children of God"**: the gift on the far side of receiving. You don't earn your way into God's family. He gives it.

You'll be asked to recite this verse. The meaning we'll talk through in class; you won't be graded on how you feel about it.

## Where the whole unit lands

Every voice in these four scenes points the same direction, away from the speaker and toward Jesus. John the Baptist points. Andrew brings Peter. Philip brings Nathanael. The people who came and saw ended up following, and the One they found is the Lamb who carries sin away and the ladder that joins heaven and earth. That's the caption of John 1:12 over four real men in one week: they received him, and he made them children of God.
