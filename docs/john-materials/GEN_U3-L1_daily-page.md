# Daily page, Unit 3 Lesson 1: The wedding at Cana, the first sign (John 2:1-11)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "This, the first of his signs, Jesus did at Cana in Galilee, and manifested his glory." (John 2:11, ESV)

Where do you most want to see that Jesus is more than you assumed? Honest curiosity is welcome.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 2:1-11** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: a wedding in Cana. The wine runs out, which is a real embarrassment for the host, and Jesus does the first of his signs.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Walk the scene. Where is Jesus, what runs out, and what does he do about it? Roughly how much wine, and what does the master of the feast say about it (verse 10)?

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Count the stone jars in verse 6 and read what they were for. How much did each one hold?

______________________________________________________________

Verse 11 does not call this a "miracle" or a "wonder." What word does it use? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verse 4 Jesus says, "My hour has not yet come." Later he says, "The hour has come for the Son of Man to be glorified" (John 12:23), and in John that "hour" is the cross. Trace the word "hour" across the book if you want a thread to follow. Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. A sign is a window. Notice what this one lets you see about who Jesus is.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

A sign is a window onto who Jesus is. Looking through this one, what is one thing you would say Jesus is like? Write honestly.
