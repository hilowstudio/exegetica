# Daily page, Unit 19 Lesson 5: Another Helper (John 14:15-17)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Helper, to be with you forever." (John 14:16, ESV)

Where do I try hardest to be good, and how is that going on my own strength? Be honest about the gap between what you want to do and what you do.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 14:15-17** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus ties love and obedience together, then promises the One who makes obedience possible.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 15, what does Jesus say love for him will look like? Which comes first in the sentence, the loving or the keeping?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 16, what does Jesus promise to ask the Father for, and how long will this Helper stay? Look at the word "another."

______________________________________________________________

In verse 17, what does Jesus call the Helper, and what does he say is true of him?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "another Helper" means another of the same kind as Jesus. From verse 17, hold the question of who the Spirit is, a Person or a force.

## 4. The one thing from today

What is the order in verse 15, and who does Jesus promise in verse 16?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

What would change if you stopped trying to earn God's love and started obeying because you already have it? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
