# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 2: The folded grave clothes (John 20:3-10)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "and the face cloth, which had been on Jesus' head, not lying with the linen cloths but folded up in a place by itself." (John 20:7, ESV)

What kind of evidence would it take for me to believe something huge? Write honestly.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:3-10** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Mary's report sends Peter and John running to the tomb to see for themselves.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 3 and 4, what do Peter and John do, and who reaches the tomb first? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 6 and 7 slowly. What exactly does Peter see inside, and what is different about the face cloth? Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

In verse 8, what happens to the other disciple when he goes in? Write his words from the verse.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 9 says they "did not yet understand the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead." They believed before they fully understood. Hold the question of what an empty tomb does to how they will read their Old Testament now, and leave it open.

## 4. The one thing from today

"He saw and believed." John looked at an empty, orderly tomb and trusted before he had it all figured out. Write the one thing that stays with you about how faith came to him.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

John had enough to trust Jesus before every question was answered. Where do you have enough to trust him, even with questions you're still carrying? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and questions are welcome here.
