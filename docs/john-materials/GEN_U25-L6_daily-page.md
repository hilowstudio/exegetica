## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 19 and 20, what does the sign over the cross say, and in how many languages is it written? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 21, what do the chief priests want the sign changed to say? Write it.

In verse 22, how does Pilate answer them? Write his words.

*Go deeper (optional):* the three languages were the languages of religion, empire, and everyday culture. Hold the question of who was meant to be able to read this sign, and why that matters.

## 4. The one thing from today

Pilate meant the sign as a jab at the leaders, and it announced the truth about Jesus to the whole world. Write the one thing you notice about how God works, even through people who are not trying to serve him.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The sign called Jesus King, and it was true. Is he actually King of your life, or just a claim you would make on paper? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
