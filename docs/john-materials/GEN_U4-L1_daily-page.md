# Daily page, Unit 4 Lesson 1: The night visit and the new birth (John 3:1-8)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "You must be born again." (John 3:7, ESV)

Is there anything about yourself you have tried and failed to change? What would it be like if that change were God's job, not yours? Honest is welcome.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:1-8** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: a religious insider comes to Jesus at night, and Jesus tells him the one thing all his religion cannot give him. Remember 2:25, "he knew what was in man." Here is the man.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Who is Nicodemus (verse 1)? What does he call Jesus in verse 2, and how sure does he sound? Then what does Jesus tell him in verse 3 he must have to see the kingdom of God?

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Jesus keeps saying "born again" and "born of the Spirit." Underline every time birth comes up in these eight verses. How many times?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 8, "The wind blows where it wishes." Write what Jesus says you do and do not control about the wind.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read John 1:13, which describes people "born, not of blood nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God." How does that verse back up what Jesus tells Nicodemus? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice who does the new birth, and who cannot.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus tells a good religious man that his religion cannot save him. He needs God to give him new life. Is that good news or unsettling news to you right now? Write honestly.
