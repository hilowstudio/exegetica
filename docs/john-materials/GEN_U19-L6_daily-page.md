# Daily page, Unit 19 Lesson 6: I will not leave you as orphans (John 14:18-24)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I will not leave you as orphans; I will come to you." (John 14:18, ESV)

When do I feel most alone? Name it plainly, even if you are not sure anyone is listening.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 14:18-24** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus is about to leave, and he promises the opposite of abandonment.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 18, what does Jesus promise he will not do, and what does he promise he will do?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 19, why does Jesus say the disciples will live? Write the reason.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 23, what do the Father and the Son do for the one who loves Jesus and keeps his word?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in verse 2 Jesus goes to prepare a place for you in the Father's house; in verse 23 he and the Father come to make their home in you. Hold the question of how both can be true at once.

## 4. The one thing from today

According to verse 23, what do the Father and Son do for the one who loves Jesus and keeps his word?

## 5. Respond

Jesus promises never to leave his people as orphans. What would it change about your loneliest moments to believe that? Write honestly.
