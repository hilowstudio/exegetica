## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 7:37-44** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: it is the last and biggest day of the feast. All week the priests poured out water at the temple each morning, remembering how God gave Israel water in the desert. On this day the pouring has stopped, and into the silence Jesus stands up and shouts.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 37, what does Jesus shout to the crowd? What is the invitation?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 39. What does John tell us the "rivers of living water" actually are?

Look at verse 38. What word does Jesus use for how a person "drinks"?

*Go deeper (optional):* back in John 4, Jesus offered a Samaritan woman "living water" at a well. Read John 4:13-14 next to John 7:37-39. Hold the question of what the two scenes together show about the thirst Jesus means and how it gets filled.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what is the living water, and how does a person get it?
