# Daily page, Unit 9 Lesson 5: No one comes unless the Father draws him (John 6:41-51)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him." (John 6:44, ESV)

If God came looking for me first, how does that change how I see myself? You can write your honest question about whether God wants you.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:41-51** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the crowd starts grumbling that they know Jesus' family, and he tells them their coming to him is the Father's gift, then points ahead to the cross.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 41 and 42, what is the crowd's complaint, and what do they think they already know about Jesus?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 44 next to verse 37. Write what verse 44 says has to happen before anyone comes, and what verse 37 promises the one who does come.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 51, Jesus says the bread he will give "for the life of the world" is his flesh. Write what he says he will give.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read verse 44 beside the wind-Spirit of new birth in John 3:8. Hold the question of why it is comforting that your coming to Jesus started with God coming after you first.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what does verse 44 say has to happen before anyone comes to Jesus, and how could that be good news?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says your coming to him starts with God drawing you first. What changes if your faith rests on God's grip and not on yours? If you are not sure you have been drawn, you can write the honest prayer "God, draw me."
