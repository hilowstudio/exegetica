# Daily page, Unit 9 Lesson 1: The feeding of the five thousand (John 6:1-15)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Where are we to buy bread, so that these people may eat?" (John 6:5, ESV)

What am I hungry for that food does not fix? Write it honestly, whatever it is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:1-15** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: a huge crowd follows Jesus up a mountain near Passover, and there is no food.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 9, what does the boy bring? In verse 13, what is left over after everyone has eaten?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 15. What does the crowd try to do to Jesus, and what does Jesus do in response?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 4 says the Passover was near, the feast when Israel remembered God rescuing them from Egypt and feeding them in the desert. Read Exodus 16:4. Hold the question of why John would point to Passover right before Jesus feeds a crowd in a wilderness.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what did the crowd want from Jesus, and what did he do about it?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The crowd wanted Jesus for what he could give. Where do you do that, and what would it look like to want him for himself? Honest is welcome, and no one grades what you believe.
