## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 12:1-19** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: six days before Passover, Jesus is at a dinner in Bethany with Mary, Martha, and the raised Lazarus, and then he rides into Jerusalem.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 3, what does Mary do? In verse 5, how much is the perfume worth?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 5 Judas objects that the money should have gone to the poor. In verse 6, what does John tell us was really going on with Judas?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 7. What does Jesus say Mary's gift was for?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 12 through 15. The crowd welcomes Jesus as King, waving palm branches. What animal does Jesus ride in on?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* a king on a warhorse rides out to fight. A king on a donkey comes another way. Turn to Zechariah 9:9, written centuries before, and read how the promised king comes to his people. Hold the question of what kind of King Jesus is showing himself to be.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what kind of King did the crowd want, and what kind did Jesus show himself to be?

## 5. Respond

Mary treated Jesus like he was worth everything. Do you want a Jesus who fixes your problems, or the real King who died for you? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
