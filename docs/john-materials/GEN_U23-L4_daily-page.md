# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 4: In the world, not of it (John 17:14-16)

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "I do not ask that you take them out of the world, but that you keep them from the evil one." (John 17:15, ESV)

Where do I feel torn between fitting in and following Jesus? Write one honest line.
