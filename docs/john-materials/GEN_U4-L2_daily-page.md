# Daily page, Unit 4 Lesson 2: The snake on the pole (John 3:9-15)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "So must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in him may have eternal life." (John 3:14-15, ESV)

Is there something in you that you cannot fix on your own? Sit with that honestly for a minute.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:9-15** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Nicodemus asks, "How can this be?" and Jesus answers with an old, strange story about a snake on a pole.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 9 Nicodemus asks, "How can these things be?" The teacher of Israel is stuck. What does that tell you about how well the smartest religious teacher is following along?

______________________________________________________________

Read Numbers 21:4-9, the story Jesus points back to. What was happening to Israel, and what was the cure? What exactly did a dying person have to do?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 14 Jesus says, "as Moses lifted up the serpent... so must the Son of Man be lifted up." Write what Jesus says will happen to him.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "lifted up" comes back in John 12:32, "And I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to myself." The phrase carries both "nailed up on the cross" and "raised up in glory." How can one event hold both meanings? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what the dying people had to do to live, and what that says about you.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Looking at the snake took nothing impressive. It was simply trusting God's cure. What would it mean for you to look to Jesus rather than fix yourself first? Write honestly.
