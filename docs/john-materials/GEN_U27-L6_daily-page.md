# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 6: What is that to you (John 21:20-25)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus said to him, 'If it is my will that he remain until I come, what is that to you? You follow me!'" (John 21:22, ESV)

Where do I compare my life to someone else's instead of following Jesus? Write honestly.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:20-25** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: right after being told to follow, Peter looks over at another disciple and asks about him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 21, Peter sees the disciple John and asks Jesus a question. What is it? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 22. How does Jesus answer Peter's "what about him?" Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 24 and 25. What does John say about himself in verse 24, and what does he say about Jesus in verse 25? Write both.

*Go deeper (optional):* look back over the whole year, from "the Word became flesh" (1:14) to why John wrote it all (20:31). Hold the question of why John ends the whole story here, on a beach, with "do you love me" and "follow me." Sit with it; don't rush to answer.

## 4. The one thing from today

"What is that to you? You follow me." Write the one thing you want to carry out of the whole book about Jesus.

## 5. Respond

We've walked all the way through John, from "the Word became flesh" to "do you love me." What is one thing about Jesus you'll carry with you? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and there is nothing here to earn.
