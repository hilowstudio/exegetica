# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 6: That they may all be one (John 17:20-21)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "That they may all be one... so that the world may believe that you have sent me." (John 17:21, ESV)

Have I ever felt part of something bigger than myself? What was it like? Write it honestly.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:20-21** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: listening in on the Son praying, and now the prayer widens. Jesus lifts his eyes past the eleven men in the room and prays for everyone who will ever believe in him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 20, who does Jesus say he is praying for, besides the disciples in the room? Write his exact words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 21, what does Jesus ask for all these believers? What is the model, the "just as"?

______________________________________________________________

Still in verse 21, why does Jesus want them to be one? Write the goal at the end of the verse.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 20 says "those who will believe in me through their word." The message reached you through 2,000 years of people passing it on. Hold the question of who, sitting in that room the night before he died, Jesus was praying for.

## 4. The one thing from today

The night before he died, Jesus was praying for everyone who would ever believe in him. After today's talk, write what it does to you to know you were in that prayer.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus was praying for you the night before he died (verse 20). Sit with that, then write: what does it change to know you were on his mind? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
