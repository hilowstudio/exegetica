## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 9:13-23** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the leaders start interrogating the healed man, and then they call in his parents.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 17, the leaders ask the man what he thinks of Jesus. What does he say? (Compare it to verse 11, where he first called him "the man called Jesus.")

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 18 through 23. What two things do the parents admit, and what question do they refuse to answer?

______________________________________________________________

In verses 22 and 23, what reason does the passage give for the parents' silence?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* think back to the man Jesus healed at the pool in John 5, who was healed and then reported Jesus to the authorities. This man is healed and gets bolder about Jesus every time he is pushed. Hold the question of why two people can both get a miracle and respond so differently.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, why would the parents not answer the question about Jesus?
