At this point in history, the Romans appointed the high priest over the nation of Israel, whoever was politically convenient for them. So the Jews would have been confused. Is Caiaphas, the acting high priest, our high priest, or is Annas? They've got Jesus bound, and the question is, whom do we bring him to? They bring him first to Annas. As we build the backstory, so that we can get to the heart of what's going on, I want you to see that this isn't a small point.

I want you to see the great irony, even hypocrisy, of the stage being set here. We're going to put on trial the King of the universe, the great High Priest, and we don't even know who heads our own court to bring him to. Let's go to Annas first. He's the senior high priest. He was there first.
