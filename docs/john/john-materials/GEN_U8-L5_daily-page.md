## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 5:31-47** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus lays out his witnesses, then names the surprising problem: the Bible experts missed the whole point of the Book.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 33-39, count Jesus' witnesses and name all four.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 39 and 40. Jesus says these men "search the Scriptures," then he says what they refuse to do. Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 46 Jesus says, "If you believed Moses, you would believe me; for he wrote of me." Write it. Who does Jesus say Moses wrote about?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* read Luke 24:27, where after he rose, Jesus walked two disciples through "all the Scriptures" about himself. Put John 5:46 next to Luke 24:27. What does Jesus say is the point of the whole Old Testament? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what these Bible experts had right, and what they missed.

## 5. Respond
