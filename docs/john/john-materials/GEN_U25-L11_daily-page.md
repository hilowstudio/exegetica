## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 19:38-42** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: after Jesus dies, two men who had followed him in secret come forward to bury him. This closes the unit, with Easter three days away.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 38 and 39, who are the two men who come to bury Jesus, and what does John tell us about each of them? Write it.

*Go deeper (optional):* Nicodemus first came to Jesus at night, back in John 3, afraid to be seen. Now he steps into the open in daylight. Hold the question of what changed him, and what the cross did to draw these secret disciples out.

## 4. The one thing from today

Everyone who came to that tomb came to bury a dead man, wrapping a real body and sealing a real grave. Write the one thing you notice about why that matters for what happens next.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Two men who hid their faith finally came into the open at the hardest moment. Where are you hiding that you follow Jesus? What would stepping into the open look like for you? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
