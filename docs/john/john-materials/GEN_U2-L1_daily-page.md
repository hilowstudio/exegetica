# Daily page, Unit 2 Lesson 1: The witness who points away (John 1:19-28)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "among you stands one you do not know" (John 1:26, ESV)

Where might God be present in your life in a way you have not noticed? It is okay to write that you are not sure he is there.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 1:19-28** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: officials come out from Jerusalem to interrogate John the Baptist, and he keeps refusing to be the story.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

The priests ask, "Who are you?" List the three things John says he is not (verses 20-21). Then write the one thing he says he is (verse 23).

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 26-27. In John's own words, what does he say about the one the crowd is missing? Quote the phrase.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* John calls himself "the voice of one crying out in the wilderness" (verse 23), a line he borrows from Isaiah. Why would he reach for "a voice" rather than a title of his own? Hold the question.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice where John aims every bit of the attention.

## 5. Respond

The experts missed the Messiah standing right in front of them. What might you be missing about Jesus because he has become too familiar, or because you have not looked? Write honestly.
