# Daily page, Unit 19 Lesson 7: Peace I leave with you (John 14:25-27)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you." (John 14:27, ESV)

Where do I go looking for peace, and does it last? Be honest about what does not actually settle you.
