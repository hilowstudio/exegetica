> "You would have no authority over me... unless it had been given you from above." (John 19:11, ESV)

Where do I feel powerless right now? Where do I think I am more in control than I am? Write one line.

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus is bound and beaten, and he is calmer than the governor who holds the gavel. Hold the question of who is really in control of this scene.

## 4. The one thing from today

"Greater sin" means guilt is real and comes in degrees, and no one in this room is off the hook. Write the one honest thing this makes you think about where you excuse yourself.

## 5. Respond

Every power over you is borrowed from God, who rules it all. Where do you need that to be true this week? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
