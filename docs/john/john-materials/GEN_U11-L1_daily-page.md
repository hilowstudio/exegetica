## 2. Hear the Word

Open your own Bible to John 8 and look just above verse 1.

> You will see a note like this: "The earliest manuscripts do not include 7:53-8:11," and the story sits inside brackets. That note is not a mistake, and no one is hiding anything. It is there because the people who made your Bible are being honest with you. The oldest handwritten copies of John do not carry this story, so they flag it right on the page. The church has treasured this account for a very long time as true to Jesus, and nothing else in the Christian faith rests on it. We read it with open eyes.

**Read: John 7:53-8:11** (slowly).

Where we are: Jesus is teaching in the temple early in the morning when religious leaders drag in a woman and try to trap him over whether she should be stoned.

*Go deeper (optional):* this story is older than the copies of John that carry it. A Christian writer named Papias knew a version of it around the year 130, and in some old copies it appears in a different place entirely. Read John 7:52 straight on into John 8:12 and notice how cleanly they connect. Hold the question of how the church can treasure this account as true to Jesus while honestly marking that it may not have been part of what John first wrote.
