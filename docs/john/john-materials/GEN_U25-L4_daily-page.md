## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "We have no king but Caesar." (John 19:15, ESV)

If someone watched where my time and love actually go, who would they say is the king of my life? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

Where we are: the leaders play their last card to force Pilate's hand, and it works.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 12, what threat do the leaders use to pressure Pilate? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 14, what does Pilate say as he presents Jesus one last time? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 15, Pilate asks, "Shall I crucify your King?" What do the chief priests answer? Write their words.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* it is Passover, the feast that celebrates God as Israel's true King. Hold the question of what it means that these leaders say "we have no king but Caesar" at that exact feast.

## 4. The one thing from today

"We have no king but Caesar" is what it sounds like to claim God while your real loyalty is somewhere else. Write the one honest thing this makes you check in yourself.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Who or what is the actual king of your life right now, if you watch where your time and love go? Name it honestly, and you can ask Jesus to be King instead. Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
