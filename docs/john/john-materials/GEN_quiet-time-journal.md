# Quiet-Time Journal

> This journal is private. It is between you and God. No one grades it, and your teacher does not read it. Make your own copy (File, then Make a copy) and keep it in your own Drive. Write honestly. If you are not sure God is there, you can say that here too.

Each class opens with a few quiet minutes. This is the place for that time: prayer, gratitude, what you are carrying, honest questions.
