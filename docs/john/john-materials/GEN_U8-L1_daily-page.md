# Daily page, Unit 8 Lesson 1: The man who could not get to the water (John 5:1-9)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Do you want to be healed?" (John 5:6, ESV)

What have I been waiting a long time for? Write the wait honestly, even the parts you are tired of.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 5:1-9** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: a crowd of sick people wait by a pool in Jerusalem, and one man has been there a very long time.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Look at verse 5. How long has this man been an invalid? Put a number on it. Then verse 7: what does he say is stopping him from getting to the water?

______________________________________________________________

Look at what the man does and does not do in this scene. Does he ask Jesus to heal him? Does he even know who Jesus is (see verse 13)?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 9, what does the man do the moment Jesus speaks, and what one detail does John add at the very end of the verse?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* your Bible probably jumps from verse 3 to verse 5, with verse 4 in a footnote. The oldest copies of John do not include verse 4 (the angel stirring the water). Why is it good news that our Bibles are honest about that? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what the man brought to Jesus, and what Jesus did anyway.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The man brought nothing and Jesus healed him anyway. Where do you feel like you have to earn help before you deserve it? What would it mean that Jesus reaches first? Write honestly.
