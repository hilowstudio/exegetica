## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 18:28-32** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the trial moves from the high priest to Pilate, the Roman governor, because only Rome could order an execution.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 28, why won't the leaders go inside Pilate's headquarters? Write the reason the verse gives.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 28, what are these same leaders arranging at the very moment they are so careful to stay "clean"? Write what is happening in the scene.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 31, what do the leaders say they are not allowed to do? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 32, John says this happened "to fulfill" a word of Jesus about the kind of death he would die. Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the penalty in that court was stoning; the Roman way was crucifixion, being lifted up. Look back at John 12:32-33. Hold the question of how the leaders' hands being tied made sure Jesus died the exact death he foretold.

## 4. The one thing from today

The leaders guarded the outside carefully and missed what their own hearts were doing. Write the one honest thing this makes you want to check in yourself.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Outward religion is easy to measure, and a heart is easy to hide, even from yourself. Is your faith a checklist you keep, or a heart open to God? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
