# Daily page, Unit 8 Lesson 4: Life now, and the resurrection to come (John 5:24-30)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "has passed from death to life." (John 5:24, ESV)

What would it feel like to know I am safe on the last day? Write what you would want to be true about what happens after we die.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 5:24-30** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus just claimed the Father's authority, and now he says what he does with it: he gives life and raises the dead.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 24, what does the person who hears Jesus' word and believes have? What do they not come into? Notice the tense of "has passed," is it later or now?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 25 and verse 28 side by side. Verse 25 says the hour "is now here." Verse 28 says the hour "is coming." Write what each one is about.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 26, where does the Son's power to give life come from? Write the verse's own words.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 29 mentions "the resurrection of judgment," which could scare a person. Look back at verse 24. What does Jesus say about judgment for the one who hears and believes? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what is already settled for the one who hears and believes.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says a believer has already "passed from death to life." If that were settled and true for you, what would you stop being afraid of? Write honestly.
