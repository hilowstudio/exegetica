# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 6: Sent, and given the Spirit (John 20:21-23)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus said to them again, 'Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you.'" (John 20:21, ESV)

Do I think of following Jesus as being sent to do something? What would that mean for me? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:21-23** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: after "peace," Jesus turns the frightened disciples into a sent people.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 21, what does Jesus say he is doing to the disciples, and what is the comparison? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 22, what does Jesus do to them, and what does he say? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 23, what does Jesus put in their hands to announce? Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the word "breathed" in verse 22 shows up in one other famous place, Genesis 2:7, where God breathed life into the first human. Hold the two side by side, and sit with what the risen Jesus is doing to his church. Leave it open.

## 4. The one thing from today

Sent, given the Spirit, handed the message of forgiveness. Write the one thing that stands out about the job Jesus gives ordinary people.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus sends ordinary, forgiven people to tell others their sins can be forgiven in him. Who in your life needs to hear that, and what holds you back from telling them? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
