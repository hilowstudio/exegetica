# Daily page, Unit 4 Lesson 3: God so loved the world (John 3:16-17)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." (John 3:16, ESV)

You have probably heard John 3:16 a hundred times. Read it slowly. What is one word in it you have never really stopped on?

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:16-17** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: this comes right after the snake on the pole, so "gave his only Son" means the Son lifted up on the cross.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Verse 16 says "God so loved the world." Look back at verse 14. What did that love actually do? What did God "give"?

______________________________________________________________

Circle the one verb in verse 16 that says what God actually did. It is one word, and it costs the Giver everything.

______________________________________________________________

Look at verse 17. What does it say God sent the Son to do, and what does it say he did not send the Son to do?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus told Nicodemus his people should already have known about the new birth. Read Ezekiel 36:25-27, written centuries earlier: God promises to "sprinkle clean water on you" and "put my Spirit within you." How is John 3 the arrival of that old promise? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words, say verse 16 back as one plain sentence, using all three: the way God loved, the world he loved, and what it cost him.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"God so loved the world." If that love was a Father handing over a Son for people who had turned away, what does it change to know you are in that "world"? Write honestly.
