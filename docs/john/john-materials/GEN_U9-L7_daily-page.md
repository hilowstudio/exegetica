> "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life." (John 6:68, ESV)

What makes following Jesus hard for me right now? You can write the honest reason you would want to walk away, if you have one.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:60-71** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the bread teaching was too hard for the crowd, and now we see who stays.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 60, what do many of the disciples say about Jesus' teaching? In verse 66, what do they do?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 63. Write what Jesus says about "the flesh" and about "the words" he has spoken.

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 67 through 69. Jesus asks the twelve if they want to leave too. Write Peter's answer.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verse 65 Jesus says again that no one comes unless it is granted him by the Father. Read that beside Peter staying while the crowd leaves. Hold the question of what kept Peter in.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, when the crowd left, why did Peter stay?

## 5. Respond

Following Jesus got hard and the crowd thinned out. When has following Jesus been hard for you, and what kept you, or would keep you, in? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
