# Daily page, Unit 19 Lesson 1: Let not your hearts be troubled (John 14:1-4)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Let not your hearts be troubled. Believe in God; believe also in me." (John 14:1, ESV)

What is troubling my heart right now? You do not have to fix it. Just name it honestly.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 14:1-4** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: it is the night before the cross, the disciples are shaken by talk of betrayal and death, and Jesus turns to comfort them.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 1, what does Jesus tell them to do with their troubled hearts, and what does he tell them to do instead?

______________________________________________________________

In verses 2 and 3, list each thing Jesus promises (where he is going, what he will prepare, what he will come back to do).

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus says "I will come again." Hold the question of when, and read to the end of the chapter to see the same comfort return at verse 27.

## 4. The one thing from today

According to verse 1, where does Jesus point their troubled hearts?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

When you are anxious, what do you usually look at? What would it change to look at Jesus instead? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
