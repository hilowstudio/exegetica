# Study guide: Semester 2 Final, The Book of Glory (John 13-21)

> **Use this on the review day (May 24) and to study at home.** It tells you exactly what's on the final, so there are no surprises.
> The final is May 25-28 and is worth 15% of your semester grade. It is **cumulative**: all sixteen memory anchors are in play. All Scripture ESV.

## What the final covers

The second half of John, chapters 13 to 21, the Book of Glory. This is the half where the same Jesus the signs revealed now gives himself to his friends and then to death: the upper room, the cross, the empty tomb, and the beach. The gravity of the whole half is the cross, and its center is "It is finished" (19:30). Keep that in front of you.

Because it's the last exam of the year, memory is cumulative: all sixteen anchors. And the big architecture to know is the deity frame: the book opens "the Word was God" (1:1) and closes with Thomas saying "My Lord and my God" (20:28).

## The five parts of the exam

1. **Memory**: write the seven Book-of-Glory anchors in full, plus any four of the nine earlier ones.
2. **The Farewell Discourse**: what Jesus gives in each chapter (13-17) and its anchor.
3. **The Passion, resurrection, and epilogue**: key moments in 18-21 and what they mean.
4. **Short answer**: the christotelic heart, including the deity inclusio.
5. **Essay**: one christotelic essay (you choose from three). Graded on understanding, not belief.

## Part 1: memory

**Write these seven in full:**
1. **13:34-35**: a new commandment, love as I have loved you.
2. **14:6**: I am the way, the truth, the life.
3. **15:5**: I am the vine; apart from me you can do nothing.
4. **16:33**: I have overcome the world.
5. **17:3**: this is eternal life, that they know you.
6. **19:30**: It is finished.
7. **21:17**: Feed my sheep.

**Then choose any four of the nine earlier anchors** (20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16, 4:24, 6:35, 8:12, 10:11, 11:25) and write them from memory. Keep all sixteen sharp.

## Part 2: the Farewell Discourse (what Jesus gives in each chapter)

The whole discourse is Jesus giving his frightened friends himself.

| Chapter | What Jesus gives | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| John 13, the towel and the basin | himself as the servant-King; a new command to love as he loved | 13:34-35 |
| John 14, the way to the Father | himself as the way home; the promise of the Helper | 14:6 |
| John 15, the vine | himself as the vine they live from | 15:5 |
| John 16, sorrow into joy | the Spirit, and his finished victory | 16:33 |
| John 17, the prayer | himself as the praying High Priest | 17:3 |

## Part 3: the Passion, resurrection, and epilogue (key moments)

- **The garden (18:5-6):** "I am he," and the soldiers fall back. He goes willingly, in control (10:18).
- **The sign over the cross (19:19-22):** "the King of the Jews," in three languages, unchanged. The King reigns from the cross.
- **No bone broken (19:33, 19:36):** he dies as the true Passover Lamb (Exodus 12:46; 1:29).
- **"It is finished" (19:30):** the Greek *tetelestai*, "paid in full." The work of salvation is complete, and he yields his spirit by his own choice.
- **The folded grave clothes (20:6-7):** a sign of resurrection, not robbery. Unlike Lazarus (who rose to die again), Jesus rose to die no more.
- **Thomas (20:28):** "My Lord and my God," closing the frame John opened at 1:1.
- **Peter restored (21:15-17):** three questions for three denials; "feed my sheep"; "follow me" (21:19).

## Part 4: the christotelic heart (be ready to answer in a sentence or two)

- **"Abide" (ch 15):** stay joined to Christ; fruit, love, and prayer flow from the union.
- **14:6 as hard line and comfort:** one true way home means there *is* a way home, spoken to steady a scared friend.
- **Eternal life (17:3):** knowing the Father and the Son; a relationship that starts now.
- **The eyewitness (19:35):** John stakes his true testimony on the real death of Jesus.
- **The sixteen anchors as one portrait:** not sixteen sayings, one Christ seen sixteen times, from "the Word became flesh" (1:14) to "feed my sheep" (21:17).

## Part 5: the essay (prepare all three, you'll pick one)

**E1.** Trace "the hour" and glory across the Book of Glory; show how John presents the cross as Jesus' glorification (use three moments from 13-19).
**E2.** What does "It is finished" mean, and how do the resurrection and Thomas's confession confirm it?
**E3.** The Farewell Discourse is Jesus giving his friends himself. Choose three chapters and explain what he gives in each.

You're graded on whether you *see* what John is showing, never on whether you personally believe it.

## How to study this week

- **Say the sixteen verses out loud,** then write the seven new ones cold and four of the earlier nine.
- **Make a Farewell chart:** chapter, what Jesus gives, anchor. Cover a column and quiz yourself.
- **Practice "It is finished."** If you can explain *tetelestai* and connect it to the empty tomb and Thomas, you can write E2.
- **Trace the deity frame:** 1:1 to 20:28. The book opens and closes on Jesus being God.
- **Keep asking "so who is Jesus here?"** at every scene from the towel to the beach.
