# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 4: My Father and your Father (John 20:17-18)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God." (John 20:17, ESV)

When I think of God as a Father, what comes to mind? Write the honest picture, whatever it is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:17-18** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Mary has just recognized the risen Jesus, and now he gives her something to carry.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 17, what does Jesus tell Mary not to do, and what reason does he give? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

Still in verse 17, what does Jesus call the disciples, and whose Father and whose God does he now say the Father is? Write the exact words.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

In verse 18, what does Mary go and announce to the disciples? Write her sentence.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* way back in John 1:12, John said Jesus gives "the right to become children of God." Hold that promise next to verse 17, where the risen Jesus calls them his brothers and shares his Father. Notice the link, and leave the question open.

## 4. The one thing from today

Because Jesus rose, he calls the disciples "my brothers" and shares his Father with them. Write the one thing that changes if his Father is now your Father.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Because Jesus rose, his Father is now your Father, not a distant judge. If that is true of you, what changes about how you come to him? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
