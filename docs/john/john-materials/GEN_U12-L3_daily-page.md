## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 8:31-38** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus offers freedom to the people who believed him, and they are insulted, because they think they are already free.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 31 and 32, what does Jesus say leads to knowing the truth and being set free?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 33 the people say they have "never been enslaved to anyone." What do they think freedom means?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 34, then verse 36. Who does Jesus say is a slave, and who can set them free?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* people love to quote "the truth will set you free" (verse 32) all by itself. Read verses 34 through 36, where Jesus defines what he means, freedom from slavery to sin, given by the Son. Hold the question of why quoting verse 32 on its own turns it into the opposite of what Jesus said.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, who can set a slave to sin truly free, and how sure is that freedom?

## 5. Respond

Jesus says everyone is a slave to something and only the Son can free you. What owns you, and what would it be like to be free of it? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
