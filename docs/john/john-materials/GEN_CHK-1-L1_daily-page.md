## Your project

Today we launch the Q1 project, "The Signs." Here is the prompt: someone you know has never opened a Bible and thinks Jesus' miracles were magic tricks. Take one of John's seven signs and make something that shows them why John calls it a sign, and what it proves about who Jesus is. A magician wants you to watch the trick and hides how it is done. A sign is a window: you look through the miracle and see who Jesus is.

You have met only one sign so far, the water into wine at Cana. Four more are coming in chapters 4 through 6 before this is due, so do not lock in a sign you have not read yet.

Write it down. The sign I am leaning toward is ____________. The one friend I am making it for is ____________. My format is ____________ (video, infographic, first-person monologue, art with commentary, or a written piece).

The milestones: Pitch, then Plan, then a Draft or rehearsal with peer feedback, then Present it to the class with live Q&A, and a short Maker's Note at the end on why you built it this way. The heart of the grade is what the sign proves about who Jesus is, not the polish. Open the window.
