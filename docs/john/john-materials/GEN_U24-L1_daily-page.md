## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 18:1-11** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the prayer of John 17 is over, and Judas leads soldiers into the garden. We are stepping into the Passion, the story of Jesus' death, on sober and sacred ground.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 3, how many people come to arrest Jesus, and what are they carrying? Write what the verse says.

______________________________________________________________

In verses 5 and 6, when Jesus says "I am he," what happens to the whole armed crowd? Write it exactly.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 8, what does Jesus ask them to do for his disciples? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 11, what does Jesus say to Peter about the sword, and what does he say about the cup? Write the line about the cup.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in John 8:58 Jesus said, "before Abraham was, I am." Hold the question of why three plain words, "I am he," would knock an armed squad to the ground.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus was not caught and was not cornered. He says he will drink the cup the Father has given him. Write one thing that changes about how you see him, knowing he went to the cross on purpose.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"Shall I not drink the cup that the Father has given me?" He chose this, for you. Is that hard news or good news to you right now? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
