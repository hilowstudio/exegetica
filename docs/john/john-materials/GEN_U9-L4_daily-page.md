# Daily page, Unit 9 Lesson 4: I am the bread of life (John 6:30-40)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst." (John 6:35, ESV)

What am I running to when I feel empty? Write what you wish could actually fill you.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:30-40** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the crowd brings up the manna their ancestors ate in the desert, and Jesus tells them the real bread from heaven is standing in front of them.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 32 and 33, who does Jesus say really gave the bread from heaven, and what does he say the true bread is?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 35 slowly. What does Jesus call himself? Mark the two promises: what happens to the one who comes, and to the one who believes?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 37. Write the exact words Jesus uses about the one who comes to him.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verses 37 and 39 say the Father gives people to the Son and the Son loses none of them. Read that next to verse 35. Hold the question of how "the Father gave you to Jesus" could be the safest thing you ever heard, not the scariest.

## 4. The one thing from today

Say verse 35 back in your own words. What does Jesus promise?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says he satisfies a hunger nothing else can reach. What have you been feeding that hunger with, and what would it look like to bring it to him? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.

**Memory:** new verse. Learn John 6:35. It is one claim said twice, so learn it in two chunks: (1) "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger," (2) "and whoever believes in me shall never thirst."
**At home:** ask me why "comes to me" and "believes in me" are the same move.
