## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "The Father is in me and I am in the Father." (John 10:38, ESV)

What would it change if Jesus really is God? Write your honest question about who Jesus is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 10:31-38** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus just said "I and the Father are one," and now the crowd reaches for stones.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 31 and 33, why does the crowd want to stone Jesus? Write the exact charge they make.

______________________________________________________________

Jesus quotes Psalm 82, where Scripture called Israel's human judges "gods" because they held God's office of judging. In verse 36, how does Jesus use that to answer the charge that it is blasphemy for him to call himself God's Son?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 37 and 38. What does Jesus tell people who cannot believe his words yet to do instead?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* two wrong ways to read the Psalm 82 argument are "so we are all gods" and "so Jesus was only a man and denied being God." Read verse 38 again, where Jesus says "the Father is in me and I am in the Father." Hold the question of how his very next sentence rules out both of those readings.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what does the crowd reaching for stones tell you about what Jesus claimed in verse 30?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus told people who could not believe him yet to "believe the works." What is one thing Jesus did that you could look at honestly this week? Write it down. No one grades what you believe.
