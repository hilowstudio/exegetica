# Daily page, Unit 28 Lesson 4: Do you love me, the whole Christ and his question (John 20:31; 21:17)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "Simon, son of John, do you love me?" (John 21:17, ESV)

A year ago, who was Jesus to me? Who is he now? Write one honest line.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:31 and John 21:17** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: on the last day, we gather the whole Christ and hear his question.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

You've carried sixteen memory verses all year. Read them in order, out loud if you can, and write what you notice when you hear them one after another.

______________________________________________________________

John told you in 20:31 why he wrote the whole book, and Jesus' last words come in 21:17 and 21:19. Write the two things John wants for you (20:31), and the last thing Jesus says (21:19).

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* are the sixteen verses sixteen separate sayings, or something else? Hold that question as you read them in order, from "the Word became flesh" (1:14) to "feed my sheep" (21:17). Don't tie it off; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

One person, the whole way through, from "the Word became flesh" to "do you love me." Write the one thing about Jesus you'll carry out of this year.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

After a year in John, who is Jesus to you, and what would it look like to follow him? Write honestly. There is no answer you have to reach, and no one grades what you believe. The door is still open, and the invitation still stands.
