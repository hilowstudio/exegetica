# Reading guide, Unit 22: The Holy Spirit, gathered

> Passages: John 14:16-17, 14:26, 15:26-27, 16:7-11, 16:12-15
> Big question: we've studied the Holy Spirit one saying at a time. Put them together, and who is he?
> No new memory verse this unit. Keep your thirteen sharp.
> Read it in the ESV.

## How to use this guide

You don't turn this in. Keep it open next to your Reading Journal while we gather the Holy Spirit sayings in class.

- **Read** the five short sayings first, slowly.
- **Observe** means say what each saying tells us about the Spirit.
- **Interpret** asks how they fit together.
- **Respond** asks what it's asking of you.
- **Go deeper** prompts are optional.

Over the last few weeks, Jesus kept talking about "the Helper," the Holy Spirit, on his last night with his friends. We studied each saying where it sits. Today we do something different. We gather all five and ask what they teach together. This is called synthesis: taking scattered pieces and seeing the whole picture. It's also exactly what your group project needs to do with your chapter.

## The five sayings (read them back to back)

1. **John 14:16-17.** "Another Helper... to be with you forever... the Spirit of truth... he dwells with you and will be in you."
2. **John 14:26.** "The Helper, the Holy Spirit... he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you."
3. **John 15:26-27.** "The Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will bear witness about me."
4. **John 16:7-11.** "It is to your advantage that I go away... he will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment."
5. **John 16:12-15.** "He will guide you into all the truth... he will glorify me, for he will take what is mine and declare it to you."

## Five things these sayings teach about the Holy Spirit

Here's the picture once you put the pieces together.

**1. He's a Person, not a force.**
Jesus calls him "another Helper" (14:16), another of the same kind as Jesus himself. The word for "Helper" is the same word John uses for Jesus in 1 John 2:1. The Spirit does things only a person does: he teaches, reminds, witnesses, convicts, guides. Jesus calls him "he," not "it." A force is a battery you use. A Person is Someone who knows you and stays.

**2. He's fully God.**
He "proceeds from the Father" (15:26), and everything the Father has is Christ's, and the Spirit takes from it (16:15). The church has always confessed him as God: the Nicene Creed calls him "the Lord, the giver of life, who with the Father and the Son is worshiped and glorified." One God, three Persons.

**3. He's given and sent, and he comes because Jesus goes.**
The Father gives him (14:16) and sends him in Jesus' name (14:26). Jesus sends him from the Father (15:26; 16:7). "It is to your advantage that I go away" (16:7): Jesus goes to the Father, and the Spirit comes to live in his people forever (14:16-17).

**4. His work in Christians and the church.**
He teaches and reminds (14:26). He guides into all the truth (16:13). He bears witness to Jesus (15:26). He gives joy and peace all through this talk.

**5. His work in the world.**
He "convicts the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment" (16:8-11), proving the world wrong and pointing it to Christ.

## The one thing that ties it all together

> Look at all five sayings. Everything the Spirit does points the same direction. **"He will glorify me"** (John 16:14). The Spirit takes what belongs to Jesus and makes it known. Try to look straight at the Holy Spirit, and you end up seeing Jesus.

## Think it over

- **Observe.** From the five sayings, list three things the Holy Spirit does.
- **Interpret.** If the Spirit's whole job is to point to Jesus, how does that help you tell the difference between the real Holy Spirit and a feeling or an idea that pulls you away from Christ?
- **Respond.** The Spirit is God himself, living in every Christian, making Jesus real right now. If that's true, what changes about feeling like God is far away?
- **Go deeper (optional).** The Spirit is fully God, and his entire mission is to make Someone else (Jesus) look great. What does that kind of humility, in God himself, tell you about what God is like?

## Where this lands on Jesus

The Holy Spirit is not the weird part of Christianity. He's God himself, come to live in you, and his whole work is to make Jesus real to you and to hand you everything Jesus won. God didn't leave you on your own. He gave you himself, to show you his Son.
