# Daily page, Unit 6 Lesson 5: The Savior of the world (John 4:27-42)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this is indeed the Savior of the world." (John 4:42, ESV)

Whose faith am I living on right now, someone else's or my own?

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 4:27-42** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the woman runs to tell her town, the disciples are confused about food, and by the end the whole town believes.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 28, what does the woman leave behind at the well, and where does she go?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 35 Jesus says the fields are "white for harvest," while the townspeople are walking out to him (verse 30). What harvest is he talking about?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 39-42. At first the town believes "because of the woman's testimony." By the end, what do they say has changed? Quote their words.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the Samaritans, the outsiders, are the ones who call Jesus "the Savior of the world." Read John 1:29 and John 3:16. How far does "the world" reach? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice who is first to call Jesus "the Savior of the world," and what it took for the town to believe.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The town moved from believing on the woman's word to hearing Jesus for themselves. What would "hearing him for yourself" look like for you this week? Write honestly.
