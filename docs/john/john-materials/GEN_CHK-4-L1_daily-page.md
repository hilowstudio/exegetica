## One honest line

When Jesus said "It is finished," he did not mean "I am finished." He meant the work of saving you is done, paid in full, nothing left for you to add. If that verdict is already settled, what does it free you from? You do not have to be sure of it yet to answer honestly. No one grades what you believe.
