## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:31-36** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: John the Baptist finished talking at verse 30, so from verse 31 on this is the Gospel writer thinking out loud about who Jesus is, the same voice that gave us verse 16.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 31, where does Jesus come from? Where does everyone else come from? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 36 carefully. It gives two roads. For each road, write two things: what that person does about the Son, and what that person has or does not have.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Look closely at two words in verse 36: "has" on the believing side, and "remains" on the other. Is "has eternal life" about the future or right now? Does "remains" suggest the wrath is newly arriving, or already there?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 34 says God gives Jesus the Spirit "without measure," no limit. Look at Isaiah 11:2. What did Isaiah promise about the one God would send? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what the believer has right now, and whose hand holds your life.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Verse 36 says believing in the Son gives you life now. What is one thing that would look different this week if you lived like that were true? Write honestly.
