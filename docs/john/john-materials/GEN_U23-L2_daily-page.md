# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 2: I have shown them your name (John 17:6-8)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I have manifested your name to the people whom you gave me out of the world." (John 17:6, ESV)

What do I actually know about what God is like? Where did I learn it? Write it honestly, even if it is not much.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:6-8** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: still listening in on the Son praying to his Father. He has turned from praying for himself to praying for his friends, and now he tells the Father what he has done for them.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 6, what does Jesus say he has done for the people the Father gave him? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

"I have manifested your name" means to make God himself known. Connect it to yesterday's verse (17:3, eternal life is knowing God). How does Jesus make that knowing actually happen?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 8, what did Jesus give the disciples, and what three things did they do with it?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 6 says these people were "yours" first, and "you gave them to me." Hold the question of where faith starts, with us reaching for God, or with God reaching for us.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus showed his friends what God is really like. Write one thing about God that you know because of Jesus.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

You come to know God through the words of Jesus. What would change if you opened this book expecting to meet him? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
