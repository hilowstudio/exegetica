# Daily page, Unit 2 Lesson 2: Behold, the Lamb of God (John 1:29-34)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!" (John 1:29, ESV)

If someone could carry away the thing you most regret, and it was simply gone, what would that be worth?

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 1:29-34** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the next day. John sees Jesus walking toward him, and the first thing he says about him names how Jesus will die.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 29, what does John call Jesus, and what does he say Jesus does with sin? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

Twice John says, "I myself did not know him" (verses 31, 33). Look at verse 32. What did John see that finally showed him who Jesus was?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* if you want to chase the thread, read Isaiah 53:7, "like a lamb that is led to the slaughter... he opened not his mouth," written hundreds of years before Jesus. Sit with how that line changes the way you hear "Lamb of God." We will not answer it for you.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what gets announced over Jesus before he has done a single thing.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"The Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world." Is that good news to you right now, hard to believe, or something you have never really considered? Write honestly.
