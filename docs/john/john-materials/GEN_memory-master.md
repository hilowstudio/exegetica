# Gospel of John Memory Verses: Master Sheet

> **Course:** Sophomore Bible, Gospel of John: Yorkville Christian School  
> **Estimated time:** 5 minutes a day, all year (about 29 units)  
> **Materials:** This sheet, a pen, and your Bible (ESV)  
> **Objective 1:** Students will memorize 16 verses from John word-for-word in the ESV.  
> **Objective 2:** Students will keep old verses fresh using a rolling review, so the last verse is as sharp as the first.  
> **Objective 3:** Students will trace how these verses point to who Jesus is and what he came to do.  
> **Scripture:** All verses quoted from the ESV, pulled from the Study Bible tools.

---

## How this works

You'll learn one verse at a time, but you never really leave a verse behind. Once a verse is in your set, it stays in review for the rest of the year. That's what "rolling review" means. The goal isn't to cram and forget. The goal is that in May you can still say John 20:31 from memory.

A few things to know before you start:

- Only 16 of our 29 units add a new verse. On the other units, you're reviewing, not learning something new. That's on purpose. Your brain needs the repetition.
- Can't find the passage yet? Open your Bible to the table of contents, find "John," then the big number is the chapter and the small number is the verse. John 3:16 means the book of John, chapter 3, verse 16.
- Some verses are one sentence. John 21:17 is longer. Don't panic when you hit the long ones. The plan gives you extra days for those.

> **The point of all of it:** Every verse on this sheet is telling you something about Jesus. John says why he wrote his whole book in the first verse you'll learn (John 20:31), so read that one like a map for the rest.

## The 16 verses (in the order you'll learn them)

These are printed in the exact order we memorize them, which is not chapter order. We start at the end (John 20:31) because that verse tells you John's reason for writing everything else.

**1. John 20:31, Unit 0 (why John wrote the book)**
> but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name.

**2. John 1:14, Unit 1 (the Word became flesh)**
> And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth.

**3. John 1:12, Unit 2 (children of God)**
> But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God,

**4. John 3:16, Unit 4 (God so loved the world)**
> "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.

**5. John 4:24, Unit 6 (worship in spirit and truth)**
> God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."

**6. John 6:35, Unit 9 (I am the bread of life)**
> Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst.

**7. John 8:12, Unit 12 (I am the light of the world)**
> Again Jesus spoke to them, saying, "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life."

**8. John 10:11, Unit 14 (the good shepherd)**
> I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.

**9. John 11:25, Unit 15 (the resurrection and the life)**
> Jesus said to her, "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live,

**10. John 13:34–35, Unit 18 (a new commandment)**
> A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another. By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another."

**11. John 14:6, Unit 19 (the way, the truth, the life)**
> Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.

**12. John 15:5, Unit 20 (the vine and the branches)**
> I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.

**13. John 16:33, Unit 21 (I have overcome the world)**
> I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world."

**14. John 17:3, Unit 23 (this is eternal life)**
> And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent.

**15. John 19:30, Unit 25 (it is finished)**
> When Jesus had received the sour wine, he said, "It is finished," and he bowed his head and gave up his spirit.

**16. John 21:17, Unit 27 (feed my sheep)**
> He said to him the third time, "Simon, son of John, do you love me?" Peter was grieved because he said to him the third time, "Do you love me?" and he said to him, "Lord, you know everything; you know that I love you." Jesus said to him, "Feed my sheep.

### A five-day rhythm for each new verse

When a unit adds a verse, spread it across the week like this:

1. **Day 1**: Read the new verse out loud 5 times. Read the short "context note" for it below.
2. **Day 2**: Copy the verse by hand once, then say it looking only at the first-letter line.
3. **Day 3**: Say the new verse from memory. Check it against this sheet. Fix what you missed.
4. **Day 4**: Say the new verse plus your two most recent review verses.
5. **Day 5**: Say the new verse cold, then run your full review list once.

## Memory helps

### Chunking the long ones

Break a verse into small pieces and learn one piece at a time before you string them together.

- **John 3:16** in three pieces: (1) "For God so loved the world," (2) "that he gave his only Son," (3) "that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
- **John 13:34–35** in two commands: (1) love one another *just as I have loved you*, (2) *by this* all people will know you're my disciples.
- **John 21:17** as a scene: Jesus asks a third time → Peter is grieved → Peter answers "you know everything" → Jesus says "Feed my sheep." Learn the four beats, then fill in the words.

### First-letter cues

Cover the verse and try to rebuild it from just the first letters. Here are two to start you off:

- **John 20:31, ** b t a w s t y m b t J i t C, t S o G, a t b b y m h l i h n.
- **John 14:6, ** J s t h, "I a t w, a t t, a t l. N o c t t F e t m."

Make your own first-letter line for each verse on a scrap sheet. Writing the cue is half the memory work.

### The "I am" thread

Seven of these verses are Jesus saying "I am" something. Learning them as a group makes them stick:

- 6:35 bread of life
- 8:12 light of the world
- 10:11 good shepherd
- 11:25 resurrection and the life
- 14:6 way, truth, life
- 15:5 vine

If you can list what Jesus says he *is*, you're most of the way to the verse.

### Context notes (so the words mean something)

You remember what you understand. One line of setting for each verse:

- **20:31**, John's mission statement. He wrote so you'd believe and have life.
- **1:14**, The eternal Word (Jesus) took on a human body and lived among people.
- **1:12**, Everyone who receives Jesus is given the right to be God's child.
- **3:16**, Jesus tells Nicodemus at night why God sent his Son.
- **4:24**, Jesus tells a Samaritan woman what real worship is.
- **6:35**, After feeding 5,000, Jesus says he himself is the true food.
- **8:12**, At the temple, Jesus claims to be the light people need to walk by.
- **10:11**, Jesus is the shepherd who dies for the sheep instead of running.
- **11:25**, At Lazarus's tomb, Jesus tells Martha he *is* resurrection life.
- **13:34–35**, At the Last Supper, the new command: love like Jesus loved.
- **14:6**, Jesus answers Thomas: he's the only way to the Father.
- **15:5**, Stay connected to Jesus like a branch to a vine, or you bear nothing.
- **16:33**, Jesus promises peace and warns of trouble, then says he's won.
- **17:3**, In his prayer, Jesus defines eternal life as knowing God and himself.
- **19:30**, On the cross Jesus says the work of salvation is complete.
- **21:17**, Risen Jesus restores Peter and recommissions him to shepherd others.
