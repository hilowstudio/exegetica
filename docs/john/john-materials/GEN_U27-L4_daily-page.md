# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 4: Do you love me (John 21:15-17)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Simon, son of John, do you love me?" (John 21:16, ESV)

If Jesus asked me, "do you love me," what would I honestly say? Write it. This page is safe, and an honest answer is welcome.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:15-17** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: breakfast is over, and now Jesus turns to Peter by the fire.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 15 through 17, how many times does Jesus ask Peter if he loves him? What does Jesus tell Peter to do after each answer? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

Peter denied Jesus three times, at a fire (look back at John 18:15-27). Write what you notice when you set that next to how many times Jesus now asks "do you love me."

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 17. The third time, how does Peter feel, and what does he finally say back to Jesus? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "feed my sheep." Hold the question of whose sheep they are (look back at John 10:11), and where the restored Peter went with this charge (peek at 1 Peter 5:2, written by Peter years later). Sit with it; don't rush to answer.

## 4. The one thing from today

Three denials, three chances to say "I love you," and a flock to feed. Write the one thing you want to remember about how Jesus restores people who fail.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus' one question is "do you love me," not how good you are, or how much you know. How do you honestly answer him today? Write honestly. There is no wrong answer to write here, and belief is never scored.

**Memory:** new verse, and the last one. Learn John 21:17: "He said to him the third time, 'Simon, son of John, do you love me?' Peter was grieved because he said to him the third time, 'Do you love me?' and he said to him, 'Lord, you know everything; you know that I love you.' Jesus said to him, 'Feed my sheep.'" Learn it in two chunks: (1) "He said to him the third time, 'Simon, son of John, do you love me?' Peter was grieved because he said to him the third time, 'Do you love me?'" (2) "and he said to him, 'Lord, you know everything; you know that I love you.' Jesus said to him, 'Feed my sheep.'" This is your sixteenth and last anchor, and it completes the whole set. Keep your fifteen priors sharp too: John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16, 4:24, 6:35, 8:12, 10:11, 11:25, 13:34-35, 14:6, 15:5, 16:33, 17:3, and 19:30. There is no new verse after this one; the story of Jesus in John is complete.
**At home:** ask me why Jesus asked Peter "do you love me" three times.
