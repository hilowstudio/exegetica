# Daily page, Unit 9 Lesson 3: Why are you looking for me? (John 6:25-29)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent." (John 6:29, ESV)

Why do I actually come to God? One honest minute.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 6:25-29** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the day after the feeding, the crowd tracks Jesus down, and he tells them why they are really looking for him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 26, what reason does Jesus give for why the crowd is looking for him? In verses 28 and 29 the crowd asks what they must do. What one work does Jesus name?

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 27 sets "food that perishes" next to "food that endures to eternal life." Hold the question of how believing can be called a "work" that you cannot take any credit for.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what is the one work God asks, and why is that good news instead of one more task?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Be honest. Do you come to Jesus for him, or for what he gives? Write what it would look like to come to him for himself. No shame in the answer.
