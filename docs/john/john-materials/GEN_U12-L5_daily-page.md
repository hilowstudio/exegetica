# Daily page, Unit 12 Lesson 5: Before Abraham was, I am (John 8:48-59)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Before Abraham was, I am." (John 8:58, ESV)

If Jesus really is God, what does that change for me? Write your honest question about whether Jesus is who he says.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 8:48-59** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the whole argument has been building, and now Jesus says the biggest thing of all.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 51, what does Jesus promise the person who keeps his word?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 56: "your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day. He saw it and was glad." Abraham lived two thousand years before Jesus. Write what Jesus is claiming about himself and about Abraham.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 58 slowly. Notice Jesus says "I am," not "I was." Then read verse 59: what does the crowd do right after?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "I am," not "I was," is the whole point. Read Exodus 3:14, where God names himself "I AM." Hold the question of why the present tense, "I am," about a time before Abraham was even born, is a bigger claim than "I existed long ago."

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what did the crowd understand Jesus to be claiming in verse 58, and how do you know?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"Before Abraham was, I am" is a claim you cannot ignore. Either Jesus is God or he is not. Which way do you lean right now, and what makes you lean that way? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
