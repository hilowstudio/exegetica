## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 18:12-18** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: they bind Jesus and lead him to the high priest, and Peter follows at a distance and warms himself at a fire.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 12 and 13, what do the soldiers do to Jesus, and where do they take him first? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 14, John reminds us of something Caiaphas had said earlier. What did he say about one man? Write the words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 17, the servant girl asks Peter if he is one of Jesus' disciples. What does Peter answer? Write his three words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 18, where is Peter standing, and what is he doing, while Jesus is inside being questioned? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* hold Peter's "I am not" (verse 17) next to Jesus' "I am he" from the garden (18:5). Same night, same few hours. Sit with the contrast; do not resolve it yet.

## 4. The one thing from today

The man arranging Jesus' death said the truest thing in the room without knowing it: "one man should die for the people." Write the one thing you want to remember from that.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

One man dying for the people is why Jesus came. What does it mean to you that he died in your place? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
