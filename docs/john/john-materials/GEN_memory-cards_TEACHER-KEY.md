# Teacher key - GEN_memory-cards

## Answer Key

This set is for memorization, so the "answers" are the exact ESV words a student should be able to recite from each reference. Score by comparing the student's recitation to the text below word for word.

1. **John 20:31**, but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name.
2. **John 1:14**, And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth.
3. **John 1:12**, But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God,
4. **John 3:16**, For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.
5. **John 4:24**, God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.
6. **John 6:35**, I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst.
7. **John 8:12**, I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.
8. **John 10:11**, I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.
9. **John 11:25**, I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live,
10. **John 13:34-35**, A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another. By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.
11. **John 14:6**, I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.
12. **John 15:5**, I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.
13. **John 16:33**, I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world.
14. **John 17:3**, And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent.
15. **John 19:30**, It is finished,
16. **John 21:17**, Feed my sheep.

Scoring note: Cards 1, 3, and 9 end in a comma in the ESV because the sentence continues past the memory verse. Accept a clean stop there. Count minor filler slips (a dropped "and") as review items, not failures, and have the student say the full verse once more before moving on.
