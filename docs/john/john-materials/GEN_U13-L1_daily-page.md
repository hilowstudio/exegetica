# Daily page, Unit 13 Lesson 1: The sign and the first questions (John 9:1-12)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world." (John 9:5, ESV)

Is there something hard in my life I have assumed is God punishing me? Write the honest question about your own hard things. No one reads this but you.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 9:1-12** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus and his disciples pass a man who has been blind his whole life. Last unit Jesus said, "I am the light of the world." Watch what he does here.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 2, what do the disciples assume caused the man's blindness? In verse 3, how does Jesus answer, and what does he say it is for?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 5, then verses 6 and 7. Jesus repeats the claim from last unit. What does he then do to a man born in total darkness?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 7, John stops to tell us the pool's name, Siloam, means "Sent." Write down what the name means.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus makes new eyes out of mud and spit. Read Genesis 2:7, where God formed the first man from the dust of the ground. Hold the question of what it says about Jesus that he opens blind eyes by forming them from the dust, the way the Creator once did.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what did Jesus say the man's blindness was for?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says this man's suffering was not punishment, and that God had a good purpose in it. If God could bring something good out of your hardest thing, what would you want him to do with it? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
