## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 9:35-41** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the man got thrown out for Jesus, and now Jesus comes looking for him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 35, what does Jesus do when he hears the man was cast out? In verse 38, after Jesus reveals who he is, what does the man do?

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in the Bible, angels and apostles refuse worship (an angel stops John in Revelation 19:10; Peter lifts up a man kneeling to him in Acts 10:26). Jesus lets the man worship him and does not stop him. Hold the question of what Jesus is claiming by receiving worship that others refuse.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, in verse 39 Jesus names two kinds of blindness. Which kind of person ends up seeing?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus gives sight to people who admit they cannot see. Where do you need to stop pretending you are fine and ask him to help you see? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
