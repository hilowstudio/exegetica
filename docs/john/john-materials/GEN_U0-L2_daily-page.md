# Daily page, Unit 0 Lesson 2: Entering John, day 2 (John 1:14; 19:35; 21:24)

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory." (John 1:14a, ESV)

Would it change how you read a story if you knew the person who wrote it was actually there and watched it happen? Write your honest reaction.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 1:14; 19:35; 21:24** (the three places John tells you he was there).

Where we are: three places, near the start and at the very end, where the writer stops the story to tell you he saw it himself.

In class we will watch a short video that maps the whole book. Watch it. There is nothing to fill in from it later, so just watch.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Here are four small, oddly specific things John writes down: the six stone water jars at Cana, holding twenty or thirty gallons (2:6); the five covered porches at the pool of Bethesda (5:2); a servant named Malchus, and that it was his right ear that was cut (18:10); exactly 153 fish in the net (21:11). What do these tiny details tell you about the person who wrote this book?

*Go deeper (optional):* John wrote decades after the other three Gospels, and his book reads very differently. Why might the church have wanted a fourth account like this one? Hold it. We will watch John's angle all year.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice where the writer points: past himself, to the One he saw.

## 5. Respond

John says he saw Jesus with his own eyes and wrote it down so you could decide for yourself. Are you willing to look? What would it take for you? No one will grade your answer.
