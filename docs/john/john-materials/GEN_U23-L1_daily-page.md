## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:1-5** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus stops teaching his friends, lifts his eyes to heaven, and starts praying to his Father. We get to listen in. This is the first of three parts of the prayer: he prays for himself (1-5), then for his friends, then for everyone who will ever believe. The first thing he prays about is the cross.
