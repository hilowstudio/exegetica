# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 3: He calls her by name (John 20:11-16)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "Jesus said to her, 'Mary.' She turned and said to him in Aramaic, 'Rabboni!' (which means Teacher)." (John 20:16, ESV)

Does it feel like God knows me personally, by name? Why, or why not? Write one line.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:11-16** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Peter and John go home, but Mary stays at the tomb, weeping.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 14 and 15, Mary turns and sees Jesus. Does she recognize him? Who does she think he is? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 16. What one word does Jesus say, and what does Mary say back? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in John 10:3, Jesus said the good shepherd "calls his own sheep by name," and "they know his voice." Hold that line next to verse 16, and sit with what it means that he knew her name. Don't rush to tie it off.

## 4. The one thing from today

He said one word, her name, and she knew him. Write the one thing you notice about how Jesus met Mary.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The risen Lord of everything knew one grieving woman by name, and he knows yours. If you really believed Jesus knows you personally, by name, what would change today? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
