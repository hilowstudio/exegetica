# Daily page, Unit 14 Lesson 1: The fold, the voice, and the door (John 10:1-10)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "The sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name." (John 10:3, ESV)

Whose voice do I let steer me most? Write the honest answer, even if it is not God. No one reads this but you.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 10:1-10** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: there is no break here from chapter 9, so Jesus is still talking to the leaders who threw the healed blind man out of the synagogue.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 3 through 5, how do the sheep know which shepherd to follow? What do they do when a stranger calls?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 9 Jesus says, "I am the door." Write what he says a person who enters through him will find (verse 9), and what he came to give (verse 10).

______________________________________________________________

There is a thief in verse 10. Write what the thief comes to do, set next to what Jesus came to do.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* long before this, in Ezekiel 34, God accused Israel's leaders of being bad shepherds who fed themselves and neglected the sheep, and promised he himself would come to shepherd them. Read Ezekiel 34:2-6 and 34:11. Hold the question of what Jesus is quietly saying about these leaders, and about himself, by calling himself the door of the sheep.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what does Jesus mean when he calls himself "the door"?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

A lot of voices want to lead you. Whose are you following right now, and does that voice want you or want something from you? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
