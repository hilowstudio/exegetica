## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 8:39-47** (out loud if you can).

Where we are, said carefully: this is a sharp, hard part of the argument. It is about the heart and who people belong to, and it has nothing to do with anyone's ancestry or ethnicity. Jesus is Jewish, everyone he is talking to is Jewish, and some of them had just believed him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)
