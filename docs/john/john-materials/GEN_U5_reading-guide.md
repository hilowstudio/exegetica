# Reading guide, Unit 5: He must increase

> Passage: John 3:22-36
> Big question: what happens in a person when Jesus gets bigger and they get smaller?
> Memory work: no new verse this unit. Keep your four review verses sharp.
> Read it in the ESV.

## How to use this guide

You don't turn this in. Keep it open next to your Reading Journal while we work the passage in class.

- **Read** the passage first, slowly, before you write anything.
- **Observe** means say what the text actually says. Names, actions, who talks, what changes.
- A good observation points to a word or a line you can put your finger on. "John's disciples are worried about the crowd size" beats "this is about humility."
- **Interpret** asks what it means and who Jesus is in it.
- **Respond** asks what it's asking of you.
- **Go deeper** prompts are optional. Take them if you want more.

## Lesson 1: the friend of the bridegroom (John 3:22-30)

**Read it.** John chapter 3, verses 22 through 30. If you're new to finding things in the Bible, use the table of contents up front, turn to John, then find the big 3 for the chapter and the small 22 for the verse.

The scene: Jesus and his disciples are baptizing in the countryside, and so is John the Baptist a little ways off. John's followers get upset because the crowds are leaving John and going to Jesus. They come to John expecting him to be bothered. He isn't.

- **Observe.** In verse 26, what are John's disciples worried about? Write the exact thing that's bugging them.
- **Observe.** In verse 30, what does John say has to happen to Jesus, and what has to happen to himself?
- **Interpret.** John calls himself the friend of the bridegroom, the best man at the wedding (verse 29). The best man's whole job is to bring the bride and groom together and then step back. Why is John glad when the crowd leaves him for Jesus?
- **Respond.** John's calm came from knowing exactly who he was and what his job was. Where do you feel the pull to be the center of attention? What would it look like to be glad someone else got bigger?
- **Go deeper (optional).** Back in verse 27 John says, "A person cannot receive even one thing unless it is given him from heaven." How does that one line take the pressure off John about the shrinking crowd?

## Lesson 2: the One from above (John 3:31-36)

**Read it.** John chapter 3, verses 31 through 36.

Quick note so you're not confused: John the Baptist stops talking at verse 30. From verse 31 on, it's the author of the Gospel, John, thinking out loud about who Jesus is. Same voice that gave us the famous verse 16 a few pages back.

- **Observe.** In verse 31, where does Jesus come from, and how does that make him different from every other teacher?
- **Observe.** Verse 36 lays out two roads. What does the person who believes in the Son have right now? What stays on the person who refuses him?
- **Interpret.** Verse 35 says, "The Father loves the Son and has given all things into his hand." If Jesus holds all things, and your life is one of those things, what does that mean for you today?
- **Respond.** Verse 36 says believing in the Son gives you eternal life that starts now. What would change in your week if that were actually true of you?
- **Go deeper (optional).** Verse 34 says God gives Jesus the Spirit "without measure," no limit. Look at Isaiah 11:2. What did Isaiah promise about the one God would send?

## Key words to know

- **Bridegroom.** The groom at a wedding. John uses it for Jesus. The people who belong to Jesus are his bride.
- **Friend of the bridegroom.** The best man. He sets up the wedding and then gets out of the way so the groom can have his bride.
- **Testimony.** Telling what you've seen and heard is true. Verse 32 says Jesus testifies to what he saw in heaven.
- **Wrath.** God's settled, right response against sin. Verse 36 says it stays on a person until they come to the Son. It's the very thing Jesus came to take away.

There's no new memory verse in Unit 5. Your job is to keep the four you already have sharp:

>

>

>
> John 3:16: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."

The next new verse comes in Unit 6, John 4:24.

Verse 3:30, "He must increase, but I must decrease," is one of the most famous lines in the book. It isn't on the quiz. If you want to carry it anyway, carry it.

## Where the whole unit lands on Jesus

Jesus is the bridegroom, the One from heaven who holds the Spirit without limit and holds your life in his hand. John the Baptist gets smaller on purpose because Jesus is worth it. To have the Son is to have life right now.
