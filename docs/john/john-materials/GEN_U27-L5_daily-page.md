# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 5: Follow me (John 21:18-19)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "And after saying this he said to him, 'Follow me.'" (John 21:19, ESV)

Where does following Jesus cost me something right now? Write one line.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:18-19** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: right after restoring Peter, Jesus tells him how his story will end.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 18, what does Jesus say will happen to Peter when he is old? In verse 19, what does John tell us that meant? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

Read the last two words Jesus says in verse 19. Write them, and write who he says them to.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in John 13:37, Peter bragged, "I will lay down my life for you," and then he folded. Hold that next to what Jesus predicts here, and sit with how the prediction could be grace and not doom. Leave it open.

## 4. The one thing from today

The coward at the fire would one day die faithful, because the Spirit changed him. Write the one thing that gives you about your own failures.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

"Follow me," whatever it costs. Where is Jesus asking you to follow him somewhere that costs you something? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
