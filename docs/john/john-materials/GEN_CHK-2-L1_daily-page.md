## Jog your memory

We will say these together first, then you write what you can. Three chapters, one widening offer: Jesus goes to the outsider, the helpless, and the hungry, and to each one he gives himself.

John 4: Jesus sits down tired at a well and asks a shamed outsider for a drink. What does he offer her, and what does he tell her about worship?

______________________________________________________________

John 5: a man has been down thirty-eight years and no one helps him. Jesus heals him on the Sabbath on purpose. When they come to kill him, what does he claim about himself and the Father?

______________________________________________________________

John 6: he feeds thousands from a boy's lunch, then the crowd chases him for more bread. What does he tell them he really is?

## One honest line

Jesus offers himself as living water and as bread, and says whoever comes to him will never be cast out (6:37). What are you actually hungry for right now? You do not have to believe his offer yet to answer honestly. No one grades what you believe.

## Your project

"The Signs" project comes in today. Turn it in, and remember what the assignment really was: John wrote his whole book so people would meet Christ through his signs and have life in his name (20:31). If you took one sign and opened that window for a friend, you did the very thing John did.

One honest line for your Maker's Note: what was the hardest part of showing your friend who Jesus is, and did your project actually land on him?
