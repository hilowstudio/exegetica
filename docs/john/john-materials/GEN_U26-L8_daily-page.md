# Daily page, Unit 26 Lesson 8: Why this book was written (John 20:30-31)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 5 minutes)

> "but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name." (John 20:31, ESV)

We started the year with this verse. Where am I with it now? Write one honest line.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:30-31** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: right after Thomas worships the risen Lord, John steps back and tells you why he wrote all of this.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 30, what does John say about the signs Jesus did? Did he write down all of them? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 31. John tells you the two things he wants for you: what to believe, and what to have. Write both, in his words.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* you have carried this verse since the first day of the course, and now you are standing in its home, at the end of John's own account, right after a doubter worshiped the risen Lord. Hold the question of why John put it right here. Sit with it; don't rush to answer.

## 4. The one thing from today

Believe, and have life. Write the one thing you want to carry out of this whole book.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

John wrote this whole book so that you would believe and have life. We started the year with this verse. Where are you with it today? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and there is nothing here to earn.
