Welcome. This year we're reading one book, slowly and in order: the Gospel of John, from the first verse to the last. Whether you've read the Bible your whole life or you've never opened one and can't find a passage yet, there's a place for you here. Come as you are, and bring your questions.

## Course description

We're going to read John verse by verse, in the order he wrote it. No skipping ahead, no highlight reel. John tells us exactly why he wrote the book near the end of it:

> "but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name." (John 20:31, ESV)

That's our map. Everything we read points at the person of Jesus and at one question John keeps pressing: will you believe him? We'll treat this book as the living Word of God, not an old document to dissect. And we'll read it as one story that finds its center in Christ.

## The aim of the course

The whole year keeps coming back to four questions:

- Who is Jesus, according to John?
- What does John mean by "believing," and what does believing actually change?
- How does the whole story of the Bible come together in Christ?
- What does it look like to receive grace instead of trying to earn it?

You don't have to have the answers on day one. We'll work them out together, in the text.

## How the year is organized

The year falls into two halves, following the shape of John's own book.

**Fall: the Book of Signs (John 1–12)**
- Jesus reveals who he is through seven signs and long conversations.
- We watch belief and unbelief grow side by side.

**Spring: the Book of Glory (John 13–21)**
- The upper room and Jesus' final teaching to his friends.
- The cross.
- The resurrection.

## How we study

Every class runs on the same rhythm, so you always know what to expect:

1. **Quiet time (7 minutes).** We open with prayer and journaling. Space to get still before we read.
2. **Read aloud.** We hear the passage read together.
3. **Work the text in John groups.** You'll dig into the passage in a small discussion group, in context, watching how it points to Christ.
4. **Respond in writing.** We close by writing down what we saw and what it asks of us.

Verse by verse, in context, centered on Jesus. That's the method all year.

## Projects

One project per quarter. Each one is built in steps (a proposal, a plan, and a draft) and presented to the class.

- **Q1: The Signs.** Make something that shows why one of Jesus' miracles is a "sign" and what it reveals about him.
- **Q2: Character Study.** Get inside one person's encounter with Jesus in John.
- **Q3: Teaching the Farewell Discourse.** In a small group, teach a section of John 13–17 to the class.
- **Q4: The Passion and a Personal Response.** Trace the crucifixion and resurrection across John 18–20 and respond honestly to John 20:31.
