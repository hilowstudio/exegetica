*Go deeper (optional):* way back in John 1:29, at this very spot, John the Baptist pointed at Jesus and said, "Behold, the Lamb of God." Verse 41 notes that John "did no sign" and still told the truth. Hold the question of why John records that the story circles back to where the first witness began, and what it means that you do not have to be flashy to point people to the right person.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, the chapter opens in Jerusalem with people grabbing stones and closes at the Jordan with people believing. What is the difference between the two crowds?
