## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 12:44-50** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: these are the very last words Jesus speaks to the public in the whole book. After this, he is only with his disciples.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 44 and 45, what does Jesus say happens when you believe in him, or see him?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 47, what does Jesus say he came to do? In verse 48, what will judge a person on the last day?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus says he came to save and not to judge (verse 47), and also that his word will judge on the last day (verse 48). Think about a first coming and a second coming. How might both be true, in that order? Hold it for class.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what is the one thing Jesus most wants the crowd to know in his last public words?

## 5. Respond
