## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 4:20-26** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the woman changes the subject to an old argument about where to worship, and Jesus takes it somewhere she did not expect. Then he tells her plainly, for the first time in the book, that he is the Messiah.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 20, what is the argument the woman brings up? Whose mountain versus whose city?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 21-24. Jesus does not pick the mountain or the city. Write the two words he uses in verse 24 for how true worship happens.

______________________________________________________________

Look at the first three words of verse 24. Write them. That is the reason Jesus gives for how we worship.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 26, the woman says the Messiah is coming. What does Jesus tell her?

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus tells a Samaritan woman with a rough past that he is the Messiah, before he ever says it plainly in Jerusalem. Why do you think John records that the outsider heard it first? Hold it.
