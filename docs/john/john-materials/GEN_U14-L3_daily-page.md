## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "No one will snatch them out of my hand." (John 10:28, ESV)

What am I most afraid of losing? Write the honest fear, whatever it is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 10:19-30** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: it is now winter, the Feast of Dedication, and the leaders corner Jesus and demand a straight answer about whether he is the Christ.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 20 and 21, the crowd is split. What do the people who defend Jesus point back to?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 28 and 29 slowly. What does Jesus promise his sheep, and how many hands are holding them?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 30. Write what Jesus says about himself and the Father, right after talking about the two hands.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 27 says his sheep "hear my voice... and follow me." Hold the question of how following shows that a person really is one of his sheep, without turning the following into the thing that earns the safety.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, whose hands hold the sheep, and what does Jesus say about anyone trying to snatch them out?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says your safety rests on his grip and the Father's grip, not on how tightly you hold on. If that is true, what fear could you set down? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
