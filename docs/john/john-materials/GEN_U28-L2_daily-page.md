# Daily page, Unit 28 Lesson 2: In his own words, the "I am" sayings (John 6-15)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "I am the way, and the truth, and the life." (John 14:6, ESV)

Which of Jesus' "I am" sayings means the most to me right now, and why? Write it.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: the "I am" sayings back to back, John 6:35, 8:12, 10:11, 11:25, 14:6, 15:5, and then 8:58** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: over and over, Jesus told people who he is, starting with two words: "I am."

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

List the "I am the..." sayings you can remember, and for each, write what Jesus offers and what human need it meets.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Read John 8:58: "before Abraham was, I am." Write what happens right after he says it (verse 59).

______________________________________________________________

John 14:6 says, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me." Write both halves down exactly as John has them.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back in Exodus 3:14, God tells Moses his name is "I AM." Hold that next to "before Abraham was, I am," and sit with why the crowd reached for stones. Don't answer it yet; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

Every "I am" names Jesus as the thing you're missing, and "I am" is God's own name. Write the one "I am" you most want to remember.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Every "I am" saying offers you Jesus himself for your deepest need. Which need do you most feel, and which "I am" meets it? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
