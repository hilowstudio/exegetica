# Daily page, Unit 7 Lesson 2: Take him at his word (John 4:49-54)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Go; your son will live." (John 4:50, ESV)

Is there something Jesus said that I would have to trust before I could see it? Write what you would need to believe him for, even if you are not there yet.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 4:49-54** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: last class the dad begged Jesus to come heal his son. Today Jesus answers with one sentence.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 49 the dad asks again. What exactly does he still want Jesus to do?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 50. Jesus stays where he is and gives him one sentence: "Go; your son will live." What does the dad do about it? Remember, he has not seen anything yet.

______________________________________________________________

Look at verse 50 and verse 53 together. Verse 50 says he "believed the word." Verse 53 says "he himself believed, and all his household." Write both. Notice John shows him believing twice.

______________________________________________________________

In verses 52 and 53, what does the dad find out about the timing?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* in verse 54 John calls this "the second sign." The first sign was also at Cana (John 2, water into wine). Why might John frame these two as a matched pair? Read Psalm 33:9, "he spoke, and it came to be," next to John 1:1-3. What does a healing by a bare word tell you about Jesus? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what the dad trusted, and when, next to what he could actually see.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus healed a boy fifteen miles away with one sentence, and the dad trusted it the whole way home. What is one thing Jesus says that you would have to trust before you could see it work? Write honestly.
