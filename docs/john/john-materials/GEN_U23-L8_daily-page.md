# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 8: Bring them home, and the love in them (John 17:24-26)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "That the love with which you have loved me may be in them, and I in them." (John 17:26, ESV)

When I picture heaven, what do I picture? Write it honestly.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:24-26** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the close of the prayer, and of the whole last-night talk. Jesus finishes with the thing he wants most, and the deepest note in everything he has said.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 24, what does Jesus say he wants ("I desire") for his people? Write his words.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 24, Jesus describes the goal as "be with me where I am, to see my glory." Is heaven mainly a place, or mainly a Person?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 26, the last line of the prayer, what does Jesus want to be IN his people?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* back at John 13:34, earlier this same night, Jesus commanded "love one another as I have loved you." Now the prayer ends with the Father's love placed IN his people (17:26). Hold the question of how the end makes the command possible.

## 4. The one thing from today

In verse 24 Jesus says what he wants most for you: to be with him, and to see his glory. After today's talk, write what that changes about what you are hoping for.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The last thing Jesus prayed was that God's own love would live in you. If that is the goal of everything, what is one thing it changes about how you see yourself? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
