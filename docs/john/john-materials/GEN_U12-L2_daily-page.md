# Daily page, Unit 12 Lesson 2: The I AM, lifted up (John 8:21-30)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "He who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone." (John 8:29, ESV)

Where do I feel most alone right now? Write the honest loneliness, whatever it is.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 8:21-30** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the argument in the temple keeps going, and Jesus starts using the most loaded name in the Bible about himself.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 24, what does Jesus say a person has to believe so they do not "die in their sins"?

______________________________________________________________

Read verse 28. Jesus says, "when you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he." "Lifted up" is his word for the cross. Write what he says will happen at that moment.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 30, after all this hard talk, what does the passage say many people did?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* "I am he" (verse 24) reaches back to Exodus 3:14, where God tells Moses his name is "I AM WHO I AM." Read verse 29 next to it: "he who sent me is with me... I always do the things that are pleasing to him." Hold the question of how that could be a comfort when the whole room is against you.

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, what does verse 24 say a person has to believe about Jesus?

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus says believing who he really is changes everything. Who do you actually believe Jesus is, right now? Write it honestly. No one grades what you believe.
