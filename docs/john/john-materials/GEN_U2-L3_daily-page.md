# Daily page, Unit 2 Lesson 3: The first disciples follow (John 1:35-42)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God." (John 1:12, ESV)

Jesus' first question in this Gospel is "What are you seeking?" If he asked you that today, what is your honest answer? An honest "I do not know" is a real answer.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 1:35-42** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the pointing finally works. John points at Jesus, and two men actually leave to follow him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Jesus turns and speaks his first words in this Gospel (verse 38). What does he ask? What do the two men ask back, and what does he say to them (verse 39)? Write their words.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice when Jesus gives Simon a new name, next to anything Simon has done yet.

## 5. Respond

Jesus renamed Simon before he had done anything to deserve it. What would it mean to have your identity given to you by Jesus, instead of earned by your record? Write honestly.
