# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 3: Breakfast on the beach (John 21:9-14)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus said to them, 'Come and have breakfast.'" (John 21:12, ESV)

When I've done wrong, do I expect God to be cold to me? Why? Write honestly.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:9-14** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the disciples wade ashore, dripping, and find breakfast already cooking.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 9, what do the disciples find waiting on the shore? In verse 12, what does Jesus invite them to do? Write both.

______________________________________________________________

Look carefully at the words "charcoal fire" in verse 9. Now look back at John 18:18. Write where the only other charcoal fire in the New Testament was, and who stood at it.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Before Jesus says anything about Peter's failure, what does he do first (verses 12-13)? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 14 says this is the third time the risen Jesus appeared to his disciples, and he spends it cooking and serving. Hold the question of what kind of risen King does that. Don't tie it off; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus built a fire like the one Peter failed at, and cooked breakfast before he said a word about it. Write the one thing that tells you about how Jesus meets failure.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Jesus fed the ones who failed him before he ever mentioned it. What does that change about how you think God feels about you when you've messed up? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
