## One honest line

John 17:20 is Jesus praying for everyone who would believe through his disciples' word, and that reaches this room. What would it mean to you to be prayed for by him on the last night of his life? You do not have to be sure of it yet to answer honestly. No one grades what you believe.

## Your project

The Q3 group project, "Teaching the Farewell Discourse," comes in today. Turn it in, and remember what the assignment really was: John wrote his whole book so people would meet Christ and have life in his name (20:31). A group that teaches its chapter so the class meets Jesus in it is doing the very thing John did, and the very thing Jesus did on this night. You got to teach your friends the last words of Jesus to his friends.

One honest line for your reflection: where did your chapter land the class on Jesus, and what was hardest about teaching it?
