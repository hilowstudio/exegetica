# Daily page, Unit 25 Lesson 7: Dividing his clothes (John 19:23-24)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "They divided my garments among them, and for my clothing they cast lots." (John 19:24, quoting Psalm 22, ESV)

Where does life feel random or out of control to me right now? Write one line.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 19:23-24** (out loud if you can), and read **Psalm 22:18** alongside it.

Where we are: while Jesus hangs on the cross, the soldiers below him split up his clothes.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 23 and 24, what do the soldiers do with Jesus' clothes, and what do they do with the seamless tunic? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 24, what does John say this fulfilled? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Read Psalm 22:18 next to verse 24. What did the psalm, written about a thousand years earlier, say would happen? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* the soldiers are bored guards gambling for a dead man's shirt, with no idea they are acting out a psalm. Hold the question of what it tells you that even their dice landed on prophecy.

## 4. The one thing from today

Nothing at the cross was an accident, right down to a gambling game over clothes. Write the one thing this makes you want to trust God with.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Where do you need to trust that God is in control of the parts of your life that feel random or out of your hands? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
