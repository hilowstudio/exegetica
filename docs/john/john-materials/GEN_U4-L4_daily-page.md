# Daily page, Unit 4 Lesson 4: Light, darkness, and the verdict (John 3:18-21)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Whoever does what is true comes to the light." (John 3:21, ESV)

Coming to the light means being fully seen. What in you would you rather keep in the dark? You do not have to share it.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 3:18-21** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the light has come into the world, and these verses say what happens when it does. This is the unit's last word.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Verse 18 gives a verdict. Read it. Is it about the future or about right now? Write both halves.

______________________________________________________________

Verse 19 says what "the judgment" actually is. In the text's own words, what is it?

______________________________________________________________

Verses 20-21: what is the difference between the person who stays away from the light and the person who comes to it?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* remember Nicodemus came "by night" (3:2)? Read John 7:50-51 and John 19:39, where he speaks up for Jesus and later helps bury him in the open with about seventy-five pounds of spices. He is edging out of the dark, slowly, over years. If the new birth can look like that instead of one dramatic moment, what does that mean for someone who feels only half-sure? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice that the light came to save, and that even coming to it is something God works in a person.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The light came to save you, and coming to it is safe. What is one honest thing you would bring into the light if you knew you were fully safe there? Write honestly.
