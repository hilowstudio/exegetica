# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 3: Kept, and not one lost (John 17:9-13)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Holy Father, keep them in your name, which you have given me." (John 17:11, ESV)

When I think about staying a Christian long-term, does it feel like it is up to me? How does that feel? Honesty about the pressure is welcome.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:9-13** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: still listening in on the Son praying for his friends. He is about to leave them, and he asks the Father to keep them safe.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 9, who does Jesus say he is praying for right now?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 11, what does Jesus ask the "Holy Father" to do for his people? Write the word he uses.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 12, how many were lost, and who was the exception?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* verse 12 calls Judas "the son of destruction" and says his fall "fulfilled the Scripture." Hold the question of whether being near Jesus is the same thing as belonging to him.

## 4. The one thing from today

Jesus asks the Father to KEEP his people, because he is leaving. Whose job is it to keep a Christian from falling away, according to verse 11? Write what you found.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Your security rests on God's grip on you, not your grip on him. What would change about your week if you really believed that? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
