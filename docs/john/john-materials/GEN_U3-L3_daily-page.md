# Daily page, Unit 3 Lesson 3: Destroy this temple (John 2:18-22)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up." (John 2:19, ESV)

Where do you go, or what do you do, to feel close to God? What if the answer were a person?

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 2:18-22** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the leaders demand proof of Jesus' right to do this, and he hands them the deepest sign in the book, folded into a riddle. This is the peak of the whole unit, so we slow way down.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 18 the leaders demand a sign. What does Jesus say in verse 19? What do they think he means (verse 20)?

______________________________________________________________

John steps in and tells you plainly what Jesus actually meant. Read verse 21. What is "this temple"? Write John's own words.

______________________________________________________________

Verse 22 says the disciples understood this only after something happened. After what? And what did they then believe?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* John frames the whole chapter with resurrection language: the wedding is "on the third day" (2:1) and the temple riddle promises "in three days I will raise it up" (2:19). What might John be hinting at by bracketing chapter 2 that way? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice where Jesus says the real meeting place with God now stands.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Verse 22 says the disciples did not understand this saying until after Jesus rose. They followed him for years with it unexplained. If following Jesus can mean living with some questions still open, how does that land on you right now? Write honestly.
