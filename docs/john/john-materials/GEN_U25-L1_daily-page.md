## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 19:1-3** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: after the trial, Pilate has Jesus flogged, and the soldiers turn his kingship into a cruel joke. This is holy, sober ground, and we walk it slowly.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verses 2 and 3, what do the soldiers put on Jesus, and what do they say to him? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Look closely at the crown in verse 2. What is it made of? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 3, what do the soldiers do to Jesus while they mock him? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* thorns first show up in Genesis 3, when the ground is cursed after sin enters the world. Hold the question of why the King is crowned with the very thing that stands for the curse.

## 4. The one thing from today

This is a real body, real blood, real pain, and Jesus took it for you. Write the one thing it stirs in you to see the King treated like this.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

He did not have to become this touchable, this hurtable, and he chose it out of love. How do you respond to a King who did that? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.
