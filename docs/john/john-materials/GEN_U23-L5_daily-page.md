# Daily page, Unit 23 Lesson 5: Sanctify them in the truth (John 17:17-19)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth." (John 17:17, ESV)

When I try to be a better person on my own strength, how is it going? Be honest about the struggle.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 17:17-19** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: still listening in on the Son praying for his friends. He asks the Father to make them holy, and he tells him how.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 17, what does Jesus ask the Father to do for his people?

______________________________________________________________

Still in verse 17, HOW does Jesus say they will be made holy? Write the tool.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 19, what does Jesus do "for their sake"?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* put verse 17 and verse 19 side by side. Jesus sets HIMSELF apart, for the cross, so his people can be made holy. Hold the question of where the power for a holy life comes from, and where it does not.

## 4. The one thing from today

"Sanctify" means to set apart and make holy. Look hard at verse 17: is Jesus telling them to clean themselves up by trying harder, or asking the Father to do something in them? Write what you see.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

God grows you through his word, on the ground of what Jesus did. What would change if you saw Bible reading as God shaping you instead of a chore? Write honestly. Belief is never scored.
