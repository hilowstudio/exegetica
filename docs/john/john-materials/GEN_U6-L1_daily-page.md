# Daily page, Unit 6 Lesson 1: Jacob's well and living water (John 4:1-15)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Jesus, wearied as he was from his journey, was sitting beside the well." (John 4:6, ESV)

What am I actually thirsty for right now? Write the honest thirst, even if you are not sure anyone is listening.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 4:1-15** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus is tired and thirsty at a well at noon, and a Samaritan woman comes to draw water. Jews and Samaritans did not mix.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Look at verse 9. Why is the woman surprised Jesus even talks to her?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 13 and 14 side by side. Write the difference between the water in the well and the water Jesus gives. What happens to the person who drinks Jesus' water?

______________________________________________________________

Verse 4 says Jesus "had to pass through Samaria," though most Jews went around it. Write the phrase.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* Jesus calls it "living water." If you want to chase it, turn to John 7:39, where John later tells you what the living water is, and read Jeremiah 2:13, where God calls himself "the fountain of living waters." Hold the question. We work it in class.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice how far Jesus went, and who he crossed every line to find.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The woman kept coming back to a well that never satisfied her for long. What is one "well" you keep going back to, and what would it look like to bring that thirst to Jesus? Write honestly.
