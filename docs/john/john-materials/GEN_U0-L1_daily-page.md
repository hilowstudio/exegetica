## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 20:30-31** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the last lines of the book, where John stops and tells you, out loud, why he wrote the whole thing. We start at his purpose, then spend the year watching him prove it.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Find the Gospel of John in your Bible. It is the fourth book of the New Testament. Write your page number here so you can find it fast all year: ____________

John tells you exactly why he wrote. In his own words, what are the two things he wants to happen inside a person who reads this book?
