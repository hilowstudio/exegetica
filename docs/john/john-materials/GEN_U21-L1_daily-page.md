## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "It is to your advantage that I go away." (John 16:7, ESV)

When has someone told me a hard truth I needed to hear? Was it kind or cruel? Write it straight.

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 16:1-7** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: still the same night, Jesus gets honest about what following him will cost, and why his leaving is actually good.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 1, why does Jesus say he is telling them these hard things ahead of time?

______________________________________________________________

In verse 7, why does Jesus say his going away is to their advantage? What does his going make possible?

## 4. The one thing from today

In one sentence, why does Jesus say it is good that he is leaving?

## 5. Respond
