# Daily page, Unit 7 Lesson 1: The welcome that wanted a show (John 4:43-48)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 7 minutes)

> "Unless you see signs and wonders you will not believe." (John 4:48, ESV)

What kind of proof do I want from God before I will trust him? Write the proof you wish you had. Be honest about it.

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 4:43-48** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: Jesus comes home to Galilee, and a desperate father from Capernaum comes looking for him.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

Look at verse 45. Why do the Galileans welcome Jesus? What had they seen him do?

______________________________________________________________

Read verses 46 and 47. Who comes to Jesus, and what does he ask? Write his job and what is happening to his son.

______________________________________________________________

In verse 48 Jesus answers, "Unless you see signs and wonders you will not believe." Who is he answering, the dad alone or the whole crowd around them?

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* look back at John 2:23-25, where Jesus "did not entrust himself" to people who only believed because of signs. How does that help you read the welcome he gets in 4:45? Hold it.

## 4. The one thing from today

In your own words. Notice what this crowd wants from Jesus, and what Jesus asks of them instead.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

The crowd wanted proof before they would trust Jesus. Where do you do the same thing? What would it look like to trust him without the proof first? Write honestly.
