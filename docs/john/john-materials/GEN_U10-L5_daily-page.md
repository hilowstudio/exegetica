## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* this is the same Nicodemus who came to Jesus at night in John 3. Read John 3:1-2, then this scene, then John 19:39, where he helps bury Jesus. Hold the question of what his three appearances show about how a person can move toward the light over time.

## 4. The one thing from today
