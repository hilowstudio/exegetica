## 5. Respond

The Lord who did all those signs spends his last night kneeling with a towel, washing his friends' feet, and John says he loved them to the end. What would it mean to be loved like that by a friend who knew he was about to die? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe.

Write it down. Our chapter is ____________. My role is ____________. Our format is ____________ (video, infographic, first-person monologue, documentary, art with commentary, or a written piece).

Four things to keep in front of you: land on Jesus and say what he asks of the room; use the actual text and read it out loud; reach the whole room, a low floor and a high ceiling; read it as a dying man's care for scared friends. Your full handout is the reading guide.
