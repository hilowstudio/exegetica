# Daily page, Unit 27 Lesson 1: Back to the boat (John 21:1-3)

> Keep this page next to you while we work. Write on it. It is yours to keep, and it is today's Reading-Journal page.

## 1. Quiet time (the first 6 minutes)

> "Simon Peter said to them, 'I am going fishing.' They said to him, 'We will go with you.' They went out and got into the boat, but that night they caught nothing." (John 21:3, ESV)

When I've messed up, where do I drift back to? Write one line.

______________________________________________________________

## 2. Hear the Word

**Read: John 21:1-3** (out loud if you can).

Where we are: the last chapter. Some of the disciples are back home in Galilee, and Peter decides to go fishing.

## 3. Work the text (find it yourself, in class)

In verse 3, what does Peter decide to do, and how does the night of fishing go? Write it.

______________________________________________________________

Fishing was Peter's old job, the life Jesus first called him away from. Write down what he goes back to here, and what the boat holds by morning.

______________________________________________________________

*Go deeper (optional):* hold verse 3 next to John 15:5, "apart from me you can do nothing." Sit with what an empty net all night might be picturing here. Don't answer it yet; bring it to class.

## 4. The one thing from today

Peter drifted back to the old life, and the net came up empty. Write the one thing you want to remember about where the old life leads.

______________________________________________________________

## 5. Respond

Peter drifted back to the old life, and it came up empty. Where have you drifted, and is it coming up empty? Write honestly. No one grades what you believe, and there is nothing here to earn.
