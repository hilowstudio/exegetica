# Study guide: Semester 1 Final, The Book of Signs (John 1-12)

> **Use this on the review day (January 8) and to study at home.** It tells you exactly what's on the final, so there are no surprises.
> The final is January 11-14 and is worth 15% of your semester grade. All Scripture ESV.

## What the final covers

The whole first half of John, chapters 1 to 12, the Book of Signs. This is the half where Jesus reveals who he is to the world through seven signs and long conversations, ending on "the hour has come." The one question the whole half asks is John's reason for writing: "that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God" (20:31). Keep that verse in front of you. Almost every answer on the final finishes the sentence "this shows Jesus is ___."

## The five parts of the exam

1. **Memory**: write the nine anchors from memory.
2. **The seven signs**: match each to its reference and say what it reveals about Jesus.
3. **The "I am" sayings**: list them, and explain the deity claim of 8:58.
4. **Short answer**: the heart of each major movement.
5. **Essay**: one christotelic essay (you choose from three). You're graded on understanding, not belief.

## Part 1: the nine memory anchors

Know these cold, in the ESV. Grade is for meaning and main wording.

1. **20:31**: why John wrote the book.
2. **1:14**: the Word became flesh.
3. **1:12**: the right to become children of God.
4. **3:16**: God so loved the world.
5. **4:24**: worship in spirit and truth.
6. **6:35**: I am the bread of life.
7. **8:12**: I am the light of the world.
8. **10:11**: the good shepherd lays down his life.
9. **11:25**: I am the resurrection and the life.

## Part 2: the seven signs (know the reference and what each reveals)

A sign is a window. You look at what Jesus does and see who he is (2:11; 20:30-31).

| Sign | Reference | What it reveals about Jesus |
|---|---|---|
| Water into wine | 2:1-11 | reveals his glory; the Bridegroom who brings the promised abundance |
| The official's son | 4:46-54 | his word gives life across distance |
| The invalid at Bethesda | 5:1-9 | the Son gives life and works as the Father |
| The feeding of the five thousand | 6:1-14 | the true bread from heaven |
| Walking on the water | 6:16-21 | the divine "I am" who treads the sea |
| The man born blind | 9:1-7 | the light who gives sight and exposes blindness |
| The raising of Lazarus | 11:1-44 | the resurrection and the life |

## Part 3: the "I am" sayings in the Book of Signs

Five predicated sayings: **bread (6:35)**, **light (8:12)**, **the door (10:7)**, **the good shepherd (10:11)**, **the resurrection and the life (11:25)**. Each names what Jesus is *for* people. (The way and the vine come next semester.)

One absolute "I am": **"before Abraham was, I am" (8:58)**. It echoes the divine name God gave Moses at the burning bush (Exodus 3:14). It's a claim to be God, which is why the crowd picked up stones (8:59).

## Part 4: the heart of each movement (be ready to answer in a sentence or two)

- **Prologue (ch 1):** the Word is God (1:1) and became flesh (1:14). The incarnation.
- **What a sign is:** a miracle that points past itself to reveal Jesus and call for belief (20:30-31).
- **The new birth (ch 3):** you must be born again, a work of the Spirit; the Son is lifted up like the bronze serpent so whoever looks lives (3:14-16).
- **The well (ch 4):** living water, and true worship "in spirit and truth" because "God is spirit" (4:24).
- **The Son's authority (ch 5):** the Son works as the Father, gives life and judges; they wanted to kill him for "making himself equal with God" (5:18).
- **The man born blind (ch 9):** as the man's sight grows, the seeing leaders go blind; the Light gives sight and judges (9:39).
- **The Good Shepherd (ch 10):** he lays down his life for the sheep, of his own accord (10:11, 10:18).
- **The hour (ch 12):** "the hour has come" is the time of his death and glory; the grain of wheat must die to bear fruit (12:24); he is glorified by being lifted up.

## Part 5: the essay (prepare all three, you'll pick one)

**E1.** Trace at least three signs and show what each reveals about Jesus and how the set builds toward 20:31.
**E2.** Choose three "I am" sayings and explain each; then explain what 8:58 adds.
**E3.** Explain what "the hour" means and how chapter 12 turns the Gospel toward the cross.

You're graded on whether you *see* what John is showing, never on whether you personally believe it. Explain John's claim clearly and you've earned the marks.

## How to study this week

- **Say the nine verses out loud** to a partner, then write them cold. That's easy points.
- **Make a signs chart** from memory: sign, reference, what it reveals. Cover the third column and quiz yourself.
- **Practice one essay out loud.** If you can explain three signs pointing at who Jesus is, you can write E1.
- **Keep asking "so who is Jesus here?"** at every chapter. That's the whole exam in one question.
