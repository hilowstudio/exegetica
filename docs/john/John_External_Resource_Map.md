# External Resource Map
## Vetted teaching resources for the John course, catalogued for just-in-time use

Surveyed 2026-09-04 from the 14 links provided. This is a catalog of what exists in each resource so we know which to pull when we build a given unit. Everything here is used by **derivation and citation**, never copied: we write original material and cite the source (title, presenter, publisher, URL). Access notes matter, since some content sits behind a login.

Three of the surveyed pages produced attribution errors on a first automated pass (a fake presenter name on the Streetlights "I AM" course, "Bob Marley" on the Doriani PDF, and a phantom theme-video list on BibleProject). All three were verified against raw scrapes and discarded. Nothing false is carried below.

### Legend: the three-tier room

Every resource is tagged for which student it best serves:
- **(S)** the skeptic / never-opened-a-Bible student: accessible, evidence-first, low jargon.
- **(B)** background and context: history, authorship, setting, structure.
- **(D)** the disciple / advanced: doctrinal and devotional depth.

## Catalog

### A. Whole-book orientation and structure

1. **Third Millennium — "The Gospels," Lesson 5: John** · thirdmill.org/seminary/lesson.asp/vid/110 · *(free, full manuscript, no login)* · **(B)(S)**
   A complete 124-min survey lesson of all of John with a downloadable manuscript and study guide. Gives authorship/date/audience/purpose, a fourfold book outline (Introduction / Public Ministry / Private Ministry / Conclusion), and a clean four-part frame for the Upper Room: **Service / Comfort / Preparation / Prayer**. The most reusable free John-specific source we have. *(Note: the TGC "Gospels: Summary & Theology" page below is the same Thirdmill course via TGC's portal, so cite once.)*

2. **BibleProject — Book of John guide** · bibleproject.com/guides/book-of-john/ · *(free, no login)* · **(S)(B)**
   Two animated overview videos (John 1-12, 8:46; John 13-21, 8:32; 17 min total) plus a prose walkthrough. Distinctive lens: the signs cycle organized around **four Jewish institutions (chs. 2-4) and four feasts (chs. 5-10, Sabbath/Passover/Tabernacles/Hanukkah)**, the Logos prologue tied to Genesis 1 and Exodus 33-34, and the two sets of seven "I AM" sayings. The best low-friction on-ramp for the student who has never opened a Bible. Downloadable poster (check reuse terms before distributing).

3. **TGC — "Introduction to John"** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/john/ · *(free; text public, videos embedded)* · **(B)**
   A teacher-prep background packet (ESV Study Bible text + a Carson video, two BibleProject videos, a 54-min Mark Dever whole-book sermon). Carries a **16-theme "big ideas of John" list with verse lists** and the book's full outline. Teacher-facing, not student-ready. Recommends Keener's commentary for our own hard-passage prep.

4. **TGC — "The Gospels: Summary & Theology," John lesson** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/gospels-summary-theology/ · *(free)* · **(B)(D)**
   Thirdmill content again (see #1). Its value here is a **19-question checklist** for the John lesson (authorship, date, place, audience, purpose, Synoptics-vs-John, Son of God, Word of God, "I am," signs vs. miracles, belief, eternal life, Farewell Discourse, High Priestly Prayer). Use the question list as a coverage checklist for our own Unit 0.

### B. Teacher-prep depth on John's content

5. **TGC — "Life & Teachings of Christ" (Dan Doriani, Covenant Seminary), John unit = Lectures 36-38** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/life-teachings-of-christ/ · *(free audio + study-guide PDFs)* · **(D)(B)**
   The richest teacher-prep for John content. Lecture 36 builds an **eyewitness-historicity case** (a catalog of verifiable customs, geography, and topography in John) that is gold for a "how do we know John was there" apologetics lesson. Lecture 37 gives an "Overview of Signs" framework; Lecture 38 covers the "I am" sayings, Lazarus, and the Book of Glory through Thomas. 28.8h whole course; only the 3 John lectures (~135 min) are our target.

6. **TGC — "Knowing the Bible: John" (Justin Buzzard, Crossway)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/knowing-bible-john/ · *(free, full text public)* · **(D)(B)**
   A 12-week written study. Two uses: its **passage segmentation** is a sound chaptering of John, and its **four-lens template** (Gospel Glimpses / Whole-Bible Connections / Theological Soundings / Personal Implications) is a strong model to imitate for our own lesson structure. Written for believing small groups, so weak on the (S) tier.

7. **TGC — "Parables and Miracles" (Vern Poythress, WTS)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/parables-and-miracles/ · *(free audio, 15.3h)* · **(D, teacher only)**
   Graduate-level method, not John content. Mine the "Miracle Stories" segment for how a miracle functions as a revelation-bearing **sign** (not spectacle), which is exactly John's own lens (sēmeia). Teacher framing for the signs units.

### C. The "I AM" thread (a Christological through-line we can run across the year)

8. **Ligonier — "Knowing Christ: The I AM Sayings" (R.C. Sproul)** · learn.ligonier.org/series/knowing-christ · *(lesson 1 free; 2-8 login-gated)* · **(D)(S)**
   8 messages on the "I AM" sayings (John 6, 8:12, 10:7, 10:11, 11, 14:6, 15:1, 8:58). Confessional, deity-of-Christ emphasis. We get titles and structure free; content is mostly behind a login.

9. **TGC — "The 'I AM' Statements of Jesus" (Streetlights Bible)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/the-i-am-statements-of-jesus/ · *(free, full text public)* · **(S)(D)**
   8 short devotional essays, testimony-driven, plain modern language, each ending in a direct existential question. The strongest single model for **skeptic-facing framing** at the "I AM" passages (John 6, 8, 10, 11, 14, 15).

### D. Book of Glory / Passion Week

10. **TGC — "The Final Days of Jesus" (Köstenberger, Taylor, Stewart)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/final-days-jesus/ · *(free; book sold separately)* · **(S)(B)**
    A day-by-day Passion/Resurrection **harmony** (Palm Sunday through Easter), cross-referenced across all four Gospels, with John's unique material called out (Upper Room 13-17, the Annas/Pilate trial, the crucifixion details in 19, the Peter-and-John foot race and Mary in the garden in 20). The natural spine for our Book of Glory unit and its Synoptic-harmony work.

11. **Ligonier — "Lessons from the Upper Room" (Sinclair Ferguson)** · learn.ligonier.org/series/lessons-from-the-upper-room · *(lesson 1 free; 2-12 login-gated)* · **(D)(B)**
    A dedicated 12-message exposition of exactly **John 13-17**, our densest unit. Highest-value structural/pacing guide for the Upper Room, though content is mostly paywalled so we borrow its segmentation, not its exposition. Framing hook: "five wonderful hours with the Master."

### E. Skeptic-tier apologetics and background

12. **TGC — "Evidence for the Reliability of the Gospels" (Tyndale House)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/evidence-for-the-reliability-of-the-gospels/ · *(free; page is thin, mostly a reading list)* · **(S)(B)**
    Four sections: eyewitness testimony, manuscript evidence, Talmud evidence, resurrection evidence. General-Gospel apologetics, not John-specific. The video content was not scrape-accessible; the page is a curated reading list (Bauckham, Bock, Instone-Brewer, Habermas, Craig) we build our own material from.

13. **TGC — "Introduction to the Gospels" (Craig Blomberg, ODBU)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/introduction-to-the-gospels/ · *(free audio, 2.7h)* · **(B)(S)**
    NT political/religious background, textual reliability, canon, hermeneutics; one short combined Luke+John lecture. Use the background and reliability parts for a "can we trust the Gospels" lead-in; the John content itself is too compressed.

14. **TGC — "Life of Christ" (Craig Blomberg, ODBU)** · thegospelcoalition.org/course/life-of-christ/ · *(free audio, 2.6h)* · **(B)(S)**
    A short chronological survey of Jesus' life across the Gospels. Lesson 5 explicitly compares Luke's and John's itineraries, useful right before students open John and ask why it differs from the Synoptics.

## Integration points (as built, 2026-09-06)

Where each resource actually lands in the built course, after a Theologian consult on native versus bolted-on. **Governing principle:** bring in outside media only where it carries framing the text asks for at that moment, and never where it would do the students' own meeting with the text for them. On a survey, bridge, or orientation day a video can carry the frame the teacher would otherwise lecture, which serves the talk budget. In a verse-by-verse Work block the students' discovery is the point, so no video enters it. Everything else is teacher preparation, which exists to make the Land truer. The screen points; the pulpit lands Christ.

### Available but not integrated this pass

- **Streetlights "I AM" essays (#9):** a legitimate model for deriving an (S)-facing Respond question at the "I am" plant lessons, cited and never copied. Left for later by choice (the finished Respond prompts were not reopened). If used, ground the question in the absolute "I am" of 8:58 and keep it receiving Christ by faith, never "what will you decide or do" (the assessment model refuses to grade belief; the wording should match that refusal).
- **Sproul "I AM" series (#8) and Ferguson's Upper Room series (#11):** now integrated at transcript depth (see the Teacher-prep table above). The teacher provided full transcripts and study guides on 2026-09-06 (in `sproul-transcripts/` and `ferguson-transcripts/`), so both moved from structure-only to message-specific teacher-prep at the "I am" plants and the Farewell units. Until 2026-11-12, the transcripts informed only the teacher's own Land voice and were never reproduced in student-facing material. On 2026-11-12 Ligonier approved the teacher's request to share them non-commercially with his 48 sophomores. The lessons now sit on the Sophomore Bible: Gospel of John class shelf for students, each ending with a Ligonier credit line. The files themselves still stay out of git and the app image; the shelf keeps its own encrypted copy.
- **TGC "Evidence for the Reliability of the Gospels" (#12):** best left a bibliography for the teacher's own apologetics prep; no native home in the room. Naming a resource unused is a faithful outcome, not a failure of the map.

## Access and honesty notes

- **Free and fully open:** Thirdmill L5 (#1), BibleProject (#2), and all TGC pages (#3-#7, #9, #10, #12-#14). Ligonier (#8, #11) is lesson-1-free and the rest login-gated on the site; the teacher owns full transcripts and study guides for both John series (provided 2026-09-06), so we now draw on their content for teacher-prep, derive and cite, never reproduced to students.
- **Same source via two doors:** #1 and #4 are both the Thirdmill Gospels course. Cite once.
- **Teacher-prep only (not classroom-ready):** Poythress (#7), most of Doriani (#5), Buzzard's devotional lenses (#6).
- **Copyright:** derive and cite; do not reproduce page text, study-guide PDFs, or video transcripts at length. The strongest single picks are Thirdmill L5 and BibleProject (free, John-specific, tier-flexible), the Doriani John lectures (teacher depth), and the Final Days harmony (Passion spine).
