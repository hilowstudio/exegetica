**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will describe the steps of the official's faith (4:47, 49, 50, 53).
2. Students will explain what "your son will live" shows about who Jesus is.
3. Students will explain the "second sign" note (4:54) and its tie to Cana (2:11).
4. Students will explain what "he himself believed, and all his household" adds (4:53).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): a dad trusts one sentence from Jesus and walks home on it, before he sees anything.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): faith that grows is normal. The dad believes, then believes again.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the word that does what it says (Psalm 33:9; Isaiah 55:11); the Cana-to-Cana bracket; the household-faith pattern (Acts 16:31).

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "Go; your son will live" (John 4:50).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Is there something Jesus said that I'd have to trust before I could see it?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write what you'd need to believe him for, even if you're not there yet.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 4:49-54 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 4, verses 49 through 54.
- One-sentence setup: last class the dad begged Jesus to come heal his son, and today Jesus answers with one sentence.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 49 the dad asks again. What exactly does he still want Jesus to do?
   - Cold-call check: "Sir, come down before my child dies." He still wants Jesus to walk to Capernaum in person.
2. **Entry.** Have you ever had to trust something someone told you before you could check it? How did that feel?
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 50. Jesus stays where he is and gives him one sentence: "Go; your son will live." What does the dad do about it? Remember, he hasn't seen anything yet.
   - Guide them: "The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him and went on his way." He asked Jesus to come, and he leaves without him, trusting the word alone. That's the turn of the whole story.
4. **Core.** Look at verse 50 and verse 53 together. Verse 50 says he "believed the word." Verse 53 says "he himself believed, and all his household." Why does John show the dad believing two different times?
   - Land it: his faith grows. First he trusts the promise. Then the proof comes at the exact hour, and his faith deepens and spreads to his whole house. He believed, and then he believed again.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** In verse 54 John calls this "the second sign." The first sign was also at Cana (John 2, water into wine). Why might John frame these two signs as a matched pair? And what does a healing by a bare word tell you about Jesus (look at Psalm 33:9, "he spoke, and it came to be")?
   - Draw out: both signs happen at Cana, opening and closing this stretch of the book. The word that made the world (John 1:1-3) is the word that healed the boy. Jesus speaks, and it's done.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In verses 52 and 53, what does the dad discover about the timing, and what does he do with it?
   - The boy got better at the exact hour Jesus spoke. The dad matches it up and believes.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Derive from the second half of sermon 05.

- Start with the distance. The boy is fifteen miles off, and Jesus never goes near him. A word, and the fever breaks. The sermon puts it plainly: the gap from Cana to Capernaum stands for the gap from earth to the throne where Christ sits now. He only has to will a thing, and it's done here for you.
- Name the growing faith. The dad shows the normal shape of following Jesus. You trust him for something, you see him come through, and your trust goes deeper. He believed, and then he believed again. Tell the student who's worried a wobble means it was never real: faith grows.
- Put the grace first. This official had no claim on Jesus. He didn't clean himself up first. He asked, and Jesus healed. And the healing reached a whole household through one man's faith (4:53), the way grace still reaches a house through one person (Acts 16:31).
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus' word carries God's own life-giving power across any distance, and it's worth taking him at his word.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus healed a boy fifteen miles away with one sentence, and the dad trusted it the whole way home. What's one thing Jesus says that you'd have to trust before you could see it work?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep reviewing your five memory verses.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. No new memory verse this unit.
- Sets up Unit 8: Jesus goes back to Jerusalem and heals a man who'd been sick for thirty-eight years, and a fight breaks out over the Sabbath.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Build on sermon 05's second half: the word that heals at a distance, no theatrics, and "he believed, and then he believed again." The Father-first spine matters here: Jesus serves the Father's will, and our good is wrapped inside that. Use it to speak to the student whose prayer got a no or a not-yet, without promising every prayer for healing gets this answer. The sign reveals who Jesus is.
- Memory: review-only unit. Run a light cumulative pass on the five anchors, and let 20:31 ("that by believing you may have life") sit next to this sign, since it's the engine of it. Offer 4:50 as an optional stretch, off the graded line. Keep 4:48 off the stretch list.
- Guard the big pitfall: the dad's faith did not power the miracle. Christ's word did. Locate the power in Jesus, never in the size of anyone's belief.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
