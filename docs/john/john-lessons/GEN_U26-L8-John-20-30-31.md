**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will recite John 20:31 and state its two aims (believe; have life).
2. Students will explain that John selected the signs to bring the reader to faith (20:30-31).
3. Students will explain 20:31 as the purpose of the whole Gospel and the whole course.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): John wrote this book to help you believe.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the point of everything in John is belief that leads to life.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the purpose statement as the key to reading the whole Gospel; the anchor coming home.

---

**Talk budget:** short day, single focus. This is the aim of the whole book and the anchor coming home. Give it the weight of a homecoming, and keep it warm.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (5 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room fast. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "so that you may believe... and that by believing you may have life" (John 20:31).
- Students write one line in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "We started the year with this verse. Where am I with it now?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3 min)

- Have one student read John 20:30-31 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 20, verses 30 and 31.
- One-sentence setup: right after Thomas worships the risen Lord, John steps back and tells you why he wrote all of this.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 18 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Single focus: the aim of the whole book, and the anchor coming home. Ask these.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 30, what does John say about the signs Jesus did? Did he write down all of them?
   - Cold-call check: Jesus did many other signs that are not written in this book. John selected the ones he included.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 31. John tells you exactly why he chose and wrote these particular signs. What are the two things he wants to happen in you?
   - Guide them: first, that you would BELIEVE that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. Second, that by believing, you would HAVE LIFE in his name. Believe, and have life. That's the whole reason the book exists. Everything John included, every sign, every "I am," every conversation, was picked to bring you to faith and to life.
3. **The homecoming.** You have carried this exact verse since the very first day of this course. And here it is, in its home, at the very end of John's own account, right after Thomas fell down and worshiped the risen Jesus. Why do you think John put his purpose statement HERE, at this exact spot?
   - Land it: because this is the response he's been aiming at the whole time. The book ends with a doubter worshiping Jesus as God, and then John says, "that's why I wrote this, so you'd do the same, believe and live." The whole Gospel has been walking you to this moment. The verse you started the year with has been the point all along.
4. **Core.** John says the goal is "life in his name." Look back at what you've learned this year: eternal life is knowing God (17:3), it's the bread (6:35), the light (8:12), the resurrection (11:25), the way (14:6). How does 20:31 gather all of that up?
   - Draw out: all of it is "life in his name." Every sign and saying was showing you the one person who gives that life, Jesus. Believe he's the Christ, the Son of God, and you have that life. The whole book, and the whole year, points to him and this one offer: believe, and live.
5. **Memory verse, coming home.** Have students say John 20:31 together, then in pairs. This one they've held all year; today they land it in its home. Ask a few to say where they were with it in September and where they are now.

Circulate. Keep it warm and reflective.

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-6 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and close the unit. Framed fresh.

- Land the homecoming. On the first day of this class, we learned one verse: John 20:31. It's been in your pocket all year. And today, we finally stand in the place it lives, the very end of John's account of Jesus, right after a doubter looked at the risen Lord and said "my God." John wrote every word so that you would do the same.
- Say the two words. Believe, and have life. That's it. That's what all of this has been for. Not to make you religious, not to fill your head with facts. So that you would believe Jesus is the Son of God, and have real, forever life in him. Every sign we studied was a signpost pointing to him.
- Make the invitation, gently. So the question John has been asking you since page one, he asks you plainly now: do you believe? Not "can you pass the quiz," but "have you come to trust him?" The whole book was written to bring you here. And the offer is open: believe, and live.
- Christ connection to say out loud: John wrote everything so you would believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and have life in his name. That's the point of all of it.

## Move 5: Respond (3-4 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt on the board: "John wrote the whole book so you would believe and have life. We started the year with this verse. Where are you with it today? Write honestly."
- Give it quiet, and let it be reflective. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 21:1-14 once before next class. There's one more chapter, one more breakfast on a beach.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 20:31 stays in the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up Unit 27: the final chapter, where Jesus restores Peter and asks, "do you love me?"

## Teacher notes

- Day-type note: Thursday, 36 minutes, single-focus. This is the aim of the whole Gospel AND the anchor coming home. Give it the weight of a homecoming.
- MEMORY: this is a REVIEW-AND-LAND, not a re-plant. 20:31 is the course's very first anchor (Unit 0, Day 1), and this is its home text. Teach it as the aim of the whole book arriving where John wrote it, right after Thomas's confession. Do not treat it as new.
- Land the two aims: believe (that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God) and have life (in his name). Gather the year's "life" thread (17:3, 6:35, 8:12, 11:25, 14:6) into "life in his name."
- Keep the invitation warm and open; belief is invitational, never coerced. This is a natural moment to ask the room, gently, where they are with the verse now versus September.
- Note this closes John's own account (chapter 21 is an epilogue). Derive the frame from sermon 39; cite it, don't read it aloud.
