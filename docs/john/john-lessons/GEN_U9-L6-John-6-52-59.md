# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 9 Lesson 6 of 7: eat the flesh, drink the blood (John 6:52-59)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U9 Bread of Life |
| Lesson | 6 of 7 |
| Date / Day type | Tue Nov 3 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 6:52-59 (ESV) |
| Big idea | Jesus says feeding on him is how you live, and the eating means trusting him, taking all of him into yourself by faith. |
| Christ connection | Jesus gives himself as true food, and the one who feeds on him by faith abides in him and lives forever. |
| Memory verse | John 6:35 was planted at L4. Review it, do not re-plant. Keep the five earlier anchors sharp too. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain why the crowd is confused by "eat my flesh" (6:52).
2. Students will explain what "eat my flesh and drink my blood" means, reading it with 6:35, 40, and 47.
3. Students will explain how the Lord's Supper pictures this feeding.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus says something shocking, and it needs unpacking, not panic.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): "eating" Jesus is trusting him all the way, taking him in like food.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the by-faith reading defended from the parallel verses; the Lord's Supper as the sign of it.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "Whoever feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood abides in me, and I in him" (John 6:56).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What would it mean to have Jesus that close, like food inside me?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write your honest reaction to a teaching that sounds strange.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 6:52-59 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 6, verses 52 through 59.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus says something that sounds shocking, and the crowd takes it the wrong way.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 52, how does the crowd react to Jesus talking about eating his flesh? What question do they ask?
   - Cold-call check: they're disgusted and confused. "How can this man give us his flesh to eat?" They're hearing it literally.
2. **Entry.** When has something Jesus (or the Bible) says sounded weird to you at first, before it made sense?
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Jesus isn't asking for cannibalism. Look back at verse 35, where "coming" and "believing" satisfy hunger and thirst. Now read verse 54 next to verse 47. Verse 47 says "whoever believes has eternal life." Verse 54 says "whoever feeds on my flesh has eternal life." Work this: what is "eating his flesh" the same as?
   - Guide the groups. Draw out: verse 47 and verse 54 promise the exact same thing (eternal life, raised at the last day). One says "believes," the other says "feeds on my flesh." So eating his flesh means believing in him, taking all of Jesus into yourself the way food becomes part of you. It's total trust. The crowd's mistake was hearing it as a literal meal.
4. **Core.** Verse 56 says the one who feeds on Jesus "abides in me, and I in him." What kind of closeness is that? How is it more than just agreeing Jesus existed?
   - Land it: it's union. Jesus in you and you in him, the way food you eat becomes part of your body. Faith isn't distant admiration. It's taking Christ in and living off him.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** We use this flesh-and-blood language at the Lord's Supper (communion). If "eating" Jesus means trusting him, how does the Supper picture that with real bread and cup?
   - Draw out: the Supper is a sign God gives us of this feeding. Every time you take the bread and cup, you're picturing and receiving Christ by faith. The eating in John 6 is faith, and the Supper is the meal that shows it. You don't have to take communion to be saved (verse 47 says believing is enough), and the Supper is the beautiful sign of what faith does.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what does "eat my flesh and drink my blood" mean?
   - It means trust Jesus fully, take all of him into yourself by faith, and live off him.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. This is sermon 08's deepest point.

- Clear the confusion first. Jesus isn't talking about a literal meal of his body. He tells us so next class: "the flesh is no help at all" (6:63). He's talking about taking him in by faith.
- Then press how total it is. You don't admire food from across the room. You take it in and it becomes part of you. That's the faith Jesus asks for. Not a nod, not a fan club, but taking all of Christ into yourself and living off him.
- Name the closeness. Verse 56: he abides in you and you in him. The God of the universe, that close, feeding your soul from the inside. That's what the cross bought.
- Point to the Table. When you take communion, you're doing in a picture what faith does all the time, receiving Christ, feeding on him. The bread and cup preach this verse.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus gives himself as true food, and the one who takes him in by faith is joined to him and lives forever.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus wants to be taken in, not admired from a distance. What would it look like to feed on him this week instead of just knowing about him?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 6:35. Read John 6:60-71 once before Wednesday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L7: the teaching is too hard for the crowd, and most of them walk away.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Carry the by-faith ruling. Eating his flesh means feeding on Christ by faith, and the passage proves it: verse 54 and verse 47 promise the identical thing, one saying "feeds," the other "believes." Verse 35 already fused coming and believing.
- Name the Lord's Supper resonance fairly. The flesh-and-blood, food-and-drink, abiding language is the Table's own language, so the Supper is the appointed sign of this feeding. Guard both errors: you don't have to take communion to be saved (6:47), and the Supper isn't emptied of meaning either.
- Set up 6:63 for next class as the interpretive key ("the flesh is no help at all").
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
