# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 5 Lesson 2 of 2: the One from above (John 3:31-36)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U5 He must increase |
| Lesson | 2 of 2 |
| Date / Day type | Tue Oct 6 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 3:31-36 (ESV) |
| Big idea | To have the Son is to have life right now, and the Son the Father loves holds your life in his hand. |
| Christ connection | Jesus comes from heaven, holds the Spirit without limit, and holds all things, so believing in him is having eternal life today. |
| Memory verse | Review only. No new verse this unit. Today is the unit's cumulative review of John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, and 3:16. Next new verse comes in Unit 6 (John 4:24). |
| Resources | Paired sermon: none for this passage. External map: not needed. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will say in one sentence who's speaking from verse 31 on and why it changed.
2. Students will state where Jesus comes from (verse 31) and why that makes his testimony trustworthy.
3. Students will lay out the two roads in verse 36 and what each person has.
4. Students will explain what "the wrath of God remains" means and how the gift of life in verse 36 answers it.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): verse 36 is a plain fork in the road, and every student can read it and say what each side gets. No background needed.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): this is the same message as verse 16 said again in different words, the Son sent from the Father, believe and live.
- **Stretch** (disciple): verse 33, faith "sets his seal that God is true," and verse 35, the Father's love for the Son, open a thread that runs to John 5, 13, and 17.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Light cumulative memory review first, 2 minutes, ungraded and low-key: say the four verses together from the board (John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16). No quiz, just keep them warm. There's no new verse this unit.
- Then students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "If Jesus holds my whole life in his hand, what would I stop carrying?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write the honest question to God and keep going.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 3:31-36 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 3, verses 31 through 36. Walk the newcomer to the page.
- One-sentence setup: John the Baptist finished talking at verse 30, so from verse 31 on this is the Gospel writer thinking out loud about who Jesus is.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

5. **Entry (any student can answer).** Look at verse 31. Where does Jesus come from? Where does everybody else come from?
   - Cold-call check: Jesus comes "from above" and "from heaven." Everyone else is "of the earth." Jesus is "above all."
6. **Entry.** If someone tells you about a place they've actually been, versus someone guessing, whose account do you trust? Verse 32 says Jesus testifies "to what he has seen and heard." Why does that matter?
7. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 36 carefully. It gives two roads. For each road, write down two things: what that person does about the Son, and what that person has or doesn't have.
   - Guide them: road one, believes in the Son, has eternal life now. Road two, does not obey the Son, does not see life, and the wrath of God remains on him. Have groups notice "has" is present tense. Life starts now.
8. **Core.** Verse 36 says "believes" on one side and "does not obey" on the other. John treats trusting Jesus and obeying Jesus as the same move. What does that tell you about what real faith is?
   - Land it: believing the Son and obeying the Son are one motion toward him. Refusing him is disobedience. Faith is turning toward the Son. Believing him and following him are one motion.
9. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Verse 33 says the one who receives Jesus' testimony "sets his seal to this, that God is true." What does it mean that your faith puts a stamp on God's honesty? (For the stretch: look at 1 John 5:10 for the sharp flip side.)
   - Draw out: to believe Jesus is to certify that God tells the truth. To refuse him, 1 John 5:10 says, is to call God a liar. Handle gently, no student pressured.
10. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence, verse 35. What does the Father do for the Son, and what has he handed him?

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Handle verse 36 with care.

- Lead with the gift, warmly and first. "Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life." Present tense. Life with God starts the day you trust him, here and now. That's the headline. Say it slowly.
- Then name the wrath truthfully, as the thing Jesus came to take away. "The wrath of God remains" means it's already resting on a person and stays there until they come to the Son. Remember Unit 4 and the bronze serpent: the venom was real, and the lifted serpent healed it. Wrath is real, and the cross is where Jesus lifts it off.
- Keep it off fear. Don't use verse 36 to scare anyone into a decision. Fear can diagnose a problem, and it never gives new life. Only the Son lifted up draws people. Use the wrath to make the gift precious, not to threaten.
- Verse 35 for assurance: the Father loves the Son and gave him all things. Your life is one of those things. The hands holding the whole world are holding you.
- Christ connection to say out loud: to have the Son is to have life right now, and the Son the Father loves holds your life in his hand.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Verse 36 says believing in the Son gives you life now. What's one thing that would look different this week if you lived like that were true?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged, not what they decided.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 4:1-26 once before the next unit.
- To the gradebook: today's participation (Quiet-Time Journal and the Move 5 response).
- Sets up Unit 6: Jesus leaves Judea and meets a Samaritan woman at a well. The next new memory verse (John 4:24) lands there.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- The speaker question (who's talking from verse 31) gets one sentence and no more. The Gospel writer is meditating, the same voice that gave verses 16 through 21. Don't spend the lesson on it. Teach the content.
- Verse 36 pitfalls: (1) don't weaponize it, lead with life and let wrath make the gift precious; (2) don't soften it into vagueness, "remains" is honest and present. Hold the danger and the rescue together, the way Unit 4 held verses 17 through 21.
- Plant verse 34 (the Spirit without measure) lightly. It opens toward the living water in John 7 and the Spirit Jesus gives the church in John 14 through 16. Don't over-teach it today.
- This lesson closes Unit 5 and the John the Baptist thread. His last recorded words were "He must increase, but I must decrease." That's the posture the whole course is after.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day. If Move 3 runs long, trim the Deeper question first, keep the cold-call checks.
