**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will recite John 17:3 and state how it defines eternal life.
2. Students will explain "the hour has come; glorify your Son" as the cross (17:1).
3. Students will explain the pre-existent glory of the Son (17:5).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): eternal life is knowing God, starting now.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): "knowing God" is more than the facts you've memorized.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the Son's eternal deity and pre-existence (17:5); eternal life includes knowing Jesus, which only works if he's God.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. This is the memory-verse plant, so give it the full period. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "This is eternal life, that they know you" (John 17:3).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "If someone asked me what 'eternal life' means, what would I say?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write your honest guess.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 17:1-5 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 17, verses 1 through 5.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus stops teaching, lifts his eyes to heaven, and starts praying to his Father, and the first thing he prays about is the cross.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 1, Jesus says "the hour has come." What hour is he talking about, given everything leading up to this night?
   - Cold-call check: the cross, his death. The "hour" he's been pointing to since chapter 12.
2. **Entry.** Jesus asks the Father to "glorify" him, and he's talking about the cross. That's a strange place to find glory. How is the cross Jesus' glory, not his defeat?
   - Draw out: the cross is where Jesus does the thing he came to do, saves his people, and the Father raises him and brings him to the throne. The glory comes through the cross, not around it.
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verse 3. This is the memory verse. How does Jesus define eternal life? Write his definition in your own words.
   - Guide them: eternal life is to KNOW God, "the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent." Not first a place you go. A knowing. A relationship with God that starts now.
4. **Core.** The word for "know" here means to know a person by experience, the way you know someone you actually spend time with. What's the difference between knowing a bunch of facts about a famous person and knowing your best friend? Which kind is eternal life?
   - Land it: eternal life is the second kind. You can know every fact about God and still not know him, the way you can know stats about a celebrity you've never met. Jesus says eternal life is actually knowing God, personally. And that knowing starts the moment you come to him, not just when you die.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Look at exactly who verse 3 says you have to know for eternal life: "the only true God, AND Jesus Christ." Jesus puts himself right next to the only true God as part of what eternal life is. Why does that only work if Jesus is God?
   - Draw out: if Jesus were just a good teacher, knowing him couldn't be eternal life; only knowing God is. But Jesus names knowing himself alongside knowing the true God as the definition of eternal life. He's claiming to be God. (An early Christian named Novatian caught it: Jesus didn't say "the man Jesus Christ." He set himself beside the only true God.)
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** Recite verse 3 from memory. Then: what is eternal life, in one sentence?
   - Knowing God, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom he sent.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Framed fresh.

- Reframe eternal life. A lot of you hear "eternal life" and picture a place, clouds, forever, someday. Jesus defines it differently, and better. Eternal life is knowing God. Not knowing about him. Knowing him, the way you know a person who loves you. And it starts now, not when you die.
- Press the difference gently. You can grow up in church, memorize verses, ace the quiz, and still only know about God, the way you know facts about someone famous. Jesus is after more than that for you. He wants you to actually know him.
- Land the deity. And notice who Jesus says you have to know: the true God, and himself, side by side. He's not being humble and small here. He's saying that knowing him is knowing God, because he is God. The man praying this prayer is the God you were made to know.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus gives eternal life, and eternal life is knowing him, the true God who came to make himself known.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Eternal life is knowing God, not just knowing about him. Which one do you have right now, and what's one step toward actually knowing him?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Start memorizing John 17:3. Read John 17:6-8 once before Tuesday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 17:3 enters the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up L2: Jesus tells the Father he's made him known to the disciples.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- This is the plant. 17:3 is the unit's new graded anchor. Teach eternal life as a relationship of knowing (ginosko, know by experience), guarded on both sides: not bare intellectual facts (Adam's sermon 29: "we let the Greeks steal the word knowledge"), and not a mere feeling. Real knowing of a real Person.
- The deity implication of 17:3: eternal life is knowing the true God AND Jesus Christ together, which only works if Jesus is God (Novatian). Keep it for the disciple tier.
- "The hour" (17:1) is the cross, the glory-through-the-cross thread from 12:23, 28. 17:5 is the Son's pre-existent glory and deity (tie to 1:1).
- Frame the whole prayer as overheard intimacy: the Son talking to his Father the night before the cross. Derive the frame from sermon 29; cite it, don't read it aloud.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block for the plant.
