**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain why the world hates Jesus' followers (17:14).
2. Students will explain what Jesus does and does not ask for (17:15).
3. Students will explain the difference between being in the world and of the world (17:14-16).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Christians live in the world and belong to Jesus.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): Jesus keeps his people in the world on purpose, not out of it.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the posture guarded against both withdrawal and worldliness.

---

**Talk budget:** short day, single focus. Keep your sustained voice light. One clear idea drives the block.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (5 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room fast. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "I do not ask that you take them out of the world, but that you keep them from the evil one" (John 17:15).
- Students write one line in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Where do I feel torn between fitting in and following Jesus?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3 min)

- Have one student read John 17:14-16 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 17, verses 14 through 16.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus prays about how his people should live in a world that doesn't want them.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 20 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Single focus: in the world, not of it. Ask these.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 14, why does the world hate Jesus' followers?
   - Cold-call check: because they're "not of the world," they don't belong to it, the same way Jesus doesn't.
2. **The driving question (whole class, then small groups).** Read verse 15 carefully. Jesus could have asked the Father to just take his people OUT of the world to keep them safe. What does he actually ask for instead? Why not just pull them out?
   - Guide them: he asks the Father to keep them FROM the evil one while they stay IN the world. He doesn't want them removed. Peek at verse 18 (next lesson): he's sending them into the world, the way the Father sent him. They can't reach the world for Jesus if they hide from it. So he keeps them safe in the middle of it instead of pulling them out.
3. **Core.** So what's the difference between being IN the world and being OF the world?
   - Land it: everybody's in the world, living in it, going to school, having friends, using their phones. Being "of" the world means belonging to it, taking its values as your own, letting it define you. Jesus' people live right in the middle of the world (in it) and belong to him instead (not of it). Two errors to avoid: running away and hiding from everyone (Jesus said no to that), and blending in so completely that you're indistinguishable from the world (Jesus said no to that too).
4. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what did Jesus ask for his people, and what did he NOT ask for?
   - He asked the Father to keep them from the evil one, and did not ask to take them out of the world.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Framed fresh.

- Name the tension you live in. You're fifteen, and you feel two pulls all the time. One says blend in, be like everyone, don't stand out. The other, sometimes, says just hide, retreat, stay safe with the church kids and avoid everyone else. Jesus prays against both.
- Land where he lands. He doesn't ask the Father to airlift you out of your school and your friend group. He asks him to keep you safe in the middle of it. You belong to Jesus, and you live right there in the world, on purpose, as his.
- Point to the mission. Why keep you in it instead of out of it? Because that's where the people are. He's sending you into the world, not away from it. You're his in the middle of everything, and that's exactly where he wants you.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus keeps his people in the world, holy and guarded, and sends them into it as his own.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt on the board: "In the world, not of it. Where do you lean toward blending in, and where do you lean toward hiding? What would the middle look like this week?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 17:3. Read John 17:17-19 once before Friday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L5: Jesus asks the Father to make his people holy through the truth.

## Teacher notes

- The key guard: the church's posture against BOTH errors, withdrawal/isolation (Jesus says don't take them out, 17:15) and worldliness/compromise (they're not of the world, 17:16).
