**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain "I have manifested your name" as making the Father known (17:6).
2. Students will explain what the disciples did with the words Jesus gave them (17:8).
3. Students will explain that these people were the Father's, given to Jesus (17:6).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus shows people what God is really like.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): knowing God (from L1) happens as Jesus reveals the Father.
- **Stretch** (disciple): faith starts with God giving people to the Son (17:6), grace before response.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "I have manifested your name to the people whom you gave me" (John 17:6).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What do I actually know about what God is like? Where did I learn it?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write it honestly, even if it's not much.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 17:6-8 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 17, verses 6 through 8.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus keeps praying, and now he tells the Father what he's done for his disciples.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 6, what does Jesus say he has done for the people the Father gave him?
   - Cold-call check: he made the Father's "name" known to them, he showed them who God is.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** "I have manifested your name" means to make God himself known, what he's really like. Connect this to yesterday's verse (17:3, eternal life is knowing God). How does Jesus make the "knowing God" of verse 3 actually happen?
   - Guide them: you can't know God on your own, from a distance. Jesus shows you the Father. He's the one who makes God known, up close, in a way you can actually see. So the eternal-life knowing of verse 3 happens through Jesus revealing the Father.
3. **Core.** Read verse 8. What did Jesus give the disciples, and what three things did they do with it?
   - Land it: he gave them the words the Father gave him. And they received them, came to know that Jesus really came from God, and believed that the Father sent him. Received, knew, believed. That's what faith looks like: taking Jesus' words, and trusting where they point.
4. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Look carefully at verse 6: "the people whom you gave me out of the world. Yours they were, and you gave them to me." Who does Jesus say these disciples belonged to first, and who brought them to Jesus?
   - Draw out: they were the Father's first, and the Father gave them to Jesus. So faith doesn't start with us climbing up to God. It starts with God reaching down and drawing people to his Son. That's a comfort: if you're his, it's because he came and got you.
5. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what did Jesus do for the people the Father gave him, according to verse 6?
   - He made the Father known to them.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Framed fresh.

- Connect it to yesterday. Eternal life is knowing God (verse 3). But how do you know a God you can't see? Jesus says: I showed him to them. That's his whole job in the prayer. He makes the invisible God known, close enough to trust.
- Land the words. Notice how they came to know God: through the words Jesus gave them. That's still how it works. You come to know God through the words of Jesus, the ones written down in this book. Which is why reading it matters. It's where the knowing happens.
- Comfort the anxious one. And here's the tender part. These people were the Father's first, and he gave them to Jesus. If you belong to Jesus, it's not because you were clever enough to find him. It's because the Father reached down and handed you to his Son. You were wanted before you ever went looking.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus makes the Father known, and gathers the people the Father gives him.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "You come to know God through the words of Jesus. What would change if you actually opened this book expecting to meet him?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep memorizing John 17:3. Read John 17:9-13 once before Wednesday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L3: Jesus asks the Father to keep his people safe.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- The heart of the lesson: Jesus reveals the Father (17:6), which is how the "knowing God" of 17:3 actually happens. Tie it back to L1.
- 17:8: faith is receiving Jesus' words, coming to know, and believing. Keep it concrete.
- 17:6: these were the Father's, given to the Son. Grace before response, belonging before believing (Adam's sermon 30). Keep it pastoral, aimed at assurance, for the disciple tier.
- Derive the frame from sermon 30; cite it, don't read it aloud.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
