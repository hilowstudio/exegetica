**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain what Jesus most wants for his people (17:24, to be with him and see his glory).
2. Students will explain the climax: the Father's love for the Son placed in believers (17:26).
3. Students will connect 17:26 to the new command to love (13:34).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): heaven is being with Jesus.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the goal of it all is God's own love living in his people.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the Trinitarian love poured into the believer fulfilling the new command (13:34).

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. This closes the whole Farewell Discourse, so leave room to gather it.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "That the love with which you have loved me may be in them, and I in them" (John 17:26).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "When I picture heaven, what do I picture?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 17:24-26 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 17, verses 24 through 26.
- One-sentence setup: Jesus finishes his prayer with the thing he wants most, and the deepest note in the whole discourse.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 24, what does Jesus say he wants ("I desire") for his people?
   - Cold-call check: that they'd be with him where he is, to see his glory.
2. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Look at how Jesus describes the goal in verse 24: "be with me where I am, to see my glory." What is heaven, according to this verse? Is it mainly a place, or mainly a Person?
   - Guide them: mainly a Person. Heaven is being WITH Jesus and seeing him. It's not a vague sky or clouds and harps. It's finally being with the one you were made to know (remember verse 3, eternal life is knowing him). Heaven is the knowing of verse 3, complete, face to face.
3. **Core.** Connect verse 24 back to something Jesus said earlier this same night, in John 14:3: "that where I am you may be also." Jesus prayed for the thing he'd already promised. What does it tell you that he asks the Father for it out loud?
   - Draw out: he really means it, and he's making sure of it. Whatever Jesus asks the Father, he gets. So this isn't a wish. It's as good as done. He asked that you'd be with him, and he's the one who always gets what he asks.
4. **Core (the climax).** Read verse 26, the very last line of the prayer. What does Jesus want to be IN his people? This is the highest thing he prays for.
   - Land it: "that the love with which you have loved me may be in them." The love the Father has for Jesus, the biggest, oldest, most perfect love there is, Jesus prays that same love would be placed inside his people. And that he himself would live in them. That's the goal of the whole prayer: God's own love, in you.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Way back at the start of this discourse (John 13:34), Jesus commanded "love one another as I have loved you." Now the discourse ends with the Father's love being placed IN his people (17:26). How does the end make the command possible?
   - Draw out: you can't love like Jesus on an empty tank. The command at the start (love one another) is only possible because of the gift at the end (God's own love placed in you). First he fills you with the Father's love, then that love flows out to others. The command and the power are the two ends of the same discourse.
6. **Memory verse cement.** Have students recite John 17:3 together, then in pairs. Make sure "know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ" is solid.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the chapter?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and close the unit and the discourse. Framed fresh.

- Land what heaven actually is. When Jesus tells you what he wants most for you, it's this: to be with me, to see my glory. Heaven isn't a place he's sending you. It's a Person he's bringing you to. It's the knowing of verse 3, all the way. You were made to be with him, and that's where this ends.
- Land the last line, slowly. And the very last thing Jesus prays, the deepest note in the whole talk, is that the Father's love for him would live in you. The love that has existed forever between the Father and the Son, poured into ordinary people like you. That's the goal of everything. Not that you'd behave. That God's own love would fill you.
- Gather the whole discourse. Think about where this started, chapter 13, a towel and a basin, "love one another." And where it ends, chapter 17, the Father's love placed in his people. He commanded love at the start because he was going to supply it at the end. First he fills you, then it flows.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus prayed to bring his people home to see his glory and to fill them with the Father's own love, and whatever he asks, he gets.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "The last thing Jesus prayed was that God's own love would live in you. If that's the goal of everything, what's one thing it changes about how you see yourself?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.
