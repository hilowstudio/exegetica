# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 12 Lesson 2 of 5: the I AM, lifted up (John 8:21-30)

| Field | Detail |

| Date / Day type | Tue Nov 17 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 8:21-30 (ESV) |
| Big idea | Jesus uses God's own name "I am," warns that people who refuse him die in their sin, and says the cross is where they'll finally know it's true. |
| Christ connection | Jesus is the I AM, the divine name carried to the cross, doing only what the Father gives him. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain what "die in your sins" means and what avoids it (8:24).
2. Students will explain what "when you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he" means (8:28).
3. Students will explain what "I am he" points back to (Exodus 3:14).

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): Jesus keeps making a claim big enough to change everything.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): "I am he" is God's own name, and believing it is the difference Jesus names.
- **Stretch** (disciple): "I am he" from Exodus 3:14; the cross as where the name is spelled out.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. The Work block belongs to the students.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "He who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone" (John 8:29).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Where do I feel most alone right now?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write the honest loneliness, whatever it is.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 8:21-30 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 8, verses 21 through 30.
- One-sentence setup: the argument keeps going, and Jesus starts using the most loaded name in the Bible about himself.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 24, what does Jesus say a person has to believe so they don't "die in their sins"?
   - Cold-call check: they have to believe "that I am he." Believing who Jesus is, is the thing.
2. **Entry.** "Die in your sins" sounds heavy. In plain terms, what do you think it means to reject the light who came and stay in the dark?
   - Guide gently: to stay where you already were, in sin and cut off from God, because you turned away the one who came to rescue you.
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** "I am he" (verse 24) is God's own name. Back in Exodus 3:14, when Moses asked God his name, God said "I AM WHO I AM." What is Jesus claiming when he says "I am he" about himself?
   - Guide them: he's taking God's personal name and applying it to himself. He's claiming to be the God of the burning bush. This is the same "I am" the woman at the well heard (4:26).
4. **Core.** Read verse 28: "when you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he." "Lifted up" is Jesus' word for the cross. Why would the cross be the place people finally know who he is?
   - Land it: the cross is where his claim gets proven. He goes to it on purpose, doing only what the Father gives him (verse 28-29). A Roman soldier at the cross is the first to say, "Truly this was the Son of God." The crucifixion spells out the name.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Read verse 29: "he who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone." The whole room is turning against Jesus, and he says he's not alone. How is that a comfort for you when you feel alone?
   - Draw out: even surrounded by enemies, Jesus was held by the Father. If the Son was never abandoned in the hardest room, you're not abandoned either. The Father who was with him is with you.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In one sentence: what does a person have to believe about Jesus, according to verse 24?
   - That he is "I am," God himself.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and speak it over the room. Build on sermon 13.

- Name the claim without softening it. Jesus took the name God gave himself at the burning bush and put it on his own lips. He's saying he is God. That's the whole argument of this chapter, said louder each time.
- Handle "die in your sins" honestly and warmly. It's a real warning, and it's held out with a rescue in the same breath. The way to not die in your sins is simple: believe who Jesus really is. And notice how the passage ends. Verse 30: "many believed in him." The warning drew people in.
- Point to the cross. Jesus says the day they crucify him is the day they'll know it was all true. He walks to his death on purpose, so the name "I am" gets spelled out in what he does for you there.
- Speak the comfort of verse 29 over the anxious student. Jesus was never left alone, even in that hostile room, and neither are you. The Father is with you.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus is the great I AM, and he carried that name all the way to the cross for you.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus says believing who he really is changes everything. Who do you actually believe Jesus is, right now? Write it honestly."
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 8:31-38 once before Wednesday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation.
- Sets up L3: Jesus talks about the truth that sets people free, and the slavery they don't know they're in.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block. Turn content into questions. Keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Build on sermon 13's escalation. This lesson is a rung up from L1: from "I am the light" to "I am he," the divine name. Keep the climb visible.
- Guard "die in your sins" (8:24) against fear-leverage. Fear does not regenerate. Teach it as the seriousness of who Jesus is, and lead with the rescue and with "many believed" (8:30).
- Teach "I am he" at the disciple tier as the divine name (Exodus 3:14; Isaiah 43). Tie it back to 4:26 and forward to the climax in L5 (8:58).
- Use verse 29 for the anxious student. It's a warm, quiet beat: Jesus was not left alone in a hostile room.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day, full Work block.
