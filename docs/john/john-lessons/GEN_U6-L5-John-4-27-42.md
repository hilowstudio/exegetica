| Passage | John 4:27-42 (ESV) |
| Big idea | An outsider town hears Jesus for themselves and calls him the Savior of the world. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will describe what the woman does with her testimony (4:28-29) and its effect on the town.
2. Students will explain what Jesus means that the "fields are white for harvest" (4:35).
3. Students will state why the Samaritans' firsthand faith (4:42) matters and what "Savior of the world" claims.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): the woman's whole message was "come and see." That's all it took.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): the town moves from a borrowed faith (the woman's word) to a firsthand one (4:42). Watch that shift.
- **Stretch** (disciple): the harvest saying and the world-ward reach, from 1:29 and 3:16 out to Acts 1:8.

---

**Talk budget:** short day, single arc. One key question in the Work block. Keep your voice well under half the 36 minutes.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (5 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this is indeed the Savior of the world" (John 4:42).
- One quiet minute in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Whose faith am I living on right now, someone else's or my own?"

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have two students read John 4:27-42 aloud, one reads 27 through 34, the other 35 through 42.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 4, verses 27 through 42.
- One-sentence setup: the woman runs to tell her town, the disciples are confused about food, and by the end the whole town believes.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 16 min, one key question, no lecture)

Open with two quick read-checks, then work the key question.

1. **Read-check.** In verse 28, what does the woman leave behind at the well, and where does she go?
   - She leaves her water jar and goes to tell the town. She came for water and left with something better.
2. **Read-check.** In verse 35, Jesus says the fields are "white for harvest." The townspeople are walking out to him at that very moment (verse 30). What harvest is he talking about?
   - People. The Samaritans coming to believe. The harvest is for eternal life (verse 36). Keep this light today.
3. **Key question (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verses 39 through 42. At first the town believes "because of the woman's testimony." By the end they say, "It is no longer because of what you said that we believe, for we have heard for ourselves." Work this: what's the difference between those two kinds of faith, and why does John make a point of the change?
   - Guide the groups. Draw out: a borrowed faith rests on someone else's word. A firsthand faith has met Jesus directly. The woman's testimony was the doorway, and they walked all the way through it to Jesus himself. John wants a faith you own for yourself.

## Move 4: Land and minister (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and land the unit.

- Notice who says it. The Samaritans, the outsiders, are the ones who call Jesus "the Savior of the world." The people everyone wrote off are the first to name him rightly, while Jerusalem still hasn't.
- Say what "Savior of the world" claims. The reach runs from the Lamb who takes away the sin of the world (1:29) through "God so loved the world" (3:16) out to a Samaritan town, and past them to everyone. In that empire, "Savior of the world" was Caesar's title. John hands it to Jesus.
- Bring it home. It started with one tired conversation at a well with a woman nobody else would talk to. That's how the Savior of the world reaches a town. He knows everything about you, he stays, and he's worth telling one person about.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus is the Savior of the world, and the outsiders who heard him for themselves are the first to know it.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "The town moved from believing on the woman's word to hearing Jesus for themselves. What would 'hearing him for yourself' look like for you this week?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Keep John 4:24 going in your memory work.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 4:24 stays in the rolling memory quizzes.
- Sets up Unit 7: Jesus heals an official's son back in Galilee, the second sign at Cana.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: short day, single arc. One key question, and protect Move 4 for the "Savior of the world" landing.
- Teach the harvest saying (4:35-38) at a light touch today. Give the full sower-and-reaper detail (the prophets and John as sowers, the disciples as reapers) to the disciple tier as optional depth. Don't let it become a guilt-trip about how much students witness. It's God's harvest.
- The unit lands here, on "Savior of the world." Make sure that line gets said and lands before the bell.
- Day-type note: Thursday 36 minutes, and this passage runs long (16 verses). Keep the arc moving. If you run short, cut the harvest detail, not the 4:42 landing.
