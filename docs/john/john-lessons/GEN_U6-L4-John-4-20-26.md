# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 6 Lesson 4 of 5: worship in spirit and truth (John 4:20-26)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U6 Living water |
| Lesson | 4 of 5 |
| Date / Day type | Tue Oct 13 / Regular (50 min) |
| Passage | John 4:20-26 (ESV) |
| Big idea | Jesus lifts worship off every mountain and building, because God is spirit, and he tells the woman plainly that he's the Messiah. |
| Christ connection | Jesus ends the worship wars by giving the Spirit and revealing his own face as the Messiah. |
| Memory verse | PLANT John 4:24 here, the unit's new graded verse: "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth." Teach it fresh in this lesson. |
| Resources | No paired sermon for John 4:1-42. See the unit framing for the worship read. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will explain what Jesus means that worship is no longer bound to "this mountain" or Jerusalem (4:21-24).
2. Students will state what "God is spirit" means for where and how we worship (4:24).
3. Students will explain "salvation is from the Jews" (4:22) without sneering at Samaritans or erasing Israel.
4. Students will identify 4:26 as Jesus' first plain claim to be the Messiah.

**Reach / Hold / Stretch:**
- **Reach** (new to the Bible): everyone's argued about the "right" way to do church or faith. Jesus takes that fight apart.
- **Hold** (churched, passive): it's possible to have the right building and the right words and a heart far off (Matthew 15:8-9).
- **Stretch** (disciple): "I am he" (ego eimi) as the first plain self-disclosure, running forward to 8:24; "salvation is from the Jews" against Romans 9:4-5.

---

**Talk budget:** keep your sustained voice under about 40% of the 50 minutes. No single block past 8 to 10 minutes. This is the densest lesson in the unit, so the Work block earns its full time.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (7 min, ungraded)

- Host today: you or the rotating student. Prayer is opt-in.
- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "the Father is seeking such people to worship him" (John 4:23).
- Students write in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What do I think God wants from me when I worship?"
- On-ramp for the doubter: write what you'd want to ask God about worship if he were listening.

## Move 2: Hear the Word (3-5 min)

- Have one student read John 4:20-26 aloud, slowly.
- Navigation help: John, chapter 4, verses 20 through 26.
- One-sentence setup: the woman changes the subject to an old argument about where to worship, and Jesus takes it somewhere she didn't expect.

## Move 3: Work the text (about 25 min, the governed block, no lecture)

Ask these. Let students find it.

1. **Entry (any student can answer).** In verse 20, what's the argument the woman brings up? Whose mountain versus whose city?
   - Cold-call check: the Samaritans worship on this mountain (Gerizim), the Jews say worship belongs in Jerusalem. It's a which-side-is-right fight.
2. **Entry.** Where do people today argue about the "right" way to do church or faith? Name a real one.
3. **Core (small groups, observe in the Reading Journal).** Read verses 21 through 24. Jesus doesn't pick the mountain or the city. Where does he say true worship happens now? Write down the two words he uses in verse 24.
   - Guide them to "spirit and truth." Draw out: worship isn't tied to a place anymore. "In spirit" means the Holy Spirit makes it possible. "In truth" means it's shaped by what's real, finally by Jesus himself.
4. **Core.** Look at verse 24's first three words: "God is spirit." That's the reason Jesus gives for how we worship. Why does God being spirit mean worship can't be locked to one building or mountain?
   - Land it: God isn't made of matter and isn't stuck in one location, so the worship that fits him isn't stuck in one location either. You can worship the Father anywhere the Spirit is at work and Jesus is embraced.
5. **Deeper (high ceiling, for the disciple).** Verse 22 says "salvation is from the Jews." Jesus won't let the Samaritans off the hook ("you worship what you do not know"), and he won't sneer at them either. What does he mean that salvation comes from the Jews?
   - Draw out: God gave the promises, the Scriptures, and the Messiah through Israel (Romans 9:4-5). And the direction is outward: salvation comes from the Jews to the whole world, and a Samaritan town is about to prove it (4:42). No sneering at Samaritans, no erasing Israel.
6. **Cold-call comprehension check.** In verse 26, the woman says the Messiah is coming. What does Jesus tell her? Who's the first kind of person to hear him say it plainly?
   - "I who speak to you am he." A Samaritan woman with a rough past hears it first, before Jerusalem ever does.

Circulate. Short probes. "Where's that in the verse?"

## Move 4: Land and minister, and teach the memory verse (5-7 min, your protected pulpit)

Bring it to Christ and plant the new verse.

- Plant John 4:24 as this unit's new memory verse: "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth." Put it on the board and say it twice.
- Teach it as good news. It describes who God is, and the description sets worship free. Because God is spirit, he isn't trapped in a building or a mountain or your family's way of doing things. You can worship the Father anywhere the Spirit is working and the truth, Jesus, is embraced.
- Unpack the two words once, plainly. "Spirit" is the Holy Spirit, who makes real worship possible. "Truth" is what's real, and finally Jesus himself (14:6), so worship is shaped by him.
- Lead with grace, straight from verse 23: "the Father is seeking such people to worship him." Worship starts with God coming after you. Nobody works up spirit-and-truth worship by trying harder. The Father gives the Spirit that makes it possible, and we receive it and come.
- Christ connection to say out loud: Jesus ends the worship wars by giving the Spirit and showing his own face as the Messiah.

## Move 5: Respond (3-5 min, graded participation)

- In-room written response in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Jesus says God is looking for worshipers, and that you can worship him anywhere. What would it look like to worship God this week somewhere other than a church building?"
- Give it quiet. Grade that they wrote and engaged.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Start memorizing John 4:24. Read John 4:27-42 once before Thursday.
- To the gradebook: today's participation. John 4:24 joins the rolling memory-verse quizzes going forward.
- Sets up L5: the woman runs to tell her town, and the Samaritans call Jesus the Savior of the world.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: governed Work block, densest lesson in the unit. Convert content into questions and keep your sustained voice under 40%.
- Plant 4:24 inside the flow that produces it, in Move 4, not as a bolt-on. The verse makes sense because of the worship-wars conversation right before it.
- Two pitfalls to guard: don't flatten "spirit and truth" into "just be sincere," and don't read 4:22 as anti-Samaritan or as erasing Israel. Salvation comes from the Jews outward to the world.
- Give the plain sense of "I am he" to the whole room (he's telling her he's the Messiah). Save the deeper echo of the divine name (running to 8:24) for the disciple tier.
- Day-type note: regular 50-minute day. Protect Move 4, since that's where the memory verse plants.
