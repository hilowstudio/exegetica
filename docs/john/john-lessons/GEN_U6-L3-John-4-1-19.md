# Teacher lesson guide, Unit 6 Lesson 3 of 5: recap and memory check (John 4:1-19)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | U6 Living water |
| Lesson | 3 of 5 |
| Date / Day type | Fri Oct 9 / Half-day (26 min), memory quiz plus recap |
| Passage | John 4:1-19 (recap of L1 and L2) |
| Big idea | The well conversation so far shows Jesus crossing every barrier to offer living water to a woman he already knows completely. |
| Christ connection | Jesus is the living water who knows the outsider fully and keeps talking with her. |
| Memory verse | Cumulative check on the four verses students already hold: John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16. The unit's new verse (John 4:24) has not come up in the story yet, so do not quiz it today. |
| Resources | No paired sermon. This is a scheduled half-day recap and memory beat. |

**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will recall what living water is (the Spirit and the eternal life it brings) and the barriers Jesus crossed to offer it (4:1-15).
2. Students will write the four assigned memory verses from memory (John 20:31, 1:14, 1:12, 3:16).

---

**Talk budget:** short day. This is a recap and a graded memory quiz, not new exposition. Keep your voice light and let students do the recalling and the writing.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (4 min, ungraded)

- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never be thirsty again" (John 4:14).
- One quiet minute in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "Where did I go to God this week?"

## Move 2: Recap the well conversation (6 min)

Quick, student-driven. Ask, don't retell.

1. Who does Jesus meet at the well, and what two barriers is he crossing by talking with her? (A Samaritan, a woman alone. Jews and Samaritans didn't mix.)
2. What's the difference between the well water and the living water Jesus offers? (The well leaves you thirsty again. The living water becomes a spring welling up to eternal life. It's the Spirit and the life he gives, John 7:39.)
3. In verses 16 through 18, what does Jesus know about her, and how does she respond? (Her whole history. She calls him a prophet and stays in the conversation.)

Say one sentence to set up next week: the conversation is about to turn to worship, and that's where the memory verse comes from.

## Move 3: Memory quiz (12 min, graded)

- This is a rolling memory-verse quiz. It feeds the gradebook.
- Students write all four assigned verses from memory, by reference, in the ESV. Phones and Bibles away.
- The four references, put them on the board:
  1. John 20:31
  2. John 1:14
  3. John 1:12
  4. John 3:16
- Grade for the sense and the key phrases, not word-perfect punctuation. A student who has the meaning and the main wording has it.
- The verse texts are in the Answer Key below.

## Move 4: Land and minister (3 min)

- One short word over the room: the woman kept the memory of one conversation and it changed her town. Verses stay with you the same way. What you're memorizing now is Jesus telling you who he is and what he gives.
- Christ connection to say out loud: the living water Jesus offered at the well is the same life these four verses point to.

## Move 5: Respond (1-2 min, graded participation)

- One line in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Which of these four verses do you most need to believe right now?"

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Read John 4:20-26 once before Tuesday. That's the worship lesson and the new memory verse.
- To the gradebook: today's memory quiz (content) and the participation line.
- Sets up L4: the worship-wars question, and Jesus telling the woman plainly that he's the Messiah.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: this is a scheduled half-day. It's a memory quiz plus recap, never dense exposition. Don't try to teach 4:20-26 today.
- Keep 4:24 off the quiz. It hasn't been planted yet, and it plants fresh on Tuesday (L4).
- If a student was absent for L1 or L2, the recap in Move 2 catches them up enough to follow L4.

Standards: JN.4.1-19

## Answer Key

Memory quiz, the four assigned verses (ESV). Grade for sense and key wording.

1. **John 20:31**: "but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name."
2. **John 1:14**: "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth."
3. **John 1:12**: "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God,"
4. **John 3:16**: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
