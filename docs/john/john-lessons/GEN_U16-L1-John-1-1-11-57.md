**Content objectives (the floor quizzes and finals assess):**
1. Students will recall the seven signs and what each showed about who Jesus is.
2. Students will write the nine assigned memory verses from memory.

---

**Talk budget:** short day. This is a recap and a graded memory quiz, not new teaching. Keep your voice light and celebratory, and let students do the recalling and the writing.

## Move 1: Quiet time with the Lord (4 min, ungraded)

- Settle the room. Phones away.
- Centering line on the board: "but these are written so that you may believe" (John 20:31).
- One quiet minute in the Quiet-Time Journal. Prompt: "What's one thing I've learned about Jesus this semester?"

## Move 2: Recap the Book of Signs (8 min)

Quick and warm. Ask, don't lecture. Walk the whole arc in one sweep.

1. Where did the book start? (The Prologue: "the Word became flesh," 1:14. God came in a body.)
2. Name the seven signs and what each showed:
   - Water into wine at Cana (2:11), the new joy Jesus brings.
   - The official's son healed from a distance (4:46-54), his word carries power.
   - The man at the pool healed on the Sabbath (5), he does the Father's own work.
   - Feeding the five thousand and walking on water (6), he is the bread of life, the great "I am."
   - The man born blind (9), he is the light who gives sight.
   - Lazarus raised (11), he is the resurrection and the life.
3. Name the "I am" sayings we've collected: bread of life (6:35), light of the world (8:12), the door and the good shepherd (10:7, 10:11), the resurrection and the life (11:25), and the biggest one, "before Abraham was, I am" (8:58).
4. Tie it together with the first verse we learned: John 20:31. Why did John write all this down? So you would believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and have life in his name.

Say one sentence: look how far we've come, and look who all these signs have shown you.

## Move 3: Memory quiz (12 min, graded)

- This is a cumulative memory-verse quiz. It feeds the gradebook.
- Students write all nine assigned verses from memory, by reference, in the ESV. Phones and Bibles away.
- The nine references, put them on the board:
  1. John 20:31
  2. John 1:14
  3. John 1:12
  4. John 3:16
  5. John 4:24
  6. John 6:35
  7. John 8:12
  8. John 10:11
  9. John 11:25
- Grade for the sense and the key phrases, not word-perfect punctuation. A student who has the meaning and the main wording has it.
- The verse texts are in the Answer Key below.

## Move 4: Land and minister (1-2 min)

- One short word over the room before break: nine verses, the whole first half of John, and one Jesus that all of it points to. You're carrying real treasure into this break.
- Christ connection to say out loud: every sign, every "I am," every verse you hold points to the same person, and he came to give you life.

## Move 5: Respond (short, graded participation)

- One line in the Reading Journal. Prompt on the board: "Which verse do you most want to remember over break, and why?"
- A short blessing and prayer over the break (opt-in) closes the class well.

## After the bell

- Homework: near zero. Enjoy the break. When we come back, we start John 12, where the hour finally comes.
- To the gradebook: today's memory quiz (content) and the participation line.
- Sets up Unit 16's exposition: after break, John 12 opens with Mary anointing Jesus for his burial.

## Teacher notes

- Governing reminder: this is a scheduled half-day and the last class before winter break. It's a memory quiz plus recap, celebratory, not new exposition. The John 12 exposition begins after break (L2).
- Keep the recap a fast, warm sweep of the Book of Signs, not a lecture. Let students name the signs and the sayings.
- The memory quiz is a graded cumulative check on all nine anchors, consistent with the earlier half-day memory beat in Unit 6. Match how that one fed the gradebook.
- Send them out light. They've learned the whole first half of John and nine load-bearing verses. Let them feel that.
- Day-type note: half-day 26 minutes.

## Answer Key

Cumulative memory quiz, the nine assigned verses (ESV). Grade for sense and key wording.

1. **John 20:31**: "but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name."
2. **John 1:14**: "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth."
3. **John 1:12**: "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God,"
4. **John 3:16**: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
5. **John 4:24**: "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."
6. **John 6:35**: "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst."
7. **John 8:12**: "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life."
8. **John 10:11**: "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."
9. **John 11:25**: "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live."
