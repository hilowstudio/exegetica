# 0028: Transcripts for the Living Library when a teacher asks: Deepgram for podcast episodes, Gemini for videos

Decided by A. James Vega, 2026-11-09 ("Gemini it is, then"), after YouTube's captions stopped reaching other sites. Amended 2026-11-13: "Podcasts should always go to Deepgram", and, after a side-by-side test, "Commit to 3.8 Flash for video transcription."

**Decision.**
- A teacher's video or podcast episode gets its transcript from a free, published source first: a feed's own `<podcast:transcript>` file, or YouTube's captions on the rare video whose captions still come through. When there is none, the teacher can ask for one with **Make a transcript**.
- **A podcast episode always goes to Deepgram** (nova-2), the way Podcatch transcribes audio (`podcatch/inngest/functions/process-episode.ts`): smart formatting, punctuation, paragraphs and speakers. Deepgram is sent the episode's audio link; when the show's host refuses Deepgram, Exegetica's server fetches the audio to a temporary file, sends it, and deletes it. Requests stay in Deepgram's model-improvement program, which may keep the audio to train on (the maker, 2026-11-13: "It is not our audio, it will always be public podcasts we are transcribing, never student recordings"); the privacy page says so. Each sentence becomes a segment with Deepgram's own times.
- **A YouTube video goes to Gemini 3.8 Flash** (`gemini-3.8-flash`; `TRANSCRIBE_MODEL` changes it), which watches the public link at low resolution. Gemini is told to transcribe word for word, never to summarize, and to stamp a time at least every 30 seconds. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, the frontier speech model, takes audio only and refuses a YouTube link, and Exegetica never downloads a video, so it has no part here.
- The transcript is **kept and shared**: every teacher who adds the same video or show gets it, so each recording is transcribed once. It is published broadcast text, which the Living Library plan allows to be stored.
- **Limits:** nothing longer than two hours (YouTube's own length, or the feed's), audio under 300 MB, one job per recording at a time, ten transcripts per teacher and $5 for everyone together per school day. A capped teacher sees one fixed line, and no service is called. The two daily limits are **off while the courses are being built** (the maker, 2026-11-13: "Remove the ceiling while we are building. We will turn it back on later."): `usage.TRANSCRIBE_CAPS_ON` turns them back on, and spending is still counted on `/ops` meanwhile. A job that fails keeps a plain sentence saying why, and is never retried on its own, so a recording is never paid for twice.

**Why.** Since 2025 YouTube's caption links need a token only its own player can make, so they return an empty file for nearly every video. Podcatch's caption step fails the same way, silently. The church-world podcasts teachers use (BibleProject, The Bible Recap, Ask NT Wright Anything) publish no transcripts. Gemini watching the link is the one route for videos that works within YouTube's rules.

For videos, the same 43-minute video went to four models (2026-11-13). Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro Preview both ran its full length in 111 seconds, agreed on 96% of their words, and put the same words within a second of each other (never more than 7). Flash keeps the speaker's exact words, false starts and all; Pro tidies them, and once returned no usable transcript on an 8-minute video. 3.5 Flash-Lite broke off unreadably on the long video and skipped four stretches of speech on the short one. Gemini 2.5 Pro answered that it is "no longer available to new users." Flash cost a fifth of Pro.

For podcasts, one pass of Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite over the audio failed on hour-long episodes. On the 14 episodes of the Bible II class shelf (2026-11-13) it returned nothing twice, hit its output limit once, wrote its clock as minutes:seconds:frames four times, drifted to 1:34 in a 52-minute episode, stopped 15 to 30 minutes early, and once gave 79 words for 57 minutes. Deepgram transcribed the 11 that failed, each to within 30 seconds of its end, with word counts that fit each episode's length. On the three longest (3 hours), Gemini 3.8 Flash, Deepgram Nova-2 and Nova-3 agreed on 97 to 99% of their words with times within a second. Each Deepgram model made real errors where Flash did not (Nova-2 wrote two phrases twice and dropped a sentence; Nova-3 dropped about 25 words and wrote nonsense in their place), and both garbled a hymn the speaker recited. Deepgram took 12 to 17 seconds an episode, Flash 2 to 3 minutes. Deepgram stays for podcasts because it fetches the audio itself, and on Nova-2, which Deepgram itself recommends for English-only, low-cost batch transcription. Nova-3 is where Deepgram's improvements go, and only Nova-3 can be given key terms to listen for (names like Nicodemus, words like propitiation), so it is worth testing again when those matter.

**Cost.** Deepgram nova-2 for recorded audio is reported at about $0.0043 a minute, $0.26 an hour (opting out of the improvement program may raise it); the 11 episodes, 9.7 hours, came to about $2.54. Gemini 3.8 Flash is reported at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 output, an introductory rate through 2026-12-31 said to rise to $1.50 and $7.50. A video takes about 91 input tokens a second; the 43-minute video cost $0.24 on Flash, about $0.34 an hour (about $0.68 at the later rate), against $1.20 on 3.1 Pro ($1.67 an hour). Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, the model before, cost about $0.15 an hour (measured 2026-11-09). Each job's estimate is recorded on `/ops`.

**Enforced in.** `webapp/transcripts.py` (`deepgram_audio`, `DEEPGRAM_PARAMS`, `deepgram_cost`; `gemini_youtube`, `MODEL`, `PRICES`, `LONG_TOKENS`, `MAX_SECONDS`, `PROMPT`); `webapp/excerpts.py` (`_start`, `run`, `MAKER`, the job row in `library_transcribe_jobs`); `transcribe-media` in `webapp/inngest_app.py` (one run per recording, two at once, no retries), with an in-process task when Inngest isn't configured; `usage.transcribe_check` and `usage.transcribe_add`; the route label `library:transcribe` in `webapp/metrics.py`, so every run's cost shows on `/ops`; the privacy page's Google and Deepgram entries. Covered by `tests/test_transcripts.py` and `scripts/check_prep_sql.py`.

**Needs before it runs in production.** A deploy: `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` is staged on Fly (2026-11-13) and takes effect with the next deploy; `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` and the Inngest keys are already set. Jobs run through Inngest's `transcribe-media`, not in the server's own background: Fly stops this app's machine when no request is open (`auto_stop_machines`, `min_machines_running = 0`), and a job inside Inngest's request keeps it running.

**Reverse when.** By the maker's decision. Changing the model, the length limit or the caps needs only the constants above and this record.
