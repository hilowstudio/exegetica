# 0021: The student prompt separates what the tradition holds from how a disputed question is answered

Decided by A. James Vega, 2026-11-05. Its disputed-questions procedure was replaced the same day by docs/decisions/0022, which made answers plain by default. The attribution, method, voice and consistency parts stand.

**Decision.** The Bible student prompt is rebuilt around one distinction: the Reformed section states what the library's teachers hold, and a separate procedure governs any question the traditions answer differently.

- **Disputed questions** (Bible only, placed just before the conduct floor). The section lists the questions a John course raises that traditions read differently (baptism and John 3:5, John 6 and the Supper, Peter and the rock, John 20:23, election and free will, the extent of the atonement, perseverance, "again" or "from above", the love words in John 21, John 1:5, the disputed verses, the Passover chronology). On any of them: say in the first sentence that Christians read it differently, give each major reading at the same depth with its evidence and who holds it, take each tradition's reading from its own documents in the library (Trent, the Baltimore Catechism, Augsburg, the Thirty-Nine Articles, the Orthodox Longer Catechism), give the Reformed reading last and labeled, and never write "the text means", "John means", "the fathers insisted", or "this library teaches" for a contested reading.
- **Attribution.** The doctrine section the students get is `doctrine-student.md`, titled "The Reformed tradition", and it attributes every position to the people and documents that hold it, never to Exegetica or "this library". Its Sanctification section states Marshall's account as the tradition's teaching and leaves the care of a student's soul to their pastor, parents, and teachers. The Style and tool-list sections, and the Anchors section, are gone from it. The shared `doctrine.md` is unchanged and still serves the Theologian persona and the Generator.
- **Method** (every mode, just before the floor): name sources the way a reader cites them and never a tool, treat tool results as notes to write from (no coordinates, record ids, or field labels), cite a confession by the number the tool shows, say in one line when the library has no source and mark what follows as general knowledge, and keep quotation economy.
- **Shape.** The brief asks for the answer in the first two or three sentences, then the evidence, then where Christians differ, in place of "briefly".
- **Voice.** Chat gets `voice-student.md`, voice.md cut to the rules that can bite in third-person reference prose, with the fixed sentences the instructions prescribe exempted. The Generator keeps the full voice.md.
- **Consistency.** The floor's leftover instruction to state what the bot is (removed from decision 0001 the same day) is gone, the floor and the safeguarding note no longer break the voice rules they give, and the course note describes its tools without naming them. `scripts/lint_voice.py --prompts` lints the assembled prompts, and predeploy reports it.

**Why.** In the 2026-11-05 bake-offs, both models stated contested readings as settled, in the house direction: Nemotron gave only the Reformed reading of John 6 and of Peter's rock, and Opus leaned credobaptist on baptism. The fairness rule was two sentences, and the Reformed stance was 45% of the prompt, placed after them and labeled a default. A model resolving the two picked the louder. The doctrine also made the tool the holder of the doctrine ("the tradition this library follows", "this places the library in a credobaptist Reformed stream"), and Nemotron wrote "This library stands in the credobaptist Reformed stream. It teaches that baptism follows a credible profession." Both models copied tool names from the prompt into replies, one pasted coordinates from a place record, and the prompt itself carried em dashes and contrastive negations.

**Enforced in.** `BIBLE_STUDENT`, `METHOD`, `DISPUTED`, `CONDUCT_FLOOR`, `_bible_student_system`, `_voice`, and `_system_for` in `webapp/app.py`; `src/studybible_mcp/prompts/doctrine-student.md` and `voice-student.md`; `curriculum.system_note`; `safety.safeguarding_note`; `scripts/lint_voice.py --prompts`, called by `scripts/predeploy.py`. Covered by `tests/test_student_prompts.py`, which also checks that every section of `doctrine-student.md` still exists in `doctrine.md`.

**Reverse when.** The maker finds the disputed-question procedure giving too much room to readings the school does not hold, or a fine-tuned model follows the fairness rule without the list. The changed prompt needs the maker's probe before it ships (docs/decisions/0014).
