# 0017: Media Studio is the maker's alone for now, runs each model on the cheapest service, and keeps its images private

Recorded 2026-11-03 at the maker's request: an image generator through fal.ai, limited to his login at first. Widened the same day, again at his request, to video and to more services, with each model run on whichever service is cheapest that day.

**Decision.**

- `/studio` and its API answer only the library admin login (`LIBRARY_ADMIN_EMAILS`, checked on the server for every request). Anyone else gets a plain 404, and the rail link stays hidden.
- Four services, each turned on by its own key: fal (`FAL_API_KEY`), Runware (`RUNWARE_API_KEY`), WaveSpeed (`WAVESPEED_API_KEY`), and Replicate (`REPLICATE_API_TOKEN`).
- A fifth is the maker's own GPU: ComfyUI on one Baseten H100 that shuts down five minutes after its last request (`baseten/comfyui`, turned on by `BASETEN_API_KEY` and `BASETEN_COMFY_MODEL_ID`). It runs MiniMax H3 from the open weights, the only place a 15-second H3 clip can be made. It runs only when picked, never as "cheapest today", because waking it adds a minute or two and its price leaves out the minutes it sits idle. Its price is the GPU's hourly rate times a measured render time (about 80 seconds for a 5-second clip on a warm GPU, 2026-11-04). Since 2026-11-04 it also runs what no service here offers: H3 stills, an H3 clip that ends on a chosen last frame, and H3 from reference pictures (its ref2va weights), which takes up to nine pictures the prompt names Picture 1, Picture 2 and on, plus pictures the clip passes through at set seconds. The two models only the GPU runs, H3 still and H3 from pictures, run without being picked, since no other service could take them. Later the same day it gained P-H3-Image, H3's image model from Pruna AI, as a LoRA on the H3 weights already mounted plus its own text encoder and VAE (about 10 GB): 8-megapixel images from words, and edits of an image with up to seven more pictures. On 2026-11-05, at his request, H3 from pictures moved to HyperFlow, Video Rebirth's 8-step adapter for H3 (the build for pruned weights, through the ComfyUI-Hyperflow node pack pinned at v1.3.2): a 5-second clip took 90 seconds on a warm GPU against 154 at the reference template's 20 steps, and looked as good side by side. Words and frames keep Comfy's 8-step LoRA, which was faster there (66 seconds against 88). It also extends a clip H3 made: the clip's last 22 frames and their sound are anchored at the start of a new clip, and the whole old clip and the new frames after the overlap are joined into one, up to 60 seconds in all.
- The menu lists each model once. Under it, the catalog (`MODELS` in `webapp/studio.py`) lists every service that runs it, how that service is asked, the price it listed, and where its live price is read.
  - Images: FLUX schnell and FLUX dev (fal, Runware), FLUX Kontext (fal, Runware), FLUX.2 klein (Runware), FLUX 3 Klein and Bria FIBO 2.0 (fal, Runware, added 2026-11-04 as the two open-weights editors that ranked above the studio's own on Artificial Analysis's editing board), Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo and Pro (Runware), and MiniMax H3 still and P-H3-Image (the maker's GPU). Added 2026-11-05 at his request: Qwen-Image 3.0 Pro (Runware), GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Flare and Nano Banana 2.1 (Runware, fal), Seedream 5.0 Pro (Runware, fal), and Grok Imagine Image 2.0, Muse Image, Ideogram 4.0 Quality and FLUX 3 Image (fal). GPT Image and Nano Banana bill by tokens, and fal names some prices in abstract units, so those routes keep the price written in the catalog instead of a daily reading, and Runware is tried first where both list the same price because it reports what each run cost.
  - Video: MiniMax H3 (fal, Runware, the maker's GPU), MiniMax H3 from pictures (the maker's GPU), H3 Max Turbo (fal, Runware), Hunyuan Video 1.5 (fal, WaveSpeed), Wan 3.0 (fal, Runware, Replicate), Kling 4.0 (fal, Runware), Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 (fal), and Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast, and Veo 3.1 Fast (Runware). Added 2026-11-05 at his request: HeyGen Video 1 and FLUX 3 Max (fal), and MiniMax H3 Max, Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and Seedance 2.5 (Runware, fal). None of the five has open weights, so none can run on the maker's GPU. MiniMax H3 ranked third of 31 in Artificial Analysis's blind text-to-video rankings on 2026-11-03, ahead of Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and Veo 3.1.
- Each morning at 5:00 Central, an Inngest job (`studio-prices`) reads every service's price into a book in R2 (`studio/prices.json`). The page refreshes the book itself if it is more than 20 hours old, and has a "Check prices now" button.
  - fal: its pricing API, scaled where the studio runs above fal's base resolution.
  - Runware: the price block of each model's published schema.
  - WaveSpeed: its price-quote API, asked about the exact request.
  - Replicate: the billing tiers in the model's page.
  - A price that cannot be read, or lands more than five times above or below the listed one, is not used. The last good price stays, and the page says how many were kept.
- A run goes to the cheapest service that is set up and can make what was asked (shape, length, first frame, sound). If that service turns the account away (no credit, a refused key), the run moves to the next, and the service is skipped for six hours. The maker can also pick a service himself. The Make button names the service and its price before anything runs, and anything that would cost a dollar or more asks first.
- A video can start from words alone or from a first frame (an upload, or "Animate this" on an earlier image). On the maker's GPU it can also end on a last frame, and H3 from pictures takes reference pictures ("Use as a picture" on an earlier image) and pictures at set seconds. The page scales each of those to 2048 pixels on its long side before sending it. The service takes minutes, so the studio saves the video as pending, and the server checks on it every ten seconds until it is done. The page checks too, which picks the job up again after a restart. The maker can leave the page and come back.
- "Rewrite my words" sends the request to Claude Haiku 5.5 once, to expand it into a detailed prompt before drawing. The rewritten prompt is always shown beside the user's own words. A model's own hidden prompt expansion is switched off wherever its service allows (Qwen-Image, Wan, MiniMax H3 on both services, Hunyuan Video), so every service for a model gets the same prompt. Google always rewrites prompts for Veo 3.1 Fast, and the menu says so. A run on the maker's GPU is rewritten on the GPU instead, at his request (2026-11-04), as the first step of the same ComfyUI job. It adds about 20 seconds of GPU time, about 4 cents, to the run's cost, and the rewrite shows when the run comes back.
- Each service is asked to keep as little as it can:
  - fal keeps generated files forever at public links unless told otherwise. Every request asks fal to keep no request history and to delete an image after ten minutes and a video after two hours. Once a video is saved, the studio also asks fal to delete its files at once, which works when the fal key has the FULL permission preset. fal holds a finished video's result for only an hour, which is why the server follows each video itself.
  - Runware returns images inline, so it never hosts them. A video's link expires after one day. Without Runware's enterprise Zero Data Retention option, Runware may keep prompts and inputs. It says it never trains on them.
  - WaveSpeed keeps media up to seven days. The studio deletes each finished prediction there once the file is saved.
  - Replicate deletes an API prediction's inputs and outputs after an hour on its own. Its file links need the key, which the studio sends only to Replicate's own hosts.
  - On the maker's GPU, the pictures sent with a job and the finished file sit on the GPU's scratch disk until it shuts down, and the studio deletes each job's history once the file is saved. ComfyUI runs with `--offline`, so it makes no calls of its own, and with `--disable-metadata`, so the video file does not carry the prompt.
- Work can be sorted into folders, moved and deleted many at once (2026-11-04). New work lands in the folder being viewed. A folder may have a password, kept only as a salted scrypt hash. Until the folder is opened with it in the browser session (for 30 minutes, or until locked), its items stay out of every list, file, check, and new run, and nothing can be moved in or out. The "All" view never shows a protected folder's items. Five wrong passwords in ten minutes pause tries on that folder. The files themselves are not encrypted, so the lock guards against someone using the maker's signed-in browser, not against access to R2. Deleting a folder moves its items back out to the Library. Each folder is shown as the house cover laid landscape, drawn by `scripts/house_covers.py` (`build_folder`): its name engraved as the title and no other words, never a preview of its work. Its cloth is a house theme for light pages and one for dark, chosen in "Cover color…" with a live preview, or picked by the folder's id until then. A folder's ⋯ button (or a right-click on its card) and an open folder's header hold Rename, Cover color, the password, Lock now, and Delete; on a locked folder each asks for the password first. A protected folder carries a diary lock pressed into the same cloth, near the fore-edge: a raised strap in the binding's color running off the edge into a foil clasp plate with a keyhole while locked, and only the foil catch while open.
- Everything lands in the app's private R2 bucket under `studio/<user id>/` and is served only to the signed-in owner. Deleting an item deletes it from R2. Deleting the account deletes the folder. Removing a video that is still being made stops the studio waiting for it. The service may still finish it and charge for it, and the page says so.
- Each finished image or video leaves one metrics row (route `studio` or `studio:video`) with its cost, so the spend shows on `/ops`. Where a service reports what it charged (Runware), that figure is used, and otherwise the price quoted when the run started.

**Why.** The maker wants to explore image and video generation before deciding whether any school audience should have it. The same model often costs half as much on one service as another, and prices move week to week (on 2026-11-03 Replicate's Wan 3.0 showed three different prices in one day), so the choice is made from that day's prices. Students and teachers see nothing until he decides. Eden AI and Alibaba Cloud's own API were considered the same day and left out at his direction. Alibaba asks for a passport or driver's license before taking payment, and its cheaper tier can run jobs on mainland China nodes. Kie.ai and PiAPI were left out because neither is authorized by the model makers.

**Open for the maker.** Whether the studio should decline to depict Christ or any person of the Trinity. The Westminster Larger Catechism (Q109) forbids such images, and the doctrine file follows that tradition. Nothing in the studio decides this yet. Also which audience, if any, comes next, and what caps it gets. Talking-head and lip-sync models are left out on purpose, since they can animate a real person's face from one photo. H3's soundtrack guide, which sets a clip to a supplied recording, is left out of the GPU modes for the same reason until the maker rules on it.

**Enforced in.** `webapp/studio.py` (`may_use`, `MODELS`, `candidates`, `_runnable`, `make`, `check`, `_follow`, routes), `webapp/studio_services.py` (one client per service and its retention settings), `webapp/studio_comfy.py` and `baseten/comfyui/` (the maker's GPU), `webapp/studio_prices.py` (the price book), `webapp/inngest_app.py` (`studio-prices`), `webapp/static/studio.html`, the hidden `.admin` rail links revealed by `webapp/static/settings.js`, `webapp/account.py` (the folder goes with the account), and `metrics.ROUTES`. Covered by `tests/test_studio.py`, `tests/test_studio_prices.py`, and `tests/test_studio_comfy.py`.

**Reverse when.** Widening `may_use` to teachers or students needs a new decision, with caps and a privacy-page entry.
