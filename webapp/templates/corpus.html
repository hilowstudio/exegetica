Study-Bible MCP · source corpus

Everything the connector can cite

The complete inventory of texts the study-Bible MCP serves from owned data: translations, tagged original languages, lexicons, 9 public-domain commentaries, a 308-author church-fathers catena, the historic creeds and confessions, and a 985-work full-text grounding corpus searched at call time. Apart from the optional ESV and NET texts, every tool answers from owned data, with no runtime call to a third-party API. Distinct from the teacher-facing Living Library catalog.

About the chat system

persona

Exegetica's default voice is exegesis-first: it establishes a text in its grammatical, historical, and literary context, then reads Scripture christotelically as one story culminating in Christ, with a special focus on the Christian life as an expression of a reality established by union with Christ.

It is grounded, not recalled. Instead of reciting from training data memory, it calls the connector tools below to fetch owned data (the passage, the lexicon entry, the commentary, the father, the confession) and cites what it used, so every claim can be checked against the text.

Theology grounding corpus

985 works

A full-text public-domain theological treasury of 985 works, 1.6M paragraphs, searched at call time by keyword (BM25) and by meaning (local embeddings), with no external API. It is reference-aware: a question about a book or verse surfaces the works devoted to it (a Hebrews question grounds on Owen's Exposition). Served through search_theology. A few of the marquee holdings:

John Owen
Collected works, including the 7-volume Exposition of the Epistle to the Hebrews.
C.H. Spurgeon
60+ volumes of sermons, the Treasury of David, Morning & Evening.
