About the Generator

Turn any point in the K-12 standards spine into a finished, ready-to-teach resource.

Navigate from a subject down to a single learning objective, choose a format, and Exegetica writes the document, grounded in the standards and its tools.

Built on a real standards spine

Thirteen subjects, down to the individual objective. Every objective is grade-placed and aligned to a national academic standard (Common Core, NGSS, the C3 Framework, CSTA, National Core Arts, among others), or marked honestly where no secular standard applies. The objectives are real, distinct competencies, right-sized to each topic rather than padded to a template.

Faith and Bible integration on every lesson

For any subject, strand, topic, or objective, the Faith & Bible Integration Guide helps you weave Christian perspective and devotion to Jesus into the material truthfully, matched to what the content genuinely supports, and without forcing it. Where a lesson only supports a posture of wonder, diligence, or gratitude rather than a content link, it says so plainly.

Grounded and cited

Biblical, original-language, confessional, and church-father content is drawn from the Exegetica tools and cited. The academic content stays faithful to the standard and the grade level.

Many formats, one engine

Worksheets, lesson plans, quizzes, study guides, rubrics, close readings, lab reports, and dozens more, each adapted to your grade band from primary through seminary. The page suggests the formats that fit the material; pick the one the lesson calls for.

Yours to shape

Generate a teacher or student version, view or edit it in place, revise it with a plain instruction, and download the Markdown or print to PDF.

The tools ground and discipline the work. They do not make it infallible. Review the biblical and factual content before you teach it, and verify against Scripture.

Biblical, original-language, and confessional content is drawn from these bundled sources through the Exegetica connector and cited. All are public-domain or openly licensed.

English translations (bundled)

Berean Standard Bible (BSB), World English Bible (WEB), King James Version (KJV), American Standard Version (ASV), and Young's Literal Translation (YLT).

Modern translations (fetched live)

English Standard Version (ESV, via a Crossway API key) and the New English Translation (NET, keyless). Quoted live at request time with attribution, never bundled or stored.

Original languages

Tagged Hebrew and Greek with full morphological parsing, and the Greek Septuagint (LXX).

Lexicons

Brown-Driver-Briggs (Hebrew), Liddell-Scott-Jones (Greek), and Abbott-Smith (New Testament Greek), keyed to Strong's numbers.

Concordance

Every-occurrence search across the tagged text, by English word or Strong's number.

Cross-references & parallels

A cross-reference network and the UBS parallel-passage set.

Classic commentaries

Matthew Henry, John Gill, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, Adam Clarke, John Calvin, and Keil & Delitzsch (Old Testament).

Church fathers

A catena of Ante-Nicene, Nicene and Post-Nicene, Eastern/Byzantine, Syriac, and medieval writers (Chrysostom, Augustine, Irenaeus, and more).

Study notes

Tyndale, SIL, and unfoldingWord translation notes.

Creeds, confessions & catechisms

Ecumenical creeds (Apostles', Nicene, Athanasian, Chalcedon); Reformed and Protestant (Westminster Confession with the Larger and Shorter Catechisms, Belgic, Canons of Dort, Heidelberg, 1689 London Baptist, the Thirty-Nine Articles, Augsburg); Catholic (Council of Trent, Baltimore Catechism); and Orthodox (Confession of Dositheus, Philaret's Catechism).

People, places & genealogy

A graph of biblical people, places, events, and family lines to explore and connect.

Bible dictionary

The Tyndale Bible Dictionary: 500+ articles on people, places, doctrines, customs, and background.

Key terms & vocabulary

Passage key-term and vocabulary lists for study and teaching.
