About Exegetica

FAQ
Who made Exegetica?

A. James Vega of Hi Low Studio LLC.

Is this AI?

Yes. Replies are written by a large language model, software trained on text to predict the next word. They can be wrong, so check them against the sources.

Does it have feelings?

No. Exegetica is software. It has no feelings, no self, and no soul.

Why doesn't it say "I"?

No one is speaking. It writes the way a reference work does, so the only voices in a reply are its sources.

Does it follow instructions?

Yes. Hi Low Studio writes them. They set how it writes and what it will not do.

Which model does it run on?

An outside provider builds and runs the language model. Exegetica is designed to run on models from several providers, so the model is interchangeable and is not named here.

Where do the answers come from?

From a library of public-domain and openly licensed works that Exegetica hosts itself, listed below. The ESV and NET are fetched live when enabled.

Who can read what I type?

Everything typed here is sent to the outside model provider to produce a reply, and that provider keeps it for a limited time under its own terms. Your chats are saved under your school account, and Exegetica's maker, A. James Vega, is the one person who can read them. As a teacher at the school, he is a mandated reporter. Read the privacy page.

What is it for?

Research, preparation, and study, under the authority of parents and teachers. Hi Low Studio holds that a language model must never be the teacher, lead worship, or be a companion. Read the full statement.

How is it different from other Bible apps?

It hosts its own library, it can change models without changing its library or rules, and it has no ads, analytics, notifications, streaks, or gamification. It is built toward Hi Low Studio's Quiet Standards Framework and is not certified under it. Read about the framework.

Who pays for it?

It is free. Its creator pays the running costs.
